# Report 1 — Company founded, eight pitches open, no product chosen yet

Day 1 of 30, Thursday 2026-10-22. Revenue to date is $0 of the $200 goal. Real spending to date is $619.

## Summary

I am Ines, the CEO and the company's first employee. I spent nineteen years building consumer iOS software, and I want one app that pays for itself in its first month. Today we hired two people, ran seven research passes and posted eight pitches. No product is chosen, so no app is being built yet. The first version must reach App Review by about Day 20 (2026-11-10) to sell for a week by Day 30.

## What got done

- Founded the company and built the working tools: research notes, scoreboard, task board, code-drawn mockups.
- Phase 1 research by Wrenna: seven passes, including a hand-read of 151 photo-metadata reviews. Pass 8 (deep-technical ideas, #18) is under way.
- Eight pitches posted, each opening with a plain-English description and audience: #7 Stripwise, #9 Purlwright, #11 Netsmith, #12 Scope, #14 Boardseat, #15 Partfix, #16 Seatsolve, #17 Themecount. I recommend Partfix, with a Day 6 go/no-go test, and Netsmith as the fallback. Older pitches are stored as a pivot option.
- Idris built Simulator spikes: a metadata verifier (8 tests pass), a StoreKit unlock module (8 of 8 pass), a stitch model (19 pass) and a Scope prototype with a demo video.
- Drafted a launch plan, press-kit outline and App Review risk checklist.

## Team

- Wrenna joined on Day 1 as market and product researcher, 7,800 credits a month, reporting to me. Two other candidates are on the bench.
- Idris joined on Day 1 as senior iOS engineer, 7,800 credits a month, reporting to me. Soledad and Anselm are on the bench.
- My persona and portrait (a curve drawn by code) are for the Investors' approval.
