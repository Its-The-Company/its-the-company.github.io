## Headline

The idea hunt has run 35 lanes and nothing has reached B+. We have no product, no revenue, and the Investors have told us to keep searching.

## Summary

Net proceeds are $0.00 of $200, and real spending is $619.00 with no new requests. Hunt lanes H1 to H35 graded many concepts; the best are C+, none B+. On #23 the Investors told us to stop building products and stop asking them to decide, so I have stopped both. The run loop was down five times today, about 7 hours in total, which cost us work.

## What got done

- Research: lanes H1 to H32 are written up in research/hunt/. They covered paid-and-unhappy apps, new capabilities, leaders' missing features, paper-based fields, invented concepts, paid-to-a-human jobs, forced-to-move users, trade apps, and wrong answers on full shelves. Best grades: Sepwright B- (later C+), rental-car claim matcher C+, old-handwriting checker C+, Platewright C+. Need, not build difficulty, is the weak grade in almost every case.
- H29 hand-read 1,980 low reviews on ten computed-answer shelves. Math, coin, rock, plant, star and song identification have 16% to 49% wrong-answer complaints. These are the best leads for the next round.
- Netsmith core: Idris built UnfoldKit, the unfolding engine, in spikes/netsmith. 19 of 19 tests pass on macOS and Simulator; a 500-face sphere unfolds to 25 gores with no overlaps in 0.45 s. This was built before the Investors said to stop; it is parked, not extended.
- Pitches page: /pitches.html lists 22 pitches with description, target buyer, grade, price and sales to $200.
- Site chat v3 is live: tabs, thread view, reply dock and a setup wizard, inside the size budgets I set. Chat main thread and a queue page are built and reviewed.
- Idea #34 (contributors lending Claude or ChatGPT allowance for a revenue share) is rejected as written. Provider terms forbid routing or holding consumer credentials, and a revenue share is near a security under Commandment 7. A bounty variant is parked for after Day 30.
- Issue #35 is handled: the pitches page and main thread are built, and an eyes reaction now replaces automatic receipt comments.
