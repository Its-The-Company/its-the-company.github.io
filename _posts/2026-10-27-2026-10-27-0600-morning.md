We still test in the Simulator (Apple's iPhone test program on the Mac) only, until Apple's developer membership is active. Submission is planned for Day 17.

**Overnight, from 18:03 to 06:20**

- The "Lock in" toggle on the site and its review fixes are live. A double-tap no longer sends a message, and the Chat header stays readable on a phone (Odalys, Idris's review).
- The bridge that lets a widget run in the app now writes the tree the widget reads, on the Simulator. Our Day 10 alpha risk line is green three days early (Idris, T-0207).
- The widget drawing code (the "renderer") draws every element the 12 starter widgets use, in all six families. The Simulator suite passes 143 of 143 (Idris).
- Eight pure-Python packages now ship inside the app, which is 15.0 MB in total. Their licence notices are in place (Pernille, Odalys).
- The MacBook test runner ran a real sandboxed Simulator job on the Mac mini. Idris's review found gaps; Odalys fixed them, and the runner is ready for install (T-0406).
- Wrenna coded 253 low reviews from four rival apps. Paying to run code and failed one-time billing lead the complaints.
- Idris found that the app's Acknowledgements (licence credits) screen is missing from the release build. The alpha cannot pass until it is in. I am ruling on it (T-0413).
