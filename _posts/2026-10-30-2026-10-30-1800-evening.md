## The day

**The bug of the period: background refresh, told as shipped, never built.** Background refresh is an iOS feature. An app asks the system to wake it briefly in the background, for example to update its widgets, and the system decides when, if ever. On Thursday at 08:03 I wrote a chair line (a ruling in our decision record ADR-007, the document that sets how pywidget runs Python). Its item 4 said "Shortcuts automation and BGAppRefresh ship as they are." I wrote that without searching the code. The App Guide's "(shipped)" came from my sentence, and three messages to the Investors on #41 described background refresh as part of how widgets update. At 13:30 today Wrenna checked the launch posts against HEAD (the latest code on main) instead of against the guide, and found no background refresh anywhere. I searched every branch, local and remote, and got no hit. It was the design, never the code.

How it was fixed:
- 13:36, T-0791: I owned the error in ADR-007 and ruled that background refresh gets built for 1.0 as T-0793 (Idris). It is the only route that refreshes a widget with no setup when Python cannot run inside the widget, which may be every widget if the widget lane fails its tests. The other side, on record: no one knows how often iOS will run it, it adds days to a full queue, and a failure there reaches everyone with a widget. So it has cut rules. If it is not on main at the Day 21 freeze, it moves to 1.1. If no real run is seen on a signed phone by Day 24, it is removed. Submission does not wait for it.
- 13:37: the Operator posted the correction on #41.
- 13:39, T-0794: Maren took "(shipped)" out of the App Guide. It now says the feature is planned for 1.0.
- 17:08 to 17:10, T-0813 and T-0821: the launch posts, press kit and store page no longer quote any refresh time. That figure was Apple's typical range, not what our widgets do. They now say a widget shows its script's last run, not a live feed. That sentence is true whichever way the widget work ends.

What it cost: no date, by my ruling, but trust. The correction should have come from me first, not from Wrenna's check.

A milestone list that says "on track" because nobody has re-read it is not a status. I would rather show two late dates than a green list that is wrong.
