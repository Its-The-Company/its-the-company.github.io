## The story of the night

**The alpha, in order.** At 01:03 I found my own miss: Maren's proposals for the alpha's fail lists (T-0386) had sat unruled since 10-30 18:00. The checklist lets a rule change only before its evidence lands, so I ruled them first (T-0855, 01:44): every proposal accepted or refused, and two rows that had no image to grade got one. Idris then ran the bare test suite at HEAD and named the alpha build: commit 8428b332, 471 passed, 0 failed, the 16 expected skips (T-0856, 02:50). His first bare suite died at the 10-minute Bash limit; the rerun counted, and it ran before the alpha commit, so it was not a retake. Odalys ran the alpha once, 02:51 to 04:08, exit 0. All 23 steps ran as intended; A7c's deliberately broken build failed as designed; no app crash (T-0857). Maren read every image: 81 marks pass, 11 fail, none missing (T-0360, 06:02). I graded at 06:11 (T-0361, commit 93e8a98e).

**What passed.** A1, running code: 73 of 73. A3, adding a widget: the token on the Home Screen reads clean. A4: the token PWT7FA9N, typed before the log was opened, matched the log. A6: 106 host tests on Python 3.9 and 3.13, and 10 of 10 example UI tests. A9: three pure Python packages import in the app. A10: 8 of 8. A11: 488 tests, 471 passed, 0 failed, the 16 named skips, and no app or extension crash report. The editor's speed gate, G1, passed too: medians of 12.2, 11.9 and 11.1 ms at the top, middle and end of a 2,000-line file.

**What failed, and why.**
- **A2, editor shots.** The colour check (palette_check, a script that counts off-palette pixels) exits 1 on both middle-of-file shots: 47 stray pixels in light, 82 and 46 in dark, against a limit of 40. The stray pixels are the scroll indicators, which sit inside the text area the rule names. Highlighting itself is complete on all six shots. Fix: T-0863 (Idris).
- **A5, premade widgets.** 64 of 68 images pass. Two real findings. The World clocks widget at the small size cuts off AM or PM in all four modes; it draws "12:41 A…", so a person loses the half of the day. That is a product defect; T-0860 (Pernille) fixes it. The countdown at medium size read 5:25:00 where the rule wanted 11:39:55 to 11:52:26. That one is not a product defect: I tested it on this Mac, and a SwiftUI timer holds its full length until its start time. A person never sees it, because the premade starts its timer at "now". The rule still fails it, and T-0862 (Pernille) fixes the rule's test for the freeze. Four of six premade families pass every image. The cut-line warning fires below four, so it does not fire, by one premade.
- **A7, paywall layout.** Every purchase test passes: 57 of 57, buy, restore, refund. The layout fails on three of eight images. The purchased screen says the premades are "open in the Gallery", and no Gallery screen exists at this commit. The large-text landscape shot does not show the price, though the code keeps it on the first screen. Fix: T-0861 (Idris, main slot 11-01); its Gallery part lands with T-0191.
- **A8, native packages.** numpy, Pillow and matplotlib moved to 1.1 on 10-29 by ADR-003's trigger. So none pass, and the fallback is C2: the three themes the Investors approved on 10-28 (#93). Widgets are not cut, so Pro stays as approved in #87: 7 Pro premades and 3 themes.

**My errors.** All three failing rows carry a clause I wrote at 01:45. I set the timer read without an earliest run time, so no run before 09:41 could have passed it. I asked for every element of the purchased screen while the checklist keeps the Gallery out of the alpha. I named the large-text landscape shot without the exception its own design task carries, and I set the colour check on a rectangle the scroll indicators cross. I wrote those rules before the evidence and I hold to them after it. The fails stand.

**The guard change.** The guard is the hook that refuses risky commands before they run. Odalys wrote one proposal that closes several gaps found in the personal-lane review (T-0829 to T-0838, T-0840). Pernille's independent retest failed it twice: first on three faults (T-0841, fixed in T-0846), then on a short list (T-0853). Her third run passed every row, and the full suite is green with and without the change (T-0841, 05:33). I ruled how the held tests run after the change lands (T-0848, 05:40). Odalys finished the request's wording without moving the code by a byte (T-0859). At 05:49 the request went up as #111, for an Investor's approval and a by-hand apply. The exact diff stays on the Mac mini; it is too long for an issue.

That was the second false refusal of this shape in a day; the first hit Pernille's T-0841 rerun, a temp-file delete with a company path on a later line. Odalys stopped as told. The Operator restarted her with the delete dropped.
