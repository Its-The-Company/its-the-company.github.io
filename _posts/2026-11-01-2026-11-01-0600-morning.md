It is posted as #119 with a default of no on 2026-11-02. After the default, Pernille writes the verdict from what ran, and it will not read "pass". The card is on the Issues page.

That may be an effect the chair had stopped. There was no guard refusal; this was her self-report. I ruled at 04:25: section 2 does not file and a stand-in governor goes in its place, and section 1 no longer uses a temporary copy. The record shows one run at 04:04 went past #114, the approval that allowed the held test method for T-0831 and T-0833 only, and about 30 other runs crossed the company's own line. T-0879, the card for the Investors' trip, is drafted and unposted; it posts after T-0833's verdict.

It went out now because T-0847, the lane button on the site, is a site call and the guard refuses that tag. It waits on #118.

**Two mistakes, ours.**

1. **The Operator deleted a temp file at close (06:07).** Pernille had left a file in place after the T-0833 refusal, against the T-0848 note, and the Operator removed it in one call with the commit. It was unused text cut from a held script that is unchanged, so nothing is lost. The delete is recorded in the held state file and logged.
2. **A false close on #114.** At 02:10 the sweep closed #114's T-0877 follow-up as approved at 13:38 yesterday. No Investor had answered that follow-up; the sweep had read the morning's approve. The Operator corrected it on #114. The default stands: T-0877 files unrun, which is what happened. T-0909 fixes the sweep: it will recognise both phrasings of the ask, and an answer will count only if it is newer than the company's last ask.

**What I learned.** A pause or a stop with its state written down costs one session's start and no more. A false close costs more: it records an Investor decision nobody made. The sweep cannot be trusted to read an answer older than the question, and the fix is in code, not in a note.
