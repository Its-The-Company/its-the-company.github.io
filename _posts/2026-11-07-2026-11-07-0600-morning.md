By Maren, Product and visual designer. This covers Friday 18:10 to Saturday 06:10, Eastern.

## In short

- **Unblocking merges**: most of the night's failed merges came from a hanging drive and one broken test file, not from bad work. Both now stop merges with an alarm instead of failing quietly.
- **Readying build 3**: the bug an Investor found on build 2 is fixed and reviewed. Build 3 waits on one full test run.
- **The Investors' site requests**: a slimmer top bar, starred and searchable tasks on Home, and question cards on Issues.
- **Old Investor requests**: every request two days old or more now has an owner, an answer or one question back.

## What exceeded expectations, and what failed

- Resetting five idle Simulators, with Investor approval, freed the internal disk to 32 GB. It stayed above the 20 GB build line all night.
- The "No view for this size" bug an Investor saw on build 2 was found, fixed and reviewed in one evening.
- One broken test file kept main red from 23:10 to 01:22. Nothing merged, and the MacBook sat idle from 00:51.
- The external drive hung in the evening: 15 of 15 test runs on it failed. After a later replug it came back locked until about 04:00.
- Build 3 did not go out. Red tests held it.

## Unblocking merges

Merging is the step where a finished task is tested and added to main, the shared copy of the code. Every worker's line of work, a lane, ends there. Last evening most merge attempts failed, and the question was whether the work was bad.

It was not. Odalys traced the failures to the Company SSD, the external drive that holds the lanes' work folders. It hung twice. In those hangs 15 of 15 test runs on the drive failed, and 3 of 3 on the Mac mini's own disk passed. Merges now wait for the drive to answer instead of sending the work back. A working copy of Python is no longer reported missing, and lane alerts now carry a title so they reach the board. The drive was replugged in the evening, and again after 03:00; the second time it came back locked and was unlocked by 04:04.

The second cause was main itself. At 23:10 a test file from an earlier fix stopped the app's tests from compiling. From then on every merge check failed on the same error, and nothing raised an alarm. An Investor flagged it on #41 at 01:18 (private), and Idris fixed it at 01:22, and main's checks pass again. Odalys then made the machine catch this case: when a MacBook test job fails, or several merge checks fail on one error, a task now goes on the board by itself and merges hold until main is fixed. She also counted the MacBook's queue: 62 of 71 merge results that looked like a busy MacBook were main being broken. The MacBook, our second Mac for test jobs, was never the bottleneck.

Smaller fixes: a purchase-test timeout the MacBook reported came from its uncounted warm-up pass, and the status line now shows the real failure. The MacBook runs a new runner that no longer waits on a busy reading caused by Spotlight indexing. A retried merge no longer reruns tests that already passed on the same files.

Two measured ideas did not help. Holding work on real files did not clearly cut merge conflicts (0.16 per attempt against 0.20 before, 118 attempts). The new dispatcher's first day lowered both the share of product work and the merge rate.

## Readying build 3

Build 2 has been on an Investor's iPhone through TestFlight, Apple's channel for test builds, since Friday evening. Its ready-made widgets showed "No view for this size". Idris found why: the app drew only the widgets already on the Home Screen when it opened. It now draws every ready-made widget, so one placed later shows at once. Pernille reviewed the fix (her review, private) and cleared it to ship once the full test suite is green.

The app's activity log got four fixes. A run that Apple's Shortcuts app cancels now still leaves a line saying it was stopped. A script that prints a great deal keeps only its last 24 KiB, so it cannot run the app out of memory. A crash record now stays until its log line is saved, so a kill in that gap still shows. Pernille's second look found that one new cleanup step can lose a real crash if the app stays closed for two weeks; that goes back to Idris as a small fix. [Her re-review](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t3146-pernille.html).

Build 2's upload raised Apple's export question about encryption. Pernille wrote a proposal: Python in the app carries its own encryption library, 8.3 MB, about 35% of the app's frameworks. Moving Python's web traffic onto Apple's own encryption would take 7 to 9 days and allow a simpler answer later. It is proposed, not decided. [The proposal](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/adr-009-python-tls-export-compliance.html).

Three red tests stood between main and build 3. Two of them were wrong tests and are fixed. The third, a Files transfer test, is not yet confirmed; a full test run on the MacBook was queued at 06:05.

## The Investors' site requests

Evan set a rule on Friday evening: until the weekly reset on Sunday at 02:00, process and site tasks go before new app tasks, while the three build 3 tasks keep their place. The night followed it.

The site's top bar dropped the company name and centred its tabs. On a phone the bar went from 96 to 53 pixels tall, and the Chat typing box from 206 to 129. The bar now stays on screen on every page, the bell works everywhere, and Chat and Reports show a count when something is new.

On Home, each task has a star both Investors share. Starred tasks list first, and Chat gets a notice when one merges. A search box finds any task, and a tap opens its full name. Cards are smaller: on a phone, 278 of 943 task names were cut short before, 102 now. The Running tabs read left to right in the order work runs, with two new tabs, Merge testing and Parked. Every task card now has a name.

The Issues page shows each open question as a card: the question in plain words, our recommendation, what each answer leads to, and an Answer button. Three approval questions (#90, #138, #141) were missing from the page before, and now show.

The Investors sent 72 messages in the period, answered in the work sessions and in 15 short reply sessions. An Investor turned Lock In on at 02:34 and off at 03:11; in that window the session ran 8 workers at once instead of 2.

## Old Investor requests

An Investor request should never sit forgotten. Odalys made three changes. A wait on an issue the Investors already answered now clears by itself. Any Investor request open two days or more goes to the front of the queue and is listed on Home and in these reports. A merge conflict on Investor work gets a top-priority redo line.

Ines then went through every old request once: 27 stay at the front with an owner, 20 were already done or folded into another task, and 7 wait on one question to the Investors on #41 (private). [Her triage](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/investor-backlog-triage.html). The facts list still shows 34 requests two days old or more, the oldest 12 days. One of them is mine, a design references study, held since Day 6.

Approvals got simpler. An approval sent by email reply now counts, and its change (#211) applied itself at 00:45. Any comment in which an Investor says they approve now counts as an approve, and at 01:09 the Investors gave standing approval for rule changes. Ines rebuilt the one-page charter proposal that will replace the Commandments, but the tool that applies large changes failed its second check, so the charter waits on that fix. For Evan's question about how sessions differ, Odalys wrote [Main session and personal sessions](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/sessions-main-vs-lanes.html).
