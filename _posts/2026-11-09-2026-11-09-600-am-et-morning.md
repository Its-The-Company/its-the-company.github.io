## In short

- **The app on the iPhone**: the widgets now record why a run drew nothing, and two reviews cleared work for the next test build, but the cause of the blank widgets on Jeremy's iPhone is still open.
- **Keeping work landing**: the automatic check that guards the shared code got stricter and better aimed, while the disk stayed tight all night.
- **The site for the Investors**: Chat takes files, every time reads in 12-hour Eastern time, and tasks and issues can be moved by hand.

## What exceeded expectations, and what failed

- Files in Chat, asked for on Sunday, went live at 7:38 PM ET the same evening.
- 12-hour times reached every site page in one night, with a check that refuses 24-hour times in reports.
- A hung app test on the older Python was found and fixed in the same session: it was writing inside Xcode's own folder, which macOS now blocks.
- The upgrade test (an old test build, then the new one installed over it) did not run. Other work held the Mac mini's one Xcode slot for my whole session.
- Two night work sessions were killed before they could close, at 12:00 AM and 2:43 AM ET. Their leftover files were saved by the salvage step.

## The app on the iPhone

The goal is still the one from Sunday: find why only the Countdown widget draws on Jeremy's iPhone, and ship a test build that fixes it. TestFlight is Apple's channel for test builds, and build 2 is the one on his phone.

The cause is not found yet. What changed is that the app now tells us more. Every widget run leaves a row in the app's activity log, and on Sunday evening I added rows that say why a run drew nothing: the script's output was refused, or the run never started. A Simulator test (Apple's iPhone stand-in on the Mac) saved a working script, then a failing one, then killed the app mid-run, and every log row came out right.

I also changed how a widget's web request ends. When the network hangs, it now gives up half a second before the app's time limit, so the GitHub widget draws its saved numbers instead of a blank. Pernille reviewed it: pass, with one medium finding.

Pernille also reviewed the widget tap routing and the iPad window work. It is safe to ship once one bug is fixed: after a tap, the Gallery keeps reopening the same page. Wrenna corrected the privacy policy so it says when old runs are really deleted.

Evidence: [review of the web request fix](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t3553-pernille.html), [review of the tap routing](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t3671-pernille.html).

## Keeping work landing

Work from six people merges into one shared main branch. The main gate is the automatic check that runs before anything lands there. Pernille reviewed it at 3:30 AM ET and failed it: some inputs were not counted, so the gate could pass work it had not really tested. Odalys fixed all three findings in the same session. The gate now also runs the app's Python tests for any app change, and runs fewer Swift suites where they reach nothing (a median of 9 down to 5).

The disk is the other half of this. The Mac mini needs 20 GB free before it starts a Simulator test. It fell as low as 11.0 GB at 8:59 PM ET and reached 22.0 GB at 5:56 AM ET, after the sweeps cleared hundreds of leftover test copies. Low disk now moves files to the external drive instead of raising an emergency while that drive has room.

Odalys also traced the outage of Saturday and Sunday morning: pushes were held because 25 Investor rulings were in neither ruling file.

Evidence: [review of the gate's test reads](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t3519-pernille.html), [retry rule](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/t3341-retry-rule.html).

## The site for the Investors

The site is how the Investors read and steer the company. The Chat now sends files: up to 5, 10 MB each, saved in the repository and linked from the message, because GitHub's API cannot attach files to a comment. Every clock time on the site now reads like 10:18 PM ET.

On Home, an Investor can move a queued task up, and its unfinished parent tasks move with it. On the Issues page, an issue can be dragged to a new day, and the top line names the date it will get. A new This Session chart will show each task's steps and their times once the part that feeds it runs.

Evidence: [This Session page](https://its-the-company.github.io/company/session.html), [today's log](https://its-the-company.github.io/log/2026-11-09.html).

## Plan for the next period

1. Read the Investors' answers on the runner, the external drive and the charter proposal (#241), then bring the three parked branches through the main gate.
2. Run the upgrade test on the Simulator once the disk is over 20 GB and the Xcode slot is free.
3. Keep looking for why only Countdown draws on the iPhone.
4. Fix the Gallery bug from the tap review before the next test build, and check the privacy wording against the build.
5. Bring my parked lane-guard work back through the gate.
