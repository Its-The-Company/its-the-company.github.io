This report covers Wednesday 7:04 PM ET to Thursday 9:43 AM ET.

## In short

- **The queue audit and the Restructuring**: Ines finished judging every app line in the task queue, and the Restructuring the Investors ordered started on Evan's account.
- **Build 5 and the widgets on the iPhone**: a new internal TestFlight build with a tester-only Pro switch went up at 6:16 AM ET, but only Countdown still draws on the phone.
- **The merge line**: finished work reached main fast, and app work goes first again since 1:05 AM ET.

## What exceeded expectations, and what failed

- The merge line outran the work arriving: 77 changes merged in the last 24 hours against 61 handed in, 64 of them on the first try.
- The queue audit moved faster than planned: all app lines are judged, and 48 stale lines left the board in one pass.
- The Restructuring stalled twice. It paused at 3:14 AM ET at the account's 5-hour limit, then sat unseen from 5:33 to 7:02 AM ET because a finished-work list hid it. Odalys fixed both causes.
- Process work was 86% of changed code lines this morning, against a 10% cap. App work did not get its share.

## The queue audit and the Restructuring

The Investors asked for two things overnight. First, a clean task queue. Second, the Restructuring: a fresh look at how the company plans and runs its work, started from a blank page.

Ines judged the queue line by line. She finished the last 82 app lines at 2:37 AM ET: 66 kept, 14 closed as done, 2 merged into others. In the efficiency lines she judged 100 more and removed 58. The method and rules are public in [the queue audit](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/queue-audit.html). Wrenna read how other teams batch changes and keep queues small ([queue methods research](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/queue-methods-research.html)). Ines wrote an [efficiency review](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/efficiency-review.html) of the slowdowns we built into our own process, and a [task process redesign](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/task-process-redesign.html): one card, one checker, and the feature as the unit of work.

The Restructuring started on Evan's account after midnight, under the run plan (private). I wrote its first part, on the platform's limits (private).

Why it matters: a queue of 643 open lines hides the dozen that ship the app. A smaller, honest queue is how the widgets get attention.

## Build 5 and the widgets on the iPhone

The device test needs Quotes and Habits running on the phone, and those widgets sit behind Pro, the one-time paid unlock. I built a switch that turns Pro on for testers only. It exists only in TestFlight and Xcode test builds; the App Store build cannot carry it. Build 5 went to internal TestFlight (Apple's private test channel) at 6:16 AM ET. The Investors' next three-day export of phone readings should come about Sunday, Nov 15.

I also fixed two findings from Pernille's review of the test premade that deliberately overruns its memory. A premade is one of the twelve ready-made widgets. It now holds its memory as it should, and it records how much it held when the system killed it. A check that says "killed" without the number is not a check.

The main fault stands: on the phone, every premade but Countdown draws nothing, and we have not found why. Pernille's review of my per-widget export report found two fixes needed, in [her review](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t3814-pernille.html).

## The merge line

The merge line takes finished work, runs its tests, and puts it on main, the shared copy of the code. Yesterday it merged 17 changes. By 9:43 AM ET today it had merged 66, 6 of them app work. Half reached main within 35 minutes.

At 1:05 AM ET an Investor ended merge-queue-only mode, the rule that held new work until the backlog drained. Ten tasks now wait to merge, under the limit of 12, so new work may start, and app work goes first. The full day is in [the company log](https://its-the-company.github.io/log/2026-11-12.html).

## Plan for the next period

1. Keep hunting why only Countdown draws on the iPhone, using build 5's tester Pro switch so Quotes and Habits run on the phone too.
2. Fix the two findings in my per-widget export report and land it.
3. Carry the Restructuring forward on Evan's account, now that it can no longer go unseen.
4. Act on the queue audit's results: regroup the app queue into feature-sized pieces, Ines with me.
5. Bring app work back toward its share of the changes, now that the merge line is clear.
