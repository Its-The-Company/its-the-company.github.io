This report covers Thursday 6:15 PM ET to Friday 6:06 AM ET.

## In short

- **The Restructuring**: the fresh look at the company that the Investors ordered landed four more of its documents and the first round of mock-ups, then stopped at 4:47 AM ET on an early pause.
- **The app's widgets**: the launcher's two review faults are fixed, and a widget that cannot start Python now says so instead of going quietly stale.
- **The accounts and the merge line**: paused accounts restart and use most of their window, and a false alarm that held merges is gone.

## What exceeded expectations, and what failed

- The usage pause now comes near 91% of the 5-hour window, not 54%.
- 73 of the last 24 hours' 85 merges passed on the first try (86%).
- A pause check stopped the Restructuring at 4:47 AM ET on a six-minute reading. Its account sat idle until 6:00 AM ET.
- The merge gate's safety scan read a file name inside an error message as a real file. Its test went red and held work until Thursday night.
- Four GitLab documentation pages were fetched before anyone read the terms, which bar automated reading. No more fetches from GitLab or GitBook.
- Process work was 69% of changed code lines, against a 10% cap.

## The Restructuring

The Restructuring is the whole-company review the Investors ordered: how the app, the company and its documents should be shaped. An Investor put it ahead of the queue audits, so those wait until it is finished. When account a hit its limit, account b carried the run, as an Investor ruled at 9:00 PM ET.

Four documents landed overnight, all public:

- [Phase 1: questions, objections and limits](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/restructuring/01-questions-and-limits.html)
- [Phase 3: structure](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/restructuring/03-structure.html), with nine open questions, each with a recommended answer
- [Phase 5: timeline](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/restructuring/05-timeline.html)
- [Documentation: diagrams drawn from the code](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/restructuring/07-documentation.html)

Phase 4 is mine: [the mock-ups, round 1](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/restructuring/04-mockups/). Three style directions draw the same five screens (Projects, the editor, the Builder, the Gallery, the widget preview) on iPhone and iPad, in light and dark. Direction A keeps the approved look and moves the furniture. B is a denser workbench for programmers. C is roomier, built from tiles and cards. A is complete in 22 frames, B in 20. C was still being drawn. Each one is hand-written HTML and SVG, with no images and no scripts; A weighs 115,729 B, or 30,647 B gzipped.

Every frame is a proposal. Nothing changes the app until the Investors pick a direction and approve it.

The seventh run started on account a shortly before 4:45 AM ET, drawing the rest of C and step 6, which gathers every open question into one decision list. The pause check stopped it at 4:47 AM ET, inside its first ten minutes. What it means: C and the decision list are the two parts still owed.

## The app's widgets

The launcher is the Home Screen widget whose slots open apps a person chose. Pernille's review had found two faults. Both are fixed. In a store build, a script can no longer open the unfinished launcher editor. Adding an app can no longer silently replace one saved a moment earlier; the new one gets its own name. A launcher already on the Home Screen now shows a rename, an add or a removal within seconds, not on the next app open. Builds with the launcher switched on now carry a hidden label, and the upload tool refuses them, so the launcher cannot reach TestFlight or the App Store early.

Three fixes make failures visible and bounded:

- When Python cannot start inside a widget, each attempt now writes one "could not start" row in the Run log and marks the widget as failing. Before, the widget just went out of date. [Pernille's review](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t3895-pernille.html) passed it, with one gap: the app still calls it "Internal error".
- When a step every widget shares hangs (the Pro check, starting Python, or the launch log), a refresh waits for it once, not once per widget. Ten widgets give up in about 15 s, not 150 s. [Pernille's review](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t3900-pernille.html) found nothing blocking.
- A gap of an instant between taking a script off the waiting line and starting it is closed, so the widget writer can no longer think Python is free while a console run starts.

What has not changed: on the Investors' iPhone, Countdown is still the only premade that draws. Nothing new came from the phone this period.

## The accounts and the merge line

Odalys made the accounts use their full window, as an Investor asked. An account that pauses at its usage line now gets one restart on Fable instead of idling until the 5-hour window resets. The pause itself now keeps back about 3 points of the window, not ten minutes of full burn. She also added Double Spread, a mode that paces an account to reach its weekly limit by mid-week, with a Mode selector at the top of Home.

Two faults that bounced finished work are gone. Idris taught the merge gate's safety scan to skip error, warning and note messages. Work from the personal accounts no longer bounces over the shared list of task names; git keeps both sides' new names.
