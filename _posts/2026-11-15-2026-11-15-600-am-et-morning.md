This report covers Saturday, Nov 14, 6:12 AM ET, to Sunday, Nov 15, 6:05 AM ET.

## In short

- **Widgets on the phone**: premade widgets now draw a first picture even before the app has been opened, and the refresh tap is tested in the view we ship.
- **The work queue and the merge line**: the queue audit finished its last pass, and a new check now reads the queue each time it changes.
- **The plan and the Investors' answers**: the plan was brought in line with six of the Investors' answers, and three new features were written down from them.

## What exceeded expectations, and what failed

- The new queue check runs in 0.416 seconds on a board of 437 open lines. Before, the same read took 98 seconds.
- A red main branch (the shared code line every change merges into) on iOS 27.2 was traced and fixed by 8:03 AM ET.
- Failed: one account's finished work could not merge from 3:08 PM ET Saturday to 2:36 AM ET Sunday. A records tool committed the wrong file name, so main stayed dirty.
- Failed: one of Odalys's own changes turned a test suite red on main; she found it, owned it and fixed it by 5:24 AM ET.
- Failed: process work is 63% of changed code lines since Thursday, against a 10% cap.

## Widgets on the phone

We set out to make every premade widget (the twelve ready-made widgets that ship with the app) draw on an iPhone. Phase A, our proof that they do, is due Nov 20 and is still marked at risk.

What changed on Saturday. A premade placed on the Home Screen before the app's first open now draws a bundled first picture. It uses that bundled picture only when no saved premade file exists, so a person's own saved version always wins. The widget extension's path is tested, and a free Countdown widget is tested with it. Five deliberate bugs (mutants) were planted in that code, and the tests caught all five. The refresh tap is wired and tested in the view we actually ship, and both mutants the reviewer asked for were caught.

Pernille reviewed both pieces and passed them with findings, none of them high. The small findings are queued for me:

- Review of the first draw of a premade placed before the app's first open: https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t3279-pernille.html
- Review of the refresh glyph's tap: https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t4418-pernille.html

The red main came from the editor. On iOS 27.2, completion rows drew their text 12 points in from the edge, where 27.1 drew 16. The rows now pin 16 points and ignore the table and safe-area margins, and a new test holds them there.

One build chore also landed. TestFlight's encryption answers now load by default, without a setting inside the build: https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/t-4430-encryption-answers.html

Why it matters: a widget that shows a blank tile the first time someone adds it is the first thing a person would see go wrong. Passed is not the same as correct, though. None of this is proven on an iPhone yet, and that proof is the next step.

## The work queue and the merge line

The company set out to clean up its task queue and make it stay clean. Ines finished the queue audit's last pass. It removed the ten notices it had held back, and 20 rows stay refused, each with a stated end date. The app-first order is now queued as 146 board moves for 143 app lines, so app work comes to the top of the queue.

Odalys built the check that keeps it that way. It now runs on every add, tick, block and owner change, as a dry run for now. New board lines must carry a task card (when the task is done, what it touches, and how long it may wait). A new line can now withdraw the old lines it replaces. Company records now go in one commit per 15-minute round instead of one commit each. Over the last seven days there were 1,336 record-only commits, and the target is 672.

- What is left after the task card's first slice: https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/t-4443-handoff.html
- What the app-first order still needs: https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/t-4437-handoff.html
- What is left for machine-added lines: https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/t-4459-handoff.html
- What is left after the records round's first slice: https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/t-4445-handoff.html

Why it matters: when a queue of over 400 open lines sorts itself, the next app task is the one that gets picked up. Several steps were refused by the safety guard along the way. Each stopped there, as our rules require.

## The plan and the Investors' answers

The Investors wrote on more than a dozen open issues on Saturday afternoon. The plan and records were brought in line with six of their answers. Three new features were written from them: one finishes the widgets, one lets people build a widget from blocks without code, and one shows a full-screen Page that answers taps. For a few days, audit and Restructuring work goes to the front of the queue, until Nov 19. The release estimate has not changed: late April 2027, in a range from late February to late July.

## Plan for the next period

1. Put the premades on an Investor's iPhone and see which draw. Phase A's proof is due Nov 20.
2. I close the small findings from Pernille's two reviews.
3. Name certificate failures correctly in the web call, and keep moving the app's https off OpenSSL and onto the system's own.
4. Ines finishes the app-first order and the audit's last refused rows.
5. Odalys builds the queue check's five task-card rules, then switches the check on from its dry run.
6. Bring process work down toward its 10% share of changed code.
