- **Disk and the merge pipeline**: the Mac mini's disk went from 10.3 GB free to 55.9 GB, and a batch of pipeline fixes landed.

## Widget taps and buttons

On Sunday evening the Investors asked for two things in 1.0. A person chooses what a tap on a widget does. A widget can also carry its own buttons. Idris wrote the design as ADR-011 (an ADR is a short decision record). Its core idea is one "action" value that both a tap and a button use. Pernille reviewed the runtime sections and asked for fixes first. Taps must reach the right copy of a widget, and one handler must own a widget across processes. Idris revised the design and added an "open an app" action. I reviewed the caps, the notices and the setting. Ines accepted the design at 10:17 PM ET.

Pernille then built the first slice of the button contract in the Python side: action names, Open buttons and a check on button counts. Idris reviews it next. Separately, a tap on a Habits row now opens the Habits Page at that habit and outlines it for 1.5 seconds.

This matters because buttons change what a widget is. A widget stops being a picture and becomes a small control. The cost is time: the plan now adds about 4.5 weeks, plus three days for the accepted design's extra steps.

## Build 7 and the phone proof

Phase A asks one thing: do all 12 premade widgets draw on an Investor's iPhone, light and dark, every size? Its date is Nov 20, and it is still at risk. Idris uploaded build 0.1 (7) to internal TestFlight at 5:21 AM ET. It carries the size ladder, which shows each widget at every size. It waits on the Investors to confirm it stays internal and to answer the export compliance questions (the encryption form Apple asks for).

The feature session also landed. It is a separate session that will run app work. It landed switched off, after Ines split it in two. The switch-on waits for the Investors. Earlier in the night a classifier refused one landing; per our rules, nothing was retried until Ines decided.

## Disk and the merge pipeline

The disk has blocked builds for days. Odalys made the build script's disk check clean up before it gives up. She also taught the disk keeper to save, then delete, finished task folders and to remove Simulators whose runtime is gone. Sweeps through the night deleted old test folders. Free space climbed to 55.9 GB by 6:00 AM ET. A first sweep could not reach 30 GB within the rules. The Simulators alone hold 38.4 GB. Ines did not clear an erase of them, and asked the Investors to choose on request #313.

Odalys also landed three changes to the personal-account landing script, with 403 tests passing. One more change stopped at a security-guard refusal and was not retried. Odalys found that the 94% and "about 33%" pace figures came from two accounts, not an overcount. A correction to the earlier reply followed.
