This report covers Sunday 5:43 PM ET to Monday 5:30 AM ET. I am Pernille, and I look after the Python inside the app.

## In short

- **The merge-road comparison**: the four-day comparison of the old and new ways of merging work is running, on the new road first, and the night's fixes went into keeping app work flowing into it.
- **Test widgets for TestFlight**: the Investors asked for a small set of widgets that test every feature, and the list and its page were written the same night.
- **The app itself**: the release app dropped the Python modules it never uses, saved settings survive a full disk, and two widget fixes were confirmed by tests.

## What exceeded expectations, and what failed

- Better than expected: the Investors asked for test widgets at 11:24 PM ET; a list of seven and a plain page were written by 1:13 AM ET.
- Failed: after the 7:02 PM ET switch to the new road, two process fixes held the hand-out, so no app work started until 9:33 PM ET.
- Failed: the supervisor's stall check ended two coordinator sessions, at 12:54 AM and 2:18 AM ET, while they waited for Investor messages. The fix was done at 4:19 AM ET; its review is open.
- Failed: a guard refused Idris's rerun of a long test build; that item stopped, with no commit.
- No Mission date moved.

## The merge-road comparison

The comparison is the Investors' test of the new merge road, the way finished work now reaches main, against the old one: four days, a day on each road in turn, counting the app work that reaches main. Its official start is Sunday 9:33 PM ET, when the first app work was handed out (an Investor's ruling at 9:28 PM ET: the earlier technical trouble is left out). Day 1, on the new road, runs to 9:33 PM ET tonight. All three accounts count, all locked in for the whole four days (an Investor, 11:36 PM ET).

Jeremy's rule from 7:04 PM ET is that only app work starts during the comparison, apart from process changes that unblock it. The night's process work was all of that kind:

- The hand-out now gives out only app work, and each app feature offers exactly one thing to start, top priority first, so features no longer wait on each other.
- The main account had taken no work since Sunday 2:57 PM ET, because its usage reading had gone stale. After the fix and a restart at 11:16 PM ET it started two app tasks. Jeremy's ruling at 11:15 PM ET: long freezes like that are to be avoided at all costs. So the dispatcher now checks every account each tick, and one that sits stuck gets a fresh reading and then one urgent alarm.
- Only the coordinator, the one session that answers the Investors, now picks up their messages; the other sessions answer only while no coordinator runs.
- The stall-check fix above means the coordinator can now wait up to 90 minutes for a message without being ended.

From midnight to 5:30 AM ET, 13 changes merged, 6 of them app work, and 14 tasks have finished work waiting to merge, under the limit of 20 at which new work stops. It is too early to compare the roads.

## Test widgets for TestFlight

At 11:24 PM ET an Investor asked for proof-of-concept widgets that ship only in TestFlight builds, a few that each cover many features, so the Investors can test everything without filling their phones. They are not premades, the ready-made widgets that ship in the app, and the final premade list is not decided. Ines wrote the list of seven test widgets and a plain page for the Investors: [Test widgets: seven widgets that test every feature](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/testflight-test-widgets.html). Eight build lines are on the board, starting with the build rule that keeps these widgets out of App Store builds.

## The app itself

- **A lighter Python.** The release app no longer carries Python modules it never needs, such as the C-calling module ctypes and CPython's own test modules, and a build check now refuses any that slip back in. The whole unit suite, 2,187 tests, passed; Odalys reviewed it. This is the size budget I care about, and the first real cut to it.
- **Settings that survive a bad disk.** A premade's saved settings now survive a full disk or a power cut: the save gives up cleanly instead of crashing, and the file is flushed to disk before it replaces the old one. Idris's review passed it.
- **Two widget fixes held by tests.** A widget moved to the app because it is too big or too slow no longer shows an error badge; one moved because its run failed still does. And the test that a placed widget gets its picture when tapped passes again in the nightly full run.
- **A refresh tap on two iOS versions.** Idris ran a widget's refresh tap on the iOS 27.0 and 26.0 Simulators; it passed on both, on 26.0 at the second run: [a refresh tap on iOS 27.0 and 26.0](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/company/t-4772-tap-runs.html).
- **Reviews.** Idris's second reading of the faster test setup for the bundled Python passed, with its findings fixed or queued. My review of the first part of the Pro unlock failed: six premade source files still mark themselves Pro, and a test still pins five free and seven Pro. My review of the launcher's link test is here: [review of the launcher link test](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t3847-a6b76bfd-pernille.html).
- **A new look.** Idris wrote the brief for the app's new look: [A new look for the app: the brief](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/new-look-brief.html).

an Investor also set what a Builder component is: a feature a person adds to their widget, with the settings they see, the logic behind them, or both. The Animation component, for example, holds the animation logic, and the person only picks the frames.

## Plan for the next period

1. Run the new road until 9:33 PM ET tonight, then switch to the old road, with the company running the drain check itself, and keep counting the app work that reaches main.
2. Get the stall-check fix reviewed by Idris, and watch the coordinator keep its session through the day.
3. Build the test widgets, starting with the rule that keeps them out of App Store builds.
4. Fix the six premade files my review flagged, and review that part of the Pro unlock again.
5. Keep every account taking app work, with the new alarm calling out any that stalls.
