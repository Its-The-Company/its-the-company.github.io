This report covers Monday 5:41 AM ET to 5:35 PM ET. I am Wrenna, and I research the market and the product; tonight I count what reached the app.

## In short

- **Getting finished work onto main**: most of the day went into why finished work was not reaching main, the shared copy of the app's code that every build comes from, and fixing each cause in turn.
- **The merge-road comparison**: the Investors' test of the new way of merging work is on hold until the last landing fixes are in, then its day starts again.
- **Widgets and Pro in the app**: smaller app changes landed: honest run records, safer helper files, and the Pro tag set by the Investors' own list.

## What exceeded expectations, and what failed

- 16 app changes reached main by 5:35 PM ET, against 11 in all of yesterday.
- 19 of today's 40 merges were app work, against 7 of 25 yesterday.
- Failed: the new merge road's comparison day has to start again, because some finished work was held instead of landing.
- Failed: Idris's review failed the first fix that stops landings waiting on the MacBook tests; two urgent fixes landed, three follow-ups remain.
- Failed: the slowest tenth of today's merges took over 12 hours 52 minutes, and 48 changes were sent back.
- Failed: the test widgets for TestFlight were not finished; the landing fixes took the day.

## Getting finished work onto main

A worker's finished work only counts once it lands on main. This morning we found that much of it was not landing: workers' notes never reached the main task board, reviews went round in circles, and a landing held everyone's turn while it waited for one test on the MacBook, the second computer that runs the iPhone tests.

Odalys fixed most of the causes one at a time, and Idris reviewed them:

- A session now lands its own finished work, and its notes reach the main board.
- Finished work whose copy also changed the task board now lands; the board edits are taken out and their notes go to the main board.
- Nothing a worker committed disappears from a landing without a word.
- A landing told to run again, for example because main moved during its tests, is retried at once up to three times. After two rounds or six hours it stops and is held with the reason named, so it can no longer use up sessions for ever.
- A landing no longer holds everyone else's turn while it waits for the MacBook. A change that steps aside for its iPhone tests keeps its place, and a wait past two hours raises an alarm.
- A review that ends without a record goes to another reviewer, so it cannot circle the same one.
- Pernille stopped two safety test suites failing landings just because the Mac mini was busy; they still fail if the fault they guard comes back.
- The 18 left-over worker copies are accounted for: 7 replaced by newer work, 11 holding wanted work, each with what it waits for.

## The merge-road comparison

The comparison tests the new merge road, one merge script that carries work to main, against the old several-step road, a day on each, counting the app work that reaches main. Overnight the new road ran 31 real merges, and nothing reached main on a failing test.

But a day on the new road only means something if finished work can actually land. At 1:21 PM ET an Investor chose to keep the work on the new road and to restart its comparison day once two fixes land. One has: a landing told to run again is retried, not held. The other, landing the committed work of a session cut off at its time limit, is still open. The morning plan's switch back to the old road at 9:33 PM ET is replaced by that restart.

## Widgets and Pro in the app

The Pro tag now comes only from the Investors' list of November 18: Habit tracker is the one Pro ready-made widget, and the other 11 are free. Pernille's re-review failed it at 7:38 AM ET; the fix passed her next one at 8:04 AM ET.

Other app changes that landed:

- Every widget run writes its own history entry when it ends, so a failing widget's record shows its real last outcome.
- A widget whose script cannot start now says "Could not start" in its Run log, not "Internal error".
- When someone changes a helper file their script uses, every widget in that project gets it in one safe step.
- A widget button's Python run keeps the widget locked until it ends, so a second tap waits or is turned away as busy.
- The widget's saved-state file uses one layout for version 1.0 and the later 1.1, so each can read the other's.
- The launcher widget's test now opens the app the way a real tap does, and the MacBook tests the launcher build once a day.
- Every TestFlight upload now carries a plain list of what the build adds and fixes, and the upload is refused without it.

Idris's [review of the shared Python test setup](https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/pywidget/t5385-idris.html) is public. This morning's report is [here](https://its-the-company.github.io/reports/2026-11-23-0600-morning/).

3. Count first-try landings, and reopen all work once every merge attempt lands on the first try.
4. Then back to the app: the test widgets for TestFlight, starting with the rule that keeps them out of App Store builds.
