## Finding

Not a hold, crash, refusal to start, LaunchAgent fault or 5-hour window. The supervisor ticked every 15.5
minutes all day (69 ticks on 10-26, no gap over 20 minutes) and each tick refused work with
`share_spent`: **Day 5's daily share was spent by 02:58**. The day's ledger had $281.54 against a work
line of $234.57 (90% of a $260.63 share).

Two things put it there:

1. **A run's cost went on the day it ended, not the day it started** (`tools/governor.py record`:
   `today = now.date()`). The 23:48 session of 10-25 started on that day's Investor concession
   (`pace_override.json` today_pct 33 until 2026-10-26 00:00, #41 c6407181591). It ran 66 agents and ended
   at 01:29, so its **$166.49 went on 10-26**: 59% of what Day 5 had spent by 03:00. Day 4 had $186 of that
   concession left when the session started, and 10-25 closed at $450.64 of a $636.80 share.
2. **The rest of the day's share was spent in a burst.** Pools of 25 to 31 agents at 01:36 and 02:25
   spent $114.72 in 82 minutes. Nothing spread the day's money across the day until f6b26ba9 (19:35 today,
   Investor-asked "spread to midnight").

The 5-hour window held work 03:10 to 04:43. The 04:12, 04:27 and 04:43 holds were false (see below). They
cost nothing today because the share gate was already shut.

## The false 5-hour hold (secondary)

At 04:12:24 the plan's meter read 0% with no reset time: the window had reset at 04:00 and no new one had
opened. `five_hour_status` uses the meter only when the reading has a reset time, so it fell through to the
rolling 5-hour estimate: $353.17 of runs since 23:12 over $330.31 = 106.9%. The plan itself said 0%.
61 of 260 meter readings to date have no reset (all 0%). This happened at 3 ticks in total, all on 10-26.

## Hours lost

- Wall-clock time with no work agent: 02:58:31 to 19:38:27 is **16.67 h**. Minus 5.7 minutes of reports,
  that leaves **16.6 h**.
- Caused by the day-booking defect. Had the 23:48 session's cost gone on 10-25, Day 5 at 04:59 would have
  had $115.06 used of $232.89 (work line $209.60). That is $94.54 left before the evening report and $117.83
  after it. At the governor's measured $10 per agent-hour (median of 34 sessions, f6b26ba9), that is
  **11.8 agent-hours not run on Day 5**.
- Caused by the burst: the rest of the gap. At the same money, a share spread to midnight runs 1 to 4 agents
  through the day instead of 25 to 66 for 3 hours and then none.
- Weekly limit: **$0 lost so far**. The unspent share stays in the week and is spread over the 6 days left.
  At 19:38 the week stood at $732.91 of $1929.70 (38%) with 25% of the week gone (pace ratio 1.635). What
  was lost is calendar time on Day 5, which Day 30 does not give back.
- Same pattern on 10-25: 11.7 h of `share_spent`. That day spent $237.01 by 07:18 against a $228.84 line
  (replay of c1af588f at 07:33), and $45.01 of the $237.01 came from a session that started on 10-24 at
  23:40.

## Fix

In tools/ (mine, Commandment 3), OPT-039 in log/optimizations.md:

1. `governor.record` puts a run's cost (days, cats, kinds, models, api_days) on the day it **started**,
   taken from its run file's name (`booking_day`). It trusts a start only from 0 to 6 h before now; the
   session limit is 210 minutes. Otherwise it uses the date now. Report state (last_*_date) stays on the
   clock's date. Daily caps stay in force: a session's `max_budget_usd` is capped at the starting day's
   share left, so the day that let the session start pays for it, up to that cap.
2. `five_hour_status` reads a meter reading with a percent and no reset time as "no window open". The
   figure is that reading plus runs since (last 5 h at most), not the rolling estimate.

Tests: tests/day_booking_test.py 14/0 (new; replays the 10-26 runs and the 04:59 gate both ways),
tests/five_hour_test.py 29 -> 36/0. In /tmp copies, each fix reverted alone fails its own checks
(3 and 4 fail). Removing only the 6-hour bound fails 1. governor_test 68/0, pacing_test 35/0, supervisor_test 153/0.

Not done, on purpose:
- ops/usage.json history is not rewritten. Moving $166.49 back to 10-25 now would reopen Day 5's share on
  top of the Investor's 19:45 LOCK IN override (today_pct 27.03, sized on "$282 already used"), so the
  same money would be counted twice.
- The spread (f6b26ba9) has no ledger entry. OPT-039's metric measures both changes together, and the
  entry says so.

Open: the week boundary is 02:00 but the ledger works in whole days. A day's runs before 02:00 count in the
new week (`in_window` tests noon). The meter's weekly figure bounds the error. Not fixed here.
