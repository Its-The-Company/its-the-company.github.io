## Evidence

CLAUDE.md after the T-1211 fold (54ddc8dd), Commandment 52: "Reports are a daily update: they carry no list or panel of
open requests, and a report session opens no request issue. Requests are raised outside reports and flagged on the
site's Issues page (Investors, 2026-10-31)." Commandment 55 likewise puts open requests on the site's Issues tab.
Lines that still contradict it:
- setup/OPERATIONS.md:267: "Every report's Needs-you panel lists each open proposal issue (Commandment 55)."
- setup/OPERATIONS.md:284: "the report's Needs-you panel lists every open one, and a decision the period raised ..."
- setup/INVESTORS.md:157: "the report's **Needs you** panel lists every open one; a reply of \"approve\" ..."

## Effect

Edits only those three sentences in setup/OPERATIONS.md and setup/INVESTORS.md to say the site's Issues page flags open
requests and reports list none. No code reads these lines; no behaviour changes, only the text workers and Investors read.

## Revert rule

If any test or tool that reads these files breaks, or an Investor disapproves, `git revert` the applied commit.
