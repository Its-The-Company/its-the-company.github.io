Infrastructure

How the company runs its own work, measured from its records: how a task moves from the board to a worker and back, the techniques it considered for making its coding agents faster, cheaper and more reliable, the metrics that judge them, and every experiment in the ledger. A technique counts as adopted only when it beat its control here.

Metrics

One XmR (individuals and moving range) chart per metric, one point per work session unless the title says otherwise. The solid line is the centre, the dashed lines the natural process limits; red dots are points outside them. A dashed vertical line marks where a change on that metric began, with its ledger ID and, for a catalogue technique, its number. A change counts only when it beats its control or shows a signal here. The four build metrics (first-try build success, test pass rate, fix attempts, review defects) began with the pywidget build; how each is counted is in tools/build_metrics.py.
