## Headline

17 merges by 11:19 PM carried 29 app tasks; an 18th (T-4191 batch 2, main) merged at 11:49 PM. Most of the time went to:

1. **iPhone tests failing on the MacBook.** 37 of 146 test runs failed or timed out (25%). Those runs used 6 h 12 min of runner time, and each one sent its work back for a fix and a rerun. Worst: T-5630 failed 5 times, T-4577 4, T-1457 4.
2. **Account b sat idle.** No b session ran for 3 h 43 min, in 7 gaps (1:47 to 10:50 PM), because nothing was allowed to start: T-4192 was "cooling" until 5:08 PM after earlier tries that made no commit, and T-4188's files were in use elsewhere. On top of that it ran 5 short sessions (2 to 3 min each) on T-4777, which had already merged at 1:35 PM. Lending other accounts' work to b only began at 9:05 PM.
3. **Reviews failing and re-running.** 7 app reviews failed on real findings: T-4183, T-5047, T-4776, T-5795, T-4476, T-5640 and T-5796 batch 2. The coordinator's 10:44 PM hand-off says each round costs 30 to 90 min. T-4476 also went red when it was rebased, after its review had passed.

Smaller causes: the MacBook queue waited 424 min in total, 296 of them between 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM, while the MacBook ran 3 jobs at once (capacity tests from 1:18 PM, then 6 from 4:35 PM). The median wait was 25 s. The main session lost about 40 min to restarts (12:29 PM to 1:03 PM and 11:13 AM to 11:19 PM). From 8:00 PM the Mac mini's load was high (median 82 to 155, peak 308), but the records don't say how many minutes that cost.
