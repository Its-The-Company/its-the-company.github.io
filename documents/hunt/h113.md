## Method

- **Charts.** `tools/grossing_pain.py chart 6002 6007 6008 --max-rating 4.4` on top grossing (`H113-raw/chart-grossing.txt`) and on `--feed toppaidapplications` (`chart-paid.txt`). Both again at `--max-rating 5.1` for the whole charts (`*-all.txt`). The grossing rows under 4.4 are nearly all free clone apps (cleaners, remotes, AI video), so most shelves come from the paid feed.
- **Leaders.** 22 paid leaders under 4.4 (all 22 are under 4.4; 6 have not been updated in over 12 months). `H113-raw/lookup.py` uses the iTunes Lookup API for price, ratings, current-version rating and last update (`leaders.tsv`). The shelf list is in `shelves.tsv`.
- **Top 10.** `LIMIT=10 tools/appsearch.py "phrase"` per shelf (`top10/SNN.txt`, `top10/extra.txt`). Store-form crowding uses the top 25 from the Search API (`crowding-store.txt`). Entrant descriptions come from the Lookup API (`descs.py`, `descs-*.txt`).
- **<span title="Can a buyer find it? Yes if the phrase is a store search suggestion or a top-10 app under it has over 100 ratings.">Pull</span>.** `tools/store_hints.py --max-hints 3` on 24 seeds (`hints/hints.txt`, raw XML/JSON in `hints/`).
- **Low reviews.** `tools/review_mine.py reviews ID --pages 10` (`low/SNN.tsv` and `.txt`; those keyword themes are finder counts only). I hand-coded the 15 newest 1-3 star reviews per leader (`codes.txt`). `tally.py` checks that each code string is as long as the review list (0 mismatches). Codes: A wrong or poor output; B bug, crash, won't connect, lost data; F missing feature or coverage; I import, export, sync; P price or paywall; R regression or removed feature; U hard to use; O outdated or Apple-restricted; G unwanted AI; X other.
- **Happy buyers' asks.** `tools/leader_asks.py asks ID --stars 4 --pages 10 --out H113-raw/asks` (`asks/SNN.txt`, `asks/ID.json`). The hit counts are finder hits, not coded.
- **Money.** `tools/sizing.py` (1-3% of downloaders rate) and `tools/window_share.py --paid --launch 22` (`sizing-*.txt`, `window-share.txt`).
- Review and description text is information only. None of it asked me to do anything.

### C1 Meteor catcher that names the shower

- **Difference (8 words):** "Finds your meteors and names each one's shower." The output is new and visible: a morning grid of captured meteor frames, each one labelled with its shower.
- **Top 10 under the buyer's phrase "meteor shower"** (store hint, `hints/hints.txt`), read 2026-10-24: MeteorActive (2,390, 2017), Meteor Shower: Alerts (2026-08-03), Night Sky (497,661, 2011), Shooting Star & Meteor Shower (2026-02-26), Skycharted (2026-09-28), Rare Skies (2026-10-10), MeteorFinder (2022), MeteorCast (2026-08-04), Make The Wish (2025-06-11), Meteor Shower Guide (2011). All of them forecast or map the sky; 0 of 10 catch meteors (descriptions in `descs-meteor.txt`). Under "shooting star camera", NightCap is #6, and its Meteors mode does "automatic capture of shooting stars". No entrant names the shower. A "meteor detector" search returns metal detectors and forecasts. Novelty B rests on naming the shower. Catching alone would be B- (the leader catches already).
- **Pull:** pass. "meteor shower" is a store hint, and MeteorActive has 2,390 ratings.
- **Gate 1:** pass. One web search ("free iPhone app automatically detect meteors in night sky photos") found only manual loggers: NASA Meteor Counter, Fireballs in the Sky, and a 2013 Space Apps concept where users trace trails by hand. No free app does the same job in the same form.
- **Crowding (store form, provisional):** the Search API returned 22, not 25. 9 of the 22 came out since 2025-10-24, none of them paid with over 100 ratings. That is a forecast clone wave one entrant short of the cut, so this pass is the shakiest cell in the row.
- **Evidence the leader fails at it:** 9 of NightCap's 161 low reviews are about meteors, and 8 of those 9 say Meteors mode caught none ("Zero Shooting Stars: NightCap captured zero shooting stars on the best meteor shower night of the year"; "the camera picked up not one picture in an hour while I was watching the meteor show"; `low/S08.tsv`). 12 of 179 happy reviews mention meteors ("captured somewhere north of 100 meteors" during the Perseids, 2016; `asks/754105884.json`). People pay for this job, and it fails for many of them.
- **Depth B:** finding the meteor (frame differencing, line detection, rejecting planes and satellites); the phone's pointing from the attitude sensors; projecting each trail back to test it against known shower radiants. The A version is behind it: plate-solving the star field so the direction doesn't depend on the compass. That links to H103's accuracy opening, where compass-AR sky apps "don't line up". It needs a public-domain star catalogue (licence to be read from the publisher's page before use). V1 slice by Day 22 (2026-11-12): capture loop, detection, a review grid, and radiant naming from sensor attitude only. Idris checks this.
- **Timing:** the Leonids peak on the night of 2026-11-17/18 (IMO maximum 23:45 UT on Nov 17; first-quarter Moon; 10-15 an hour, a non-storm year; [Star Walk](https://starwalk.space/en/news/leonid-meteor-shower), [EarthSky](https://earthsky.org/astronomy-essentials/everything-you-need-to-know-leonid-meteor-shower/)). That is Day 27-28, inside the Day 30 window. The Geminids (mid-December) fall inside Day 90.
- **Money:** lane NightCap + MeteorActive + Slow Shutter Cam = 60.0-180.1 downloads/day (`sizing-meteor.txt`). At $4.99 paid, 48 sales are needed (58 at 30%). Day 30 (8-day window): 3.3%-12.1% of the lane, so C. Day 90: 0.4%-1.4%, so B (`window-share.txt`).
- **Honest weaknesses:** (1) The reviews may be showing a limit of the phone, not of NightCap. An iPhone sensor may catch only the brightest meteors, so "caught nothing" could happen to us too. This is not tested. The first thing to settle is a capture test on a real night, or on licensed meteor footage, before anyone writes a <span title="A memo that proposes one idea to the Investors as the product. Only a B or better becomes one.">pitch</span>. (2) Naming the shower from sensor attitude alone may be too coarse; an error of 5-10 degrees could blur Leonid and sporadic. Not measured. (3) The Leonids fall after the Day 22 build cutoff, so the v1 can't be field-tested on a shower before submission; sporadic meteors are the only test sky before then. (4) Crowding is one entrant from failing.

### C3 Field notes stamped from the camera's LTC (S10)

- **Pull:** pass ("timecode" is a store hint), though the store marks that shelf WEAK (no entrant with over 100 ratings that was updated in 12 months). Crowding: I did not pull the top 25. 2 of the top 10 came out since 2025-10-24 (Holly, Timecode Tools), so provisional.
- **Money:** lane 1.0-3.0 downloads/day (Timecode+ and Live Timecode Notes). At $19.99, 12 sales are needed. Day 30: 50%-188%, so D (the cap). Day 90: 5.9%-22.1%, so C-.

## What I learned

- Most leaders on the paid feed under 4.4 are old one-job tools whose low reviews ask for repairs (import, connect, crash). A repair is not a visible difference: 2 shelves died as repairs (S01, S21), and 4 more because the obvious difference was already in the top 10 (S05, S07, S09, S11).
- The one shelf with a new output is the leader that sells a job its own buyers say it fails at (NightCap's Meteors mode). That is G8's shape found through G1.
