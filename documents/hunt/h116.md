## 1. Method

- **Doors from the SDK.** `python3 tools/sdk_doors.py new --since 26` (35 frameworks, raw `H116-raw/counts.txt`; same list as H24). `sdk_doors.py grep` over 37 sensor, camera, watch, haptics, speech, model, UWB, LiDAR and RoomPlan frameworks (`H116-raw/scan.sh`, `grep/`).
- **Tool gap found.** `sdk_doors.py` reads the iOS version only from `@available(iOS N` and `ios(N)`. It misses `@available(anyAppleOS 26, *)`, which Speech uses throughout, and it sometimes misses `@available(macOS 26.0, iOS 26.0, ...)`. Speech showed 0 new declarations and Vision under-counted. `H116-raw/scan2.py` reads both forms (`grep2/`, `counts2.txt`): Speech 32 at 26.x and 9 at 27.x; Vision 19 and 13; FoundationModels 129 and 194. sdk_doors.py should take the same fix; I have not edited it (outside this <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Each lane has its own file.">lane</span>'s files).
- **No door at all (0 declarations marked iOS 26 or 27 in this SDK, Xcode 27.1):** CoreHaptics, RoomPlan, WatchConnectivity, WorkoutKit, SoundAnalysis, VisionKit, Cinematic, AccessorySetupKit. Haptics and RoomPlan opened nothing new this year. The one LiDAR-adjacent change is in ARKit (door 14).
- **Apple documentation.** `H116-raw/fetch_docs.py` reads `developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/<path>.json` for the title, platforms and abstract (`docs.txt`). Every door below cites its page.
- **Who already names it.** `tools/capability_scan.py` with a door-specific `CAP` regex, LIMIT=25, two phrases per door (`H116-raw/scans.sh`, `cap/NN.txt`). The US store answered 403 and 429 to about half the first pass. `rerun.sh` re-ran them 35 s apart. Doors 7 (table) and 19 (EnergyKit) stayed 403 on every try. Doors 14 and 16 have their first phrase only. Nothing was bypassed.
- **Share of iPhones.** Apple's only own figure (`developer.apple.com/support/app-store/`, `appstore-share.txt`): 86% of iPhones introduced in the last four years used iOS 26, as measured on 2026-06-07. Apple publishes no iOS 27 figure yet, and no share by model or sensor, so every sensor cell says "unknown". Where I say which hardware a door needs, it comes from the API (a `isSupported` property), not from a share.
- **Day 17 (2026-11-07), one M1 Mac mini.** My judgment as the engineer who would build it. "yes" means testable end to end on this Mac or the Simulator before a device exists. "device" means it needs real hardware: the Simulator has no camera, UWB, Bluetooth radio, LiDAR or motion sensors, and our membership (Day 7) comes before any device test. "no" means hardware we don't own.
- **Pull** as H113: a store hint (`tools/store_hints.py`), or a top-10 entrant with over 100 ratings. The hints call itself hit 403 after its first seed (`hints.txt`), so only "song key" has hints.

## 5. What H116 tells us

1. This year's sensor doors are mostly in the camera and in Apple's models (Speech, Vision, Music Understanding, Media Intelligence). Haptics, RoomPlan, watch connectivity and WorkoutKit opened nothing in iOS 26 or 27.
2. The hardware doors (Channel Sounding, DL-TDOA, AR object tracking, cinematic and spatial capture) can't be tested on one M1 Mac mini by Day 17. Under method v2's Fit cell, that removes them for v1.
3. Every software door that runs on this Mac already has an entrant within weeks of the release (TempoKey 2026-09-17 on Music Understanding, Momently 2026-08-06 on highlights), as H24 found. A door gives a head start of weeks, and the difference has to come from what we build on it.
4. Tool fix for the next G3 lane: sdk_doors.py's version regex (section 1).
