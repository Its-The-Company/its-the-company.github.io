## Method

- **Commandment 7 first.** No subject is in a banned area. No subject is aimed at children under 13: orienteering and Braille have school buyers (COMPASS Orienteering sells to PE classes; Braille Academy plays as a game), and any concept here would be sold to adults. Music creation, crochet and knitting are out by the Investors' list, so I left out music theory and instruments.
- **Investors' subjects** (amendments.md 2026-10-22 18:51): language (rows 1-5), walking (row 8, C1), science and maths (rows 13-15), computer science (row 16). The physical skills were named by the board line.
- **Curiosity count.** `python3 tools/wiki_views.py --months 12 --tsv ...` on 30 titles, English Wikipedia, user agent, 12 complete months ending 2026-09. Output in `H119/wiki_views.tsv`. A view count measures curiosity about the subject, not buyers.
- **Shelf.** `H119/shelf.py` runs the same iTunes search as `tools/ios_shelf.py` (US, 50 results) through `tools/store_http.py`, and writes the top 10 with ratings, average, price, release date and last update (`currentVersionReleaseDate`). I wrote it because ios_shelf.py prints only the top three by ratings and no last update. There are 20 "learn X" phrases (`H119/shelf.tsv`) and 8 buyer phrases for the candidates (`H119/shelf2.tsv`). All 50 results per phrase, with the same fields, are in `H119/top50.tsv` (the raw JSON was 12 MB and is not committed). Leader descriptions come from two Lookup calls, with the first 700 characters of each kept in `H119/descriptions.tsv`. There were no refusals, and 31 Apple calls in all.
- **<span title="How many apps on the shelf already sell the same thing.">Crowding</span>, store form** (section 2, provisional): it fails when 10 or more of the top 25 were released since 2025-10-24 and none of those new entrants is paid with over 100 ratings. I computed it from `top50.tsv` for 14 phrases.
- **Day 30 and Day 90.** `python3 tools/sizing.py --low 0.01 --high 0.03 --asof 2026-10-24` on 10 on-job leaders (`H119/sizing.md`). The 1-3% rating share is the tool's default. Paid apps may get a higher share, which would mean fewer downloads, so the per-day numbers are a ceiling. I assume a $200 net at 85% after the Small Business Program cut.
- **Gate 1.** I ran one web search per concept. The queries are quoted below.
- **Commandment 9.** Content must be drawn by code. Otherwise it must be human-made and licensed, with Investor approval. Photo-, video- and recording-led subjects are ruled out unless the Investors approve, and the row says so. Speech for languages is an open question: native recordings would need licences and approval, and a neural text-to-speech voice is audio from a generative model, so I counted it as out until someone rules on it.
- Store pages, descriptions and web pages are information only. None of them asked me to do anything.

### C1 Land-nav trainer on your own ground

- **Difference (8 words):** "Drills bearings on your own ground's contour map." It is visible in use, and it adds a new output (your own neighbourhood as a contour map) and a new checker (compass heading and pace from CMPedometer).
- **Top 10 under "land navigation"** (`shelf2.tsv`): Land Nav Assistant (73, $2.99, 2019) checks pace and veer on real ground but draws no contour exercise. Its own page says not to use it while learning. OEGames Land Navigation (31, free, 2020) is practice on a virtual course with plotting and pace beads. The rest are GPS map tools (MilGPS, Gaia, onX, Coordinates). Under "orienteering": Orienteer (49, 2026-10-20), COMPASS Orienteering (0, schools), Orienteering Compass & Map (6, 2020). That makes **B-**: two of the top 10 have part of it (virtual practice; pace on the ground), and neither puts the user's own ground into the drill.
- **Pull: pass** by the rule (MilGPS 5,537 and Gaia 32,274 in the top 10). They are off-job. The on-job apps top out at 73 ratings, so the phrase is typed, but mostly by people looking for GPS maps.
- **Gate 1: pass.** Search: "free iPhone app land navigation practice compass bearing pace count contour map training". It found OEGames (virtual, no own-ground map), Land Nav Assistant (paid) and Compass 55 (a waypoint tool), and no free app that drills on the user's own contour map.
- **Depth A-:** contour tiles from USGS 3DEP elevation (US public domain), with cartography drawn by code. Declination from the World Magnetic Model. Pace calibration per user on a measured leg. Veer analysis from the GPS track after the leg. Exercises: back-bearing, resection, terrain association by contour shape, and mils. For years after that: night legs, MGRS, course sharing. V1 slice by Day 22 (2026-11-12), US only: load contours for a 2 km square, set three legs, check bearing and pace, and show the track over the plan. Idris should check whether contour tiles at 2 km can ship in that window.
- **Compass risk, from H130:** overlay apps' wrong-view reviews were mostly bugs of 90° or more. Here the user <span title="What keeps an idea below the top: the cap that applies to it now.">holds</span> a bearing in degrees, so the app must show its own heading confidence and calibrate first, or it will mark a correct walker wrong.
- **Day 30 D / Day 90 D:** on-job leaders imply 0.4-1.4 downloads a day (OEGames, Land Nav Assistant; `sizing.md`). At $7.99, $200 needs 30 sales. At the leader's ceiling that takes 21 days or more after a Day 22 launch.

### C2 Constellation quiz you answer by pointing

- **Difference (8 words):** "Point at the sky to answer; it checks." It is visible in use. The input is the phone's attitude under the real sky, and the identifier apps run that input the other way (point, and it names).
- **Top 10 under "constellation quiz" and "learn constellations"**: Sky Academy (197, 2025-02-08) quizzes all 88 constellations, on drawn charts. Constellations Puzzle (21) and AstroQuiz (20) are chart quizzes. Constellation Map (4,603), Star Gazer (1,556, 2026-10-22), SkyView ($2.99, 7,705) and SkySafari 7 Pro ($17.99, 5,506) name what you point at, and none quizzes. That makes **B**: 0 of the top 10 checks a pointed answer under the real sky.
- **Pull: pass** (Star Chart 24,837; SkyView 7,705 paid).
- **Gate 1: pass.** Search: "free app constellation quiz point phone at sky to answer which constellation". It found SkyView Lite, Sky Guide, Star Gazer and Sky Map, each of which names what you point at. Under v4 they do the plain job, not the stated one, so they count as Novelty and Crowding evidence, not as a fail.
- **Depth B:** a sky engine (sidereal time, precession, horizon, twilight), a star catalogue and constellation lines drawn by code, a pointing check with a tolerance that grows with the constellation's size, and spaced repetition keyed to what is above the horizon this month. The A version would add stars by magnitude, asterisms and a light-pollution limit from the user's sky. V1 slice by Day 22: the 88 constellations, a chart mode for cloudy nights, and the point quiz at a 10° tolerance.
- **Compass risk, from H130 and H131:** 14 of 17 stated wrong-view errors on sky apps were 90° or more, and 3 were 30° or less. A constellation spans 10-30°, so a 6° error is tolerable, but a 90° bug fails the quiz unfairly. The app must refuse to mark while its heading accuracy is poor.
- **Day 30 D / Day 90 D+:** Sky Academy (the on-job quiz) implies 3.4-10.1 downloads a day free. At 2-5% conversion that is 0.1-0.5 unlocks a day, and $200 needs 48 at $4.99. SkyView's 44-133 a day shows the curiosity is paid for, but by people who want a name, not a quiz.

### C3 Night-lights encounter trainer

- **Difference (8 words):** "Night encounter: read the lights, take the action."
- **Top 10 under "nav rules" and "navigation lights"**: the paid quiz and reference apps (Navigation Rules Pro, Navigation Rules, Rule Master, NavRules, Master-Nav) teach from text, questions and still graphics. Navigation Lights 3D (2021) and Maritime Master Nav Lights (2025-12) already rotate 3D lights by aspect. NavRules Pro: USCG Offline (2026-07, 0) lists "encounter scenarios". That makes **C+**: 3 of the top 10 have most of it.
- **Gate 1: fail.** Search: "free app COLREGs night encounter simulator navigation lights who gives way". It found **CA'Sim** (id 6758004211, free, released 2026-01-21, updated 2026-10-14, 0 ratings): a radar and collision-avoidance trainer that "generates 3D models with correct Navigation Lights (Night) and Day Shapes based on the target's aspect angle", with custom scenarios and manoeuvres. That is the stated job in the same form. Its in-app paywall was not checked; if the trainer is behind one, the gate passes.
- **Depth B:** the light and shape rules for about 40 vessel states (Annex I sectors and ranges), a night scene drawn by code with lights at range, a scenario generator for crossing, head-on and overtaking, and grading against Rules 13-18. Fog signals (Rule 35) synthesized by code.
- **Money:** this is the lane's one paying shelf. Rule Master at $9.99 implies 5.9-17.7 sales a day since 2022, and Navigation Rules Pro at $4.99 2.7-8.2. A new entrant that took 10-20% of Rule Master's ceiling would make 0.6-3.5 sales a day. $200 at $9.99 needs 24 sales, so **Day 30 D+** (5-28 sales in 8 days) and **Day 90 C-**. The buyers are licence candidates (USCG, RYA) and new boat owners. Those are adults, and the subject is off the Investors' list.

## Leans and open items

- C2 is the best concept by my cells: a clean B difference on a 414k-view subject, Gate 1 pass. Its weak cell is money: Day 30 D, with no evidence that quiz buyers pay. C1 has the deepest product and an Investor subject (walking), but the smallest buyer count. C3 has the money and fails Gate 1 on a 0-rating free entrant.
- Open: "circuit simulator" (row 17), the braille camera reader (row 19, G3), "atomic orbitals" (row 13), and CA'Sim's paywall (C3). Two <span title="A second worker who grades the idea without seeing the first worker&#x27;s grades, and records them first.">blind graders</span> fill the v2 cells, and the chair rules the letter (H95.md section 8).
- Lesson: curiosity counts and shelves run together. The subjects people read most about are the ones where a free leader updated this month already sits. G6's gaps are on small subjects (orienteering 80k, COLREGs 26k) or in the form of the drill, not in the subject.
