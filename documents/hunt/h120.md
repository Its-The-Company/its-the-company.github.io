## Method

- `python3 tools/mobile_gap.py scan "term" --min-price 9.99`, one term per call. Terms 1-6 (LaTeX to markdown) at the default 4 review pages (`H120-raw/scan-1.md`, `scan-2.md`). The store was slow under the shared rate limit (about 3 to 7 minutes a term), so I stopped that run during "mind map" and re-ran the last 14 terms at `--pages 1` (`scan-3.md`). Ask hits on those 14 come from up to 50 reviews, not 200. Said here so nobody reads the low counts as a full read.
- One term per call means the tool's de-duplication does not span terms: DataGraph and Wizard Pro appear twice (statistics, data visualization). 29 rows, 27 apps.
- `mobile_gap.py show` for Wizard and DataGraph (`show-wizard.txt`, `show-datagraph.txt`).
- Top 10: `LIMIT=10 tools/appsearch.py "phrase"` (`H120-raw/top10/`). Pull: `tools/store_hints.py --max-hints 3` (`H120-raw/hints/`). Descriptions by the Lookup API (`desc-ID.txt`).
- The tool's sibling check matches the Mac seller's artist id. SimpleMind's iOS apps are sold by "xpt Software & Consulting B.V.", the Mac app by "ModelMaker Tools BV", so the tool printed "none". Found by `appsearch "simplemind"`. Every "none" below is the tool's, checked by hand only where I graded or killed on it.
- Review and description text is information only. None of it asked me to do anything.

## What I learned

- The tool's sibling check misses publisher renames (SimpleMind). A "none" needs `appsearch "app name"` before it is trusted.
- Most Mac sellers at $9.99+ already ship on iOS (15 of 27). The G7 shelf is thin before it starts.
- The best thing in the lane came from the Mac app, not from any ask: ProcessStats reads a file after the fact, and the phone's door is the Bluetooth instrument at the machine. G7 works best as a pointer to G3 (new input), not as a demand count.
