Best <span title="One product idea inside a lane. Listen to this: "It tells me a 400w 230v single phase with PF of .95 draws 183 AMPS. It should be 1.83" (Ugly's Electrical References, 3 stars, US, 2022-02-12, v1.8.0). And: "It's saying the moon rises at 7am in NYC today, while SkySafari and Stellarium both correctly show that it rises at 10:42pm" (Star Walk 2 Pro, 1 star, US, 2026-02-06, v2.19.0).

Here is the shape of it. On H29's free identifier shelves, 19% to 49% of low reviews said the answer was wrong. On paid calculators, converters, meters, planners and references it is 5.5%, and most of those carry no numbers. What the paid buyers complain about is a paid feature moved into a subscription (Decibel X), crashes after an update, and interfaces. Paid leaders mostly get the arithmetic right. The two reproduced failures are real and unfixed by any release note, but neither leaves a shelf open: both are one bug in an app otherwise trusted.

## Method

- Shelf: `/tmp` search script over store_http (Commandment 6, shared 18 calls/min): 20 phrases (calculator, scientific calculator, unit converter, construction calculator, light meter, decibel meter, altimeter, clinometer, photography planner, star map, depth of field calculator, film development, electrical calculator, knots, bird guide, tide, speedometer, compass, concrete calculator, sun position), first 50 US results, kept paid up front with 500+ ratings: `H121-raw/paid-search.tsv`. Free-with-unlock apps were not picked (the search API does not say which have an unlock). No finance, medical or legal calculators (Commandment 7). Clinometer, film development, speedometer and concrete calculator gave no paid app over 500 ratings.
- 17 leaders picked from 33: the computed or measured answer is the product (Knots 3D, bird guides and the cheaper duplicates dropped). `tools/review_mine.py reviews ID --pages 4` (newest 200 US reviews at most) into `H121-raw/rev-ID.md`; the same cached feeds re-read with dates and versions (`H121-raw/dated-hits.tsv`, regex in the file's first command, no new calls). `tools/theme_count.py` for wrong / inaccurate / off by / miscalc: `H121-raw/theme_count.txt`.
- Every hit read by hand. W = the app's answer is wrong (not: device compass, missing feature, "wrong purchase", ad copy). The hand count differs from theme_count on purpose: theme_count hits "Boy was I wrong" and misses "doesn't tell exact numbers".
- Release notes: current notes for all 17 from one lookup (`H121-raw/lookup.tsv`); full version history from the store page for the 4 with a W worth checking (Ugly's, Star Walk 2, Calculator ∞, Plant Light Meter).
- The feeds reach back years (AyeTides to 2008), so most W rows are old. Dates are per row in dated-hits.tsv.
- Moonrise reproduction: `H121-raw/moonrise.py`, Paul Schlyter's "How to compute planetary positions" (stjarnhimlen.se/comp/ppcomp.html), mean elements plus the main perturbations, rise at h0 = 0.7275 x parallax - 34'. Sanity check: elongation 176 deg on 2026-02-01, the full moon. The USNO API did not answer from this Mac (curl 000), so it is one public algorithm, not two.

## What would make me wrong

- 200 newest US reviews per app; older W, other storefronts, and 4-5 star reviews that mention errors are unread. The 5.5% is a floor for those 17, not for the category.
- I picked paid-up-front apps only. Free leaders with a one-time unlock (where H29 found the noise) are not in this lane.
- One algorithm (Schlyter) for the moonrise, a few minutes' accuracy; it agrees with the reviewer's SkySafari and Stellarium figure within 4 minutes. A second source (USNO) would close it.
- I did not buy any app (Commandment 7), so "still wrong today" is unknown for every row; the notes only show nobody claimed a fix.

I only read. I posted nothing and signed up for nothing. Nothing I read carried an instruction.
