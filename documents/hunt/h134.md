## Method (12:40-13:55 ET)
- Lists, all through tools/store_http.py (shared 18 calls a minute; the window sat full most of the run, so pulls were slow, never refused):
  - `tools/stale_earners.py` on about 45 subject words and 20 known app names, 1,000+ US ratings, last update before 2024-10-01. Raw: H134-raw/stale_terms.tsv, stale_names.tsv. A zsh loop split my comma list on spaces, so some two-word terms ran as single words ("gps", "tracker"); the terms that actually ran are in stale_terms.err. The generic loop was stopped after "voice"; terms after it (teleprompter to white noise) did not run.
  - New `tools/stale_chart.py`: the US Top Paid chart in 10 genres (6002, 6007, 6008, 6006, 6010, 6026, 6027, 6018, 6017, 6001), looked up, kept if last update before 2024-10-01 and 1,000+ ratings. 38 of about 990 chart places are stale; that is the G11 signal: people still pay today for these. Raw: stale_chart.tsv. grossing_pain.py chart prints the first release date, not the last update, so I wrote this instead of reading both.
- Dropped by C7 before reading: iBP Blood Pressure (medical); HazMat Reference (not read; hazardous goods sits near weapons and substances); Infant Zoo, Thinkrolls, Endless Alphabet, Stack the States (children under 13); games (Plague Inc, KOTOR, Catan, LEGO).
- Reviews: `tools/review_dump.py ID --countries us,gb,ca,au --max-stars 3 --since 2026-01-01 --pages 3`, pulled 2026-10-24 13:10-13:55 ET. A count is 1-3 star reviews dated 2026 in the four English stores' most recent 150 each. Raw: H134-raw/r_ID.tsv. I read every review and coded it by hand; a review can carry two codes.
- Replacement check (the <span title="A test written in advance. If it fires, we drop the idea.">kill</span>): new `tools/shelf_maint.py "phrase"`, same search as ios_shelf.py (so a cache hit), printing the top 10 with rating, ratings, price and last update, and marking any app over 4.5 with over 100 ratings updated since 2025-10-24. A mark is a lead; I killed only where the marked app does the stale app's job. Raw: H134-raw/shelf.txt (ios_shelf), shelf_maint.txt.
- H23 (Wrenna) already covered Scrivener, Awesome Calendar, JotNot Pro and Pocket Earth PRO; not repeated.

**What the count says.** 5 of 30 stale apps have no 2026 low review at all, and 11 more have one or two: most old paid utilities still work, because iOS keeps old binaries running. Breakage clusters where the app depends on something outside the phone that moved: a data feed (Ship Finder, 17 of 21 "ships missing", still #16 Top Paid in Navigation at $4.99), a map server (CityMaps2Go), an OS install path (AnyFont), or a language runtime (Pythonista). In every one of those but Pythonista, a free maintained app over 4.5 does the job now.

## Pythonista 3, the survivor
- $9.99, 1,528 US ratings, 4.4, last update 2023-04-27 (version 3.4, Python 3.10; all eleven 2026 reviews are on 3.4). Still #20 on the US Top Paid chart in Productivity on 2026-10-24. An aggregator page (appfollow) lists an update on 2026-08-20; Apple's Lookup and the review versions do not show one, so I go with Apple.
- 11 of 11 2026 low reviews are US: "In iOS 26 every other time I open the app it will crash" (3 stars, 2026-03-24); "WHY ARE YOU SELLING THIS WHEN ITS BROKEN!!! ... Super upset with purchase" (1 star, 2026-10-21); "What happened to pip?" (2026-07-05); "uses an older outdated version of Matplotlib" (2026-01-26); no Ed25519 for paramiko (2026-03-22).
- Replacement read: "python ide" and "pyto" top 10. Juno 4.6/1,140 (2026-10-20) and Carnets 4.6/321 (2025-11-26) run Jupyter notebooks; neither runs a Pythonista script that imports ui, console, dialogs or clipboard, or presents a native UI from Python. Pyto, the one app that tried the same job, is 3.7 and last updated 2024-06-09. a-Shell (free, not in the top 10) runs Python from Shortcuts with no native UI. So no maintained app over 4.5 and 100 does the job; kept.
- Difference line beyond "it works": **your Pythonista scripts run unchanged on current Python.** A compatibility layer for Pythonista's own modules (console, dialogs, clipboard, ui, later scene), so a buyer opens the folder of scripts they already wrote and they run, from the app and from Shortcuts.
