Ines, 2026-10-24, written and committed before any measurement below runs. Board line: <span title="A numbered batch of planned work, with its own plan file.">Refill</span> 16, "H135 the lift memo". This file asks the other side of "no B yet": not why each B- is not B, but what would make it B, and whether we can find out.

**Result: no B- can be lifted to B by a fact the company can measure from the Mac mini today. Three (True Bearing, the four v1 rows' v2 regrade, the cycle listener's store <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Each lane has its own file.">lane</span>) have a fact we can measure; for two of those it is a 14-day wait, not a lookup. Two (meteor catcher, true-north) turn on a fact only a phone in the field or a build spike can give. One (This Spot Through Time) has no lift a measurement can give: its lane is too small at any reading. Six lines go on the board.**

## What <span title="What keeps an idea below the top: the cap that applies to it now.">holds</span> the tier at B-

Read against <span title="Our written method for finding and grading ideas, revised for issue #41.">method-v2</span>.md lines 56-62. The <span title="Our grade scale, nine steps: D, D+, C-, C, C+, B-, B, B+, A. An idea&#x27;s ladder is the letter it reaches before any cap.">ladder</span> reference (<span title="How different the idea is from what the App Store already sells.">Novelty</span>, then <span title="How much room the idea has to grow. It moves the grade up or down a step or two.">Depth</span>) is B or better for five of the eight (True Bearing, meteor catcher, true-north, This Spot Through Time, circuit simulation; the listener has no v2 Novelty yet; Anki and Ledger sit at B-). The <span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">cap</span> that binds where one binds is <span title="Our arithmetic estimate of what the idea would earn by day 30 (the $200 deadline) or day 90. It never raises a grade.">Day 30</span> at D, which caps at B-: it holds True Bearing and This Spot Through Time today, and would hold the listener under v2. Day 30 is arithmetic (window_share.py): an 8-day window from a Day 22 launch, sales needed at the price, divided by the lane's downloads. A lane leaves D when the worst case (low lane, 30% rate) needs 50% or less of the 8-day lane (bands as Idris's H113 grades file wrote them: under 2% B, 2-15% C, 15-50% D+ to C-, over 50% D). For a paid app that is: lane low per day at least sales-at-30% / 4.

So the honest finding: in a 8-day window, a small paid <span title="The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.">shelf</span> of a few hundred ratings never leaves D, and an unlock route almost never does. The lift facts below are mostly facts about lane size, because that is what holds us. No price change is made here to escape a cap: a price set after the cap is seen is the rule bent (if a price is ever argued, it must be one a top-10 on-job app with 100+ ratings already charges, ruled by a chair before the arithmetic is rerun).

## Two rules written now

1. **A lift fact lifts at most one step, to B.** A B produced by this memo is checked by one blind re-grade before any <span title="A memo that proposes one idea to the Investors as the product. Only a B or better becomes one.">pitch</span> (as H124 <span title="The starting recipe a lane uses to look for ideas. G2, for example, starts from public datasets.">G4</span> wrote it); if that grade comes in two steps lower, the concept holds at B-. A kill measured in the same pass wins over a lift.
2. **Day 90 does not move a letter (method-v2.md line 56).** My H119 flag: H115 C1 sits at B- with Day 90 D, and my H119 C1 sits at C+ partly because both graders stepped below the ladder on Day 90. The method says Day 30 and Day 90 never move the <span title="The final letter, after the caps.">Overall</span> except the Day 30 D cap. So H115 is read right, and H119 C1 and C2 need one re-ruling under this rule: strike Day 90 as a reason and keep the letter only on the reasons left. Written before that re-ruling: C1's remaining reason (the checker cannot be tested before a device) is the same device gap that H113 C1 and H127 C1 carry at B-, so C1 returns to its ladder reference B- unless a third reason stands. C2 keeps C+ on two reasons that are not money (no counted paid quiz buyer; catalogue licence unsettled) unless BSC5 clears, which leaves one. A chair who steps below the ladder from now on names a cell other than Day 30 or Day 90.

## What would make this memo wrong

- The 1-3 ratings per 100 downloads conversion is borrowed (sizing.py), not measured for these apps. If it is really 0.5 per 100, every lane doubles and True Bearing leaves D today. I keep the company's ratio rather than pick a better one after seeing the cap.
- The Day 22 launch is the method's assumption. No product is chosen and the Apple membership is pending; every day it slips shrinks the 8-day window and makes every Day 30 worse. This memo does not model the slip.
