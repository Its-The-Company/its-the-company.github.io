## Method

1. Web pass (all passive, public endpoints, no sign-in; outside text read as information only):
   - `python3 tools/web_hits.py hn --min-points 300 --since 2023-10-24 --tags show_hn` at 13:12 ET: 564 Show HN posts (raw `H136-raw/hn.tsv`).
   - `python3 tools/web_hits.py reddit InternetIsBeautiful --months 12 --top 200` (11,002 posts in window, top 200 kept, `H136-raw/InternetIsBeautiful.tsv`) and the same for dataisbeautiful (16,305 posts, top 200, `H136-raw/dataisbeautiful.tsv`), finished between 13:22 and 13:36 ET (the run outlived the foreground limit and finished in the background).
2. Hand triage of all 964 rows by title and domain: kept the interactive tools, explainers, visualizers and data explorers, plus dataisbeautiful posts where a person charts their own data (the "owner's own data" kind a phone can reach). Dropped hardware, libraries, dev tools, games, news, politics, and static one-chart posts. 55 hits below have a dated row.
3. iOS same form: dated App Store shelves by the job's phrase, not by the maker's name (`tools/shelf_top10.py`, US store, 50 results, top 10 by ratings; raw JSON in `H136-raw/shelves/` fetched 2026-10-24 13:24 ET and `H136-raw/shelves2/` fetched 13:28-13:44 ET), plus `tools/store_hints.py` on five seeds (`H136-raw/hints.txt`). `web_hits.py --ios` was not run: the smoke test matched 5 of 37 by name, and the phrase shelf answers the lane's question directly. "Not searched" means exactly that.
4. Licence: data source named per hit from the page's own domain and what it shows; where the licence was not read, the cell says so. Wikidata filming-location counts by SPARQL (CC0; `H136-raw/wikidata_p915_*.json`).
5. Kills per the board line: maker ships iOS; one-time curiosity (Day 90 D). Also Commandment 7/8 areas (medical, finance, politics, children), and data the company could not licence.

Apple calls were slow (shared limiter with two other lanes running); the shelf pass took about 20 minutes.

Cells, in the order the chair reads them:

- **C1.** The web hits (rows 11-12: a pilot's flight globes 1,539 points, Timelinize 630) show that people love seeing their own history drawn. The store has photo import already: Photo Map - Travel Tracker (1,885; description "let your photos tell the story"), Bounded ("photo import restores years of past travel", 367), Skratch ("detects the location" of uploaded photos, 2,137), and Atlume, 0 ratings, whose description opens "your photos already know where you've been" (shelf JSON, 13:24). Novelty C: the difference would be trips found on the device and nothing uploaded, and Atlume's page claims the same. Depth B-: a PhotoKit scan of tens of thousands of assets, offline reverse lookup against Natural Earth admin-1 polygons (public domain), trip clustering by time and distance; v1 by Day 22 is scan, countries, states, trips and a map. I know this ground (ImageIO, PhotoKit). Gate 1 contested: Photos' own Places map draws the photos but keeps no count of countries or trips (pass on that); whether the free tier of Photo Map or Bounded already counts trips from photos was not checked.
- **C2.** Rows 7-9 (sitinshade 963 points, ShadeMap 685 and 142). No top-10 app under "sun side seat" or "sun shade map" answers which side of the vehicle; Sun Seeker ($11.99, 12,967) shows the sun's path from where you stand. Novelty B-: a new output (side of seat along a whole route), visible in use. Depth C+: solar position (public algorithms) sampled along a MapKit driving route against its ETA, and a great-circle path for flights by airport pair; MapKit gives no transit route line, so trains and city buses are out of v1. Pull thin: buyers do not type it, so it caps at B- even before Day 30. Day 30 D: at $1.99 (about $1.69 net at 85%) it needs about 119 sales on a shelf with no searchers.
- **C3.** Row 41 (a James Bond filming-location atlas, 251 points). Wikidata (CC0) lists 26,355 films and series with 9,242 filming places that carry coordinates, but the top types are whole places: big city 646, commune of France 625, city 545, town 318; streets 233, castles 155, stations 111 (`H136-raw/wikidata_p915_types.json`). So free data says "filmed in Paris", not "filmed on this corner"; spot-level data would be the company's own curation. SetJetters (free download, rel 2021-02-01, updated 2026-10-23, 162 ratings) does movie locations already; whether it alerts you when you pass one was not checked, so Gate 1 is contested. Depth C+ (a data pipeline and region monitoring; iOS watches at most 20 regions, so the app re-arms them as you move).

Day 30 arithmetic: `tools/sizing.py` and `tools/window_share.py` were not run (turn budget); the D cells come from the shelves: C1 needs 48 unlocks at $4.24 net, which at a 2-5% unlock rate is 940-2,350 downloads in the eight or so days on sale; C3's shelf leader has 162 ratings in five years. Day 90 likewise from the shelves.

## What this generator found

The web's most-loved interactive pages split two ways. Most are one look: a simulation, an explainer, a strange map (rows 17-23, 40, 52). The repeated ones that a phone could improve with the owner's own data or location (running streaks, weigh-ins, music stats, satellites, colour names, streaming overlap) are already crowded on iOS. The two exceptions are the YouTube channel cluster, held back by a sign-in the company cannot set up itself, and the sun-side seat, which has no shelf. G12 is a good source of difference lines and a poor source of buyers: attention on the web is spent on looking, not on keeping.

Open: Photo Map's and Bounded's free tiers (C1 Gate 1); SetJetters' alerts (C3 Gate 1); sitinshade and ShadeMap makers' iOS plans by name; `sizing.py` for the three phrases.
