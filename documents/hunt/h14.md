Board line: ops/board.md "Idea hunt", H14. Stack Exchange API (academia, stats, tex, cs, math, datascience), last 12 months: asks for an app or tool that does a computed job away from the desk (check a derivation, reproduce a figure from a paper, read and query a dataset, review a paper with live equations, track citations while reading); hand-count by job; what they pay now; iPad and Mac entrants dated. Plotback (#21) is the comparison row.

Listen to this: "Using lualatex, texlive 2025, Linux (jailed on Android)... since Linux jailed in Android has some file directory peculiarities, I wish to know whether my results are generally applicable" (tex.SE, 2026-03-02, https://tex.stackexchange.com/q/760346). Here is the shape of it: in twelve months and 20,630 questions on six sites, the only person doing research work away from the desk did not ask for an app. They built a Linux jail on a phone and asked a TeX question. Nobody asked for an iPad tool. Not once.

**Result: no lane at B+. Every job counts 0 asks for a tool, mobile or desk. Best row D+. Stack Exchange is the wrong place to find this buyer, and it is also a quiet place now. No hand-off.**

## The counts behind the zeros
- Questions in the window (2025-10-23 to 2026-10-23), from the API's `filter=total`: academia 628, stats 1,697, tex 3,739, cs 375, math 13,976, datascience 215. Total 20,630.
- "ipad", "iphone", "tablet" in full text (`/search/advanced?q=...&fromdate=...`), each site: **0 iPad, 0 iPhone**; "tablet" hit 1 cs post (RAM sizes) and 2 math posts (geometry, dates), none about devices for research. As a check the same query from 2015 finds 13 academia posts and from 2020 26 tex posts ("Compile and Edit (La)TeX on iPad-pro 2020", https://tex.stackexchange.com/q/539357), so the filter works; the asking has stopped.
- Mobile at all: 2 tex posts from people running TeX in Linux on Android via Termux (2026-01-17 https://tex.stackexchange.com/q/758400, 2026-03-02 above). Workaround, not ask. Both people solved it themselves.
- Every tool ask on the six sites that I found by title ("app", "tool", "software", "program", "recommend"): 8 in total, none mobile, none for the five jobs: British English tool (academia 2026-06-05, https://academia.stackexchange.com/q/226889), word cloud tool (datascience 2025-11-05, https://datascience.stackexchange.com/q/136579), graph-drawing software x2 (math 2026-01-26 https://math.stackexchange.com/q/5121593, 2025-12-12 https://math.stackexchange.com/q/5113571), minimal polynomial (row 1), free stats software (row 3), KAN fitting software (datascience 2026-03-25, https://datascience.stackexchange.com/q/137865), and one question why systematic-review software (Covidence, Rayyan, DistillerSR) lags on LLM screening (stats 2026-09-06, https://stats.stackexchange.com/q/677087). That last one is a market note, not an ask: one person, desk software, institution-paid.

## What are they doing instead?
They do the computed job at the desk, and when they want help checking, they ask a chat model (2 posts above). Reading away from the desk is solved by general tools: "On iPad, I read research papers and books on Apple Books... on Mac, I use skim" (HN 2026-07-26, https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49059390). One HN wish fits row 1's neighbour: "I want to handwrite on an iPad and have AI transform that into LaTeX for me" (HN 2025-08-20, https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44961016): one person, outside the window by two months, and handwriting-to-LaTeX apps exist (not dated here).

## Why the lane is graded this low, and what it does not prove
- Stack Exchange is quiet: academia had 628 questions in a year and datascience 215. A zero here is a weaker zero than a zero on a busy forum. But the same sites in 2020 carried iPad LaTeX questions, and now carry none, so I read it as real: the iPad research crowd either found its tools or stopped asking strangers.
- What would change my mind: a dated count from r/PhD, r/AskAcademia or r/GradSchool (Arctic Shift dump, like H10) showing iPad-specific computed asks. Not done: the board line named Stack Exchange only.
- Plotback: SE adds nothing for it. Pass 9's C+ stands on two HN builders and one stale iOS app. I would not raise it, and I would not pitch it on this evidence.

## Method
- New `tools/se_asks.py` (dated full-text or title search via `/search/advanced` with `fromdate`) and `tools/se_dump.py` (dump every question since a date to JSONL with bodies). Read-only public API, no key; quota 300/day per IP, 165 left after H14.
- Dumped academia, datascience, cs, stats in full; tex stopped at 2,500 of 3,739 (page 26 returned 400, the anonymous page cap); math searched, not dumped (13,976). Regex per job over title + body, then hand-read: the regex list (research/hunt/H14-raw/se-job-regex-hits.txt) is mostly false positives ("mathematical" matched "mathematica", "commutative" matched "commut"); the counts in the table are my hand counts, not the regex.
- Shelf: `tools/appsearch.py` (US, iTunes Search API) on "latex editor", "plot digitizer", "research paper reader", "reference manager", "csv viewer", "proof assistant lean", "symbolic math cas", "pdf annotate equations", 13:12 ET. Prices of desk tools (Mathematica, Paperpile, Overleaf) not read today; I wrote none from memory.
