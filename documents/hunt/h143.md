## 2. What this does to the weaknesses

- **W3 (Spotify's file arrives late).** No Spotify page states a time. Not refuted, not confirmed. The pitch may say "Spotify sends it by email; the wait is not stated" and nothing stronger.
- **W4 (buyer works first).** Of six, one is a same-page download (Netflix), one is hours by email (Strava), one unknown wait (Spotify), three unread or silent on time (Letterboxd, StoryGraph, Goodreads). Every file needs a desktop or web step on at least four of six (Netflix "from a web browser", Strava strava.com, Goodreads desktop per third party, Letterboxd website per third party). The first session cannot assume a file in hand.
- **W10 (screen 1 unverified).** Corrected below; four of five file labels were not on any first-party page.

## 3. Apple's rules and what our listing may say

Read: [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) (first 100,000 of 114,428 characters; 5.2.5 truncated, not needed), [Product page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/), [Apple trademark guidelines for third parties](https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html), and [Spotify design guidelines](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design).


<div class="tbl" role="region" tabindex="0" aria-label="Table 2: 3. Apple&#x27;s rules and what our listing may say, scrolls sideways" style="--tw:783px" markdown="1">

|---|---|---|

Spotify: "The app name should not include 'Spotify'". | No service name. Askback, Backcatalog: fine on this rule. |
| Subtitle | 2.3.7: subtitles "should not ... reference other apps". | No service name. E.g. "Ask questions of your own history". |
| Keywords | Product page: do not use "Unauthorized use of trademarked terms" or "Competing app names". | No "spotify", "letterboxd", "netflix", "strava", "goodreads", "storygraph". |
| Description | 5.2.1: no third-party trademarks "without permission", no misleading names; 4.1(b) no impersonation. No line bans plain descriptive mention. Spotify allows "for Spotify" wording but no implied endorsement. | Plain text "reads the export file Spotify sends you" with "not affiliated" line. Judgment, not a quoted permission. |
| Screenshots | 2.3.3 app in use; 2.3.9 fictional account data; 2.3.14 no "references to other apps with an intent to mislead"; 5.2.1 as above. Spotify: no pairing its brand with other brands; logo must attribute Spotify content (stated for "any external developer integrating Spotify"). | Service names as plain text inside real UI (screen 1), no logos. Whether Spotify's brand rules reach a file reader (no API) is **open**. |
| Apple's own marks | Apple page: word mark only, "compatible with"/"for use with" phrasing, never in the product name. | "Uses Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones" in description only. |

</div>


Finding against weakness 3's remedy: "the listing must lead with Spotify" cannot happen in the name, subtitle or keywords, the three fields search reads. Only the description and screenshot text may name it. "spotify stats" as a ranked keyword is closed to us.
