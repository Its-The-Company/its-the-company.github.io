## 4. Weakness 1, nobody was counted typing a question (H141)

**Waits on:** H141 Q share (n = 60, seeded, bodies hand-read), the Song Infos review count, Maren's re-code (G4).

**Lift (Novelty, half of the B step), all of these:**
1. Q is at least 20% of the 60 (at least 12 posts) under G4. People type questions about their own history in words, not only ask for named charts.
2. Song Infos: of its reviews read, at least 5 name the assistant, and those that call it useful (any wording that says it answered what they asked) are at least as many as those that call it wrong, useless or missing. The question box is noticed and not resented. Fewer than 10 reviews readable in total: condition 2 is unmet (G2), and this weakness cannot lift.

**Kill (any one):**
- K1-1. Q is under 5% of the 60 (2 posts or fewer) under G4. Nobody types; the question box is a form, not a felt difference. Novelty drops one step to C+, the ladder gives B-, and the concept is **capped B-**.
- K1-2. Song Infos: at least 5 reviews name the assistant and those calling it wrong or useless outnumber those calling it useful by 2 to 1 or more. The one shipped question box is a complaint, and ours is unbuilt. Novelty holds B- but the concept is **capped B-** on the Gate 1 lesson (a shipped free version of the difference that buyers dislike is evidence the difference does not sell).
- K1-3. H141 finds, with a link, a free native iOS app that answers typed questions over two or more of the pitch's services in one library on the phone. Gate 1 fails (stated job, same form). **Cap C+.**

If neither is met, Novelty holds B- on this weakness and the chair's letter stands.

## 7. Weakness 6, Apple Intelligence hardware and the model path (H142)

**Waits on:** H142's cited Apple documentation, the 60-question table with typed queries, the template share.

**Clear (no lift; Fit check 3 and "buildable by Day 22" close), all of these:**

2. The typed grammar covers at least 80% of the 60 questions (at least 48).
3. The template path (no model) answers at least 50% of the 60 (at least 30), so a phone without Apple Intelligence gets a product on Day 22, not a chart list.
4. The documentation says whether the Simulator on this Mac runs Foundation Models. Either answer closes the check; "not found" does not (G2). If the Simulator cannot, the pitch says the model path is tested on a device after the membership is active and ships behind the template path.

**Kill (any one):**

The "shown, checkable query" difference does not hold for most questions; Novelty drops one step to C+, the ladder gives B-, **cap B-**.
- K6-2. The template path answers under 30% of the 60 (17 or fewer) and the Simulator cannot run the model. Then no testable v1 exists before the membership is active and nothing counts the model path before Day 22. Buildable-by-Day-22 fails for the product as pitched; **cap B-** until a device test is in the record.
- K6-3. Any claim in H142 about devices, OS or the Simulator without a link to Apple's own page: that claim is unmet (G2); it neither clears nor kills.

## 8. Weakness 10, the mockups use invented data; export files as they are today (H143)

**Waits on:** H143's per-service table (menu path, file name, format, fields, stated delivery time, each cell cited with a date read), Apple's rule on third-party names, the screen 1 corrections.

**Clear (no lift; Fit check 2 and Crowding's listing condition close), all of these:**
1. At least four of the six services (Spotify, Letterboxd, Netflix, StoryGraph, Goodreads, Strava) document a self-serve export whose file carries a title and a per-item date or timestamp, from the service's own help page, read and dated.

4. Screen 1 is redrawn to the cited file names and menu paths, and the README lists each correction.

**Kill (any one):**
- K10-1. Fewer than three of the six services document an export with a title and a per-item date, or fewer than two deliver at once. The free tier is empty on Day 22 and the first paid question waits on an email. Fit check 2 fails; **cap B-**.

- K10-3. Netflix's viewing-activity download is gone or no longer carries per-title dates (its help page read today). The TV module loses its largest instant file; this alone does not kill, but together with K5-1 the TV side is out of v1 and the pitch is re-cut as music-and-books; cap B- as in K5-1.

## 10. What would make these rules wrong

- The thresholds (20% / 5% Q, 15% / 2% X, 85% / 60% match, 75% / 50% runtime, 80% / 50% grammar, 50% / 30% templates, four / three of six exports, two instant) are my judgment, not calibrated against earners. I wrote them now so they cannot bend later. If a lane shows one was badly placed, I write a new rule below the append line, dated, and apply it only to lanes that land after it.
- The 60-post sample reads people who post about their stats, not people who buy stats apps. A low Q share could mean buyers ask the app, not the forum. That is a sizing gap I accept: the sample is the count we can make, and the kill at 5% is set low for that reason.
- Song Infos reviews are a one-app, Apple-Music-only proxy for the question box; condition 4.2 is written loosely (any wording) because 102 ratings will not yield many reviews that name a feature.
- Nothing here tests whether the join is worth $9.99 to the counted buyer. That is the price test the Investors would run after a choice, not a hunt rule.
