Result: 40 paid templates from Gumroad's public search, all dated 2026-10-24 14:10 ET with price and rating count, 18 with a sales count shown. 28 of 40 do a job that a native app in the <span title="The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.">top 10</span> of its store phrase already does in the same form (list or database the user fills), 7 do part of it (the all-in-one "life OS" with linked goals, projects, tasks and journal), 2 do a job no top-10 app does (a decorated home page; a gamified stats-and-levels life RPG), 3 were not checked on the store. The money in templates sits on jobs the store already answers free: at listed price the 18 rows with sales shown gross about $979k on "same form" jobs against $49k on the two "none" jobs. Four <span title="One product idea inside a lane. C1 is the lane&#x27;s first idea.">concepts</span> follow; none rests on an empty shelf. Etsy and the Notion gallery gave 0 rows (section 1).

## 1. Sources and what was not read

- **Gumroad** (`gumroad.com/products/search`, the JSON the public discover page calls; robots.txt disallows only `/purchases/`). 20 job phrases, most-reviewed first, 4 pages of 9 each: 720 rows, `H146-raw/gumroad_pull.py`. Selection, scripted in `H146-raw/select_date.py`: price above $0 (pay-what-you-want rows kept only with a minimum above $0, marked +), a template word in the name, and out: non-templates (VRChat avatars, Blender, courses, e-books, dating guides), banned subjects (finance, money, trading, budget, bookkeeping, mood, health, fitness, meal, sleep; Commandment 7), and resale bundles (KDP interiors, "MRR" bundles, an exam-answers PDF). Then the top 40 by rating count.
- **Dates.** Every row is dated by the fetch (2026-10-24). Gumroad pages do not show a creation date: the one date on every page (2023-10-16) is a site constant, not the product's. `updated_at` is shown on 9 of 40 and is in the table. A creation month would need the monthly product sitemaps (public, listed in robots.txt); not read this run.
- **Etsy: 0 rows.** robots.txt disallows `/search?*q=`, and the search page returned 403 with a captcha. A probe of `/market/notion_template` was refused by the permission check as bot-check circumvention; I stopped there (Commandment 6, no bot-wall bypass).
- **Notion template gallery: 0 rows.** Pages are public and allowed, and paid templates carry a price, but the page data shows no sales or review count (`number_of_duplicates` is -1, hidden), so "best-selling" cannot be ranked from it.
- **Store.** `tools/shelf_top10.py` on 10 phrases, US store, fetched 2026-10-24 14:12 ET; dated top 10s in `H146-raw/shelf_top10.txt`, full 50-result JSON in /tmp only.

Read of the table:
- The biggest single row is a student dashboard (4,509 ratings at $24.99). Courses, assignments and a grade view are what My Study Life, School Assistant and Power Planner do free. People pay for the look and for having everything on one page, not for a missing function.

"Life OS" apps under "life planner" in search order (HourLife, Life OS: Daily AI, LifeFlow, Mantle, Origami, Taily, Zenpath) all have 0 ratings: a wave of new apps with no buyers.
- Syllabus import is already on the "student planner" shelf (search order): B4Grad 1,933 ratings, Ahead 26, Lunori 1, DormWay 7, Feymark 0. Weighted grade calculators: Power Planner, iStudiez Pro, Dog Ate It ($9.99).

## 3. Concepts (author row, v2)

Concepts must pass Commandment 7: none touches finance, health or children; the student concept is pitched at college (age 17+ audience, no under-13 targeting).

- **C1 Template runner.** Difference line: import a Notion database export (CSV plus the Markdown page) or a Sheets CSV you already own, and it runs as a native offline tracker: a widget per view, quick-add from the Lock Screen, reminders from date properties, charts from rollups. 0 of the 10 top 10s import a template the user bought. <span title="How much room the idea has to grow. It moves the grade up or down a step or two.">Depth</span>: a type inference pass over Notion's CSV (select, multi-select, date ranges, relations by page title), an interpreter for a subset of Notion formula 2.0, and a view renderer. v1 slice by Day 22: CSV import, three property types, list and widget, no formulas.
- **C2 Life OS with the links.** Difference line: one model where a goal owns projects, a project owns tasks, and the weekly review rolls the week up to the goal, the form the $18 to $99 templates sell. Depth: the data model and the review screen; no algorithm.
- **C3 Syllabus to semester.** Difference line: scan or paste a syllabus; the app reads the grading weights and dates and keeps a live line "you need 74% on the final for a B". Depth: parsing messy grading tables (drop-lowest, <span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">capped</span> extra credit, weights that do not sum to 100) on device, then the projection.
- **C4 Stats-and-levels life RPG.** Difference line: habits and tasks raise named stats with a "status window" screen (the Solo Leveling form). Depth: tuning the XP curves; little else.

## 4. Open

- Creation months from Gumroad's monthly sitemaps; Etsy not readable; Notion gallery has no counts.
- Store phrases not run: "notion template", "solo leveling", "ramadan planner", "etsy seller".

## Lesson

A template sells the look and the single page, not a missing function: 28 of 40 do a job that a free top-10 app does in the same form. As a generator, G15 points to forms (the linked life OS) more than to jobs, and the form has its own clone wave.
