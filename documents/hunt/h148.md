## 1. Sources, what was refused, and what was not read

- Banned areas checked first (`python3 rules/lookup.py 7`). Excluded: The Old Farmer's Almanac for Kids (children under 13), anything on hunting (weapons), wine or beer logs (alcohol). Llewellyn's Moon Sign Book is astrology. That is not a banned area, so it is listed as a price row only. Knitting, crochet and music creation were not searched (Investors, 2026-10-22 18:51).
- **Amazon**: robots.txt has `User-agent: Claude-User / Disallow: /` (and ClaudeBot). I fetched one /dp/ page (it returned 404, a wrong ISBN guess) before I read that block. After that I made no more Amazon requests, so there are no best-seller ranks in this note. **Bookshop.org** lets Claude-User read only `/$`. **eBay** and **eldridgetide.com** disallow Anthropic agents. None of these was read.
- **Bot checks, stopped there (C11)**: riteintherain.com answered with an Azure WAF page and fieldnotesbrand.com with a Cloudflare "Just a moment" challenge. Neither was worked around.
- **Not reached**: Google Books API returned 429 on all 34 queries (keyless quota). store.farmersalmanac.com and shop.rasc.ca did not resolve. shop.skyandtelescope.org gave 404 and tidelog.com gave 403. Open Library has no 2026 Eldridge, RASC Observer's Handbook, NYT crossword calendar or sudoku calendar editions. Its low-content logbook ISBNs (979-8, print on demand) are not on Barnes & Noble (404).
- **What was read**: Barnes & Noble product pages `/w/?ean=` (robots allows /w/; crawl-delay 10 honoured). The review count comes from the page's JSON-LD aggregateRating. The page's salesRank field is empty, so B&N gives no rank. ISBNs came from Open Library search, newest edition first (`H148-raw/ol_bn.py`). Shopify publisher stores (store.almanac.com, leuchtturm1917.us): price and listing date from products.json, review count from the product page widget (`H148-raw/shop.py`).
- **Why 16, not 25**: of the 24 rows, 8 B&N rows show no review count on the page, and the Leuchtturm 2027 planners (0-6 reviews) are generic diaries, not paper for a hobby, so they are left out. The almanac store counts may pool reviews across editions, because a product handle such as `old-farmers-almanac` is reused each year. Read them as "people who bought some edition and said so", not as sales this year.
- Store top 10s: `tools/shelf_top10.py`, 12 phrases, US, fetched 2026-10-24 14:37 ET (`H148-raw/shelf-run.txt`). The tables below give store order, taken from the saved results. The raw JSON (5 MB) is left out of git.

**Difference lines.**
- C1: the paper is regional and printed once. This is the same yearly book computed for one backyard (sun and twilight tables, moon, nearest tide station, frost-date windows from 1991-2020 normals, meteor nights), and every "best day" shows its arithmetic.
- C2: the $12 kitchen calendar people review 156-206 times, drawn for their own frost dates, moonrise and shower peaks, and printed at home.
- C3: no difference from Navigation Almanac beyond polish. Recorded so nobody reruns it.
- C4: auto-filled entries (weather, moon, place) are the difference, and the 2026 entrants may already do this (descriptions not read).

**Depth (C1).** An ephemeris for sun, moon, planets and twilight (public algorithms), NOAA tide harmonic predictions from the nearest station, NOAA 1991-2020 frost-date normals by station, a meteor shower calendar (licence check per H129: GMN CC BY 4.0), and a yearly edition pipeline that typesets one book per place. It grows into a hobby layer per edition (garden, sky, coast, fishing), a print or PDF edition (C2 comes in here), and a comparison with last year's record.
