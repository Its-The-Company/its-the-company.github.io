## Depth beyond the framework call

- C1: the call gives text with timecodes; the app adds the shared timeline (audio sample time to photo capture time), board and slide reading into the same index, outline built in pieces under the 4,096 window (H142), search across a term, and a fallback for non-AI devices (transcript and photos, no outline). Test: a code-generated lecture (synthesised voice over a known script, photos of known slides) checks word error and photo placement to the second.
- C2: the call gives a transcript and a reply; the app adds the lesson loop, a @Generable correction type (error, fix, category), the learner's own error list with spaced repeats, and the guardrail and unsupportedLanguageOrLocale paths. The open risk is quality: a 2-bit 3B model correcting Spanish or Japanese has not been measured, and there is no pronunciation score (ELSA's paid job stays a server's).
- C3: the call gives a table of cells; the app adds multi-page merge, header detection, per-column type checks (dates, counts, names), a review screen that walks only the cells that failed, and CSV and Numbers export. Test: code-generated tables rendered to images with known cells, so every miss is counted.
- C4: the call gives words and times; the app adds line breaking to reading speed, style, burn-in through AVFoundation and SRT/VTT export. Thin past that.
- C5: the call gives one number per photo; the app adds a library sweep, thresholds, and a review grid. One call.

## Result

Five concepts. The new APIs do remove a paid server job in four of them, and the buyers pay monthly today ($9.99 a week at TLDL, $17.99 a month at Speak, $16.99 a month at Otter). The ceiling is the shelf, not the API: each comparator's shelf is crowded with free-with-subscription apps that already market "AI", and "on device, one price" is a difference a buyer must be told, not one the store shows. C3 sits on the only shelf here where paying up front already sells. Apple Intelligence devices are (figure withdrawn: source terms, see H159 corrections) of one public panel, so C2 is capped; C1 and C3 run their core without it.
