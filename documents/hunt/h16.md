## What H12-H15 tell us

1. In every lane of the Investors' subjects, builders posted as often as askers or more (genealogy 32 to about 50, astronomy about 55 to 14, radio 117 to 21, language 35 builder posts a month in one forum). The 2026 clone wave is not a feature of one niche. It is the shelf.
2. What leavers say nothing replaces is a network or an archive (komoot's community, ten years of Evernote). A new app cannot ship those on day one.
3. The one money anchor found is genealogy's desktop price ($29.95-34.99 one-time, RootsMagic and MacFamilyTree), and the pain under it is data damaged in transit (Ancestry exports losing custom tags). That is a pattern worth one more lane, not a product.
4. Stack Exchange is the wrong venue for tool demand in 2026 (H14, H15: 0 asks across nine sites). Reddit is 403; Arctic Shift works but its venues are where builders gather. The next lanes move to venues where users vote or pay, and where builders do not post launches.
