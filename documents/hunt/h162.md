Result: 345 chart apps read, 7,139 low reviews (1-2 stars, 2026, US, newest page), 331 matched a removal word, 70 of them by hand are a removed or moved job (34 are a paywall move, 227 other: "remove the app", ads, bugs). Release notes never name the removal: 0 of 100 dated versions across the four lead apps (25 each) say a job went away, and 6 of 345 current notes carry a removal word, none a job users miss. So the removal date in this <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Each lane has its own file.">lane</span> is the review date, bounded by the nearest release. The biggest removed jobs are small: 6 of 40 (Fire TV remote), 5 of 31 (YouTube Subscriptions tab), 4 of 34 (Ticketmaster date browse), 3 of 47 (Quik controls). Five <span title="One product idea inside a lane. C1 is the lane&#x27;s first idea.">concepts</span>; none has <span title="Can a buyer find it? Yes if the phrase is a store search suggestion or a top-10 app under it has over 100 ratings.">Pull</span> at full strength, and every one dies the day the maker restores the job.

## 1. Sources

- **Charts.** iTunes RSS, US, top 50 grossing and top 50 free in Productivity (6007), Utilities (6002), Photo and Video (6008), Health and Fitness (6013, kept only if name or description says walk, steps, pedometer or hike) and Entertainment (6016): 500 rows, 345 apps kept (`H162-raw/data/chart.tsv`, `apps.tsv`). Pulled 2026-10-24 ~15:30 ET by `H162-raw/pull.py`, all calls through `tools/store_http.py`.
- **Current release notes.** iTunes Lookup gives only the current version's notes. Regex hits 6 of 345 (`notes_hits.tsv`): 1Password ("We've removed ...", truncated), Zwift (Coffee Stop stops progression), four marketing lines. No job a reviewer asked back.
- **Version history.** The apps.apple.com page carries 25 dated versions in its Version History modal. Read for YouTube (2026-05-10 to 10-21), Amazon Fire TV (2025-03-11 to 2026-10-15), GoPro Quik (2025-11-10 to 2026-10-20), Ticketmaster (2025-08-20 to 2026-10-12) with `H162-raw/vh.py` (store_http, 4 calls). YouTube's 25 notes are jokes ("drank way too much coffee"); Ticketmaster's are one boilerplate line; Fire TV 4.9.0 (2026-10-07) and 4.9.1 (10-15) say "A redesigned remote with both directional pad and touchpad controls". No note in the 100 names a removal.
- **Low reviews.** Customer-review RSS, US, most recent, page 1, 2026 only, 1-2 stars: 7,139 across 335 apps with a page. Regex `removed|no longer|bring ... back|took away|taken away|got rid of` on title and text: 331 rows (`rev_hits.tsv`). Every row hand-read and coded in `H162-raw/code_rows.py` (codes in `data/coded.tsv`): R (a removed or moved job) 70, P (a free job moved behind a paywall) 34, O 227. The tool named in the brief, `shelf_reviews.py`, searches by phrase; this lane starts from charts, so pull.py reads the same RSS by id. Page 1 only, so n is the newest 50 or fewer per app, not the year.
- **Apple's iOS 26 removals.** support.apple.com "About iOS 26 Updates" (123075), read once 2026-10-24 ~16:55 ET: 317 text lines, 0 with remov / no longer / discontinu / deprecat / replac. The same command read Apple's site terms first, and they say: you may not use any "page-scrape", "robot", "spider" or other automatic device to access any portion of the Site. The fetch ran before I read that line. I have not used it beyond the zero count, and the copy in `H162-raw/apple/` is not committed. The same sentence may cover apps.apple.com pages (the version-history reads above, which go through the company's store door like every earlier lane); `vh/` is left uncommitted too until the chair rules (Commandment 6).
- **<span title="The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.">Shelves</span>.** `tools/shelf_top10.py`, US, 2026-10-24 16:51 ET: "youtube subscriptions", "fire tv remote", "gopro", "concerts near me" (`H162-raw/shelf/`).

## 3. Concepts (author row, v2)

<span title="A test written in advance. If it fires, we drop the idea.">Kill</span> for all five: the maker restores the job in a dated update. Fire TV and YouTube removals are days old (A/B-style redesigns), so a restore inside <span title="Our arithmetic estimate of what the idea would earn by day 30 (the $200 deadline) or day 90. It never raises a grade.">Day 30</span> is likely, not rare.

- **C1 Subs Only.** Difference line: your YouTube subscriptions as one newest-first list, Shorts and posts filtered out, nothing else. <span title="How much room the idea has to grow. It moves the grade up or down a step or two.">Depth</span>: OAuth subscriptions import, feed merge from RSS, Shorts detection (RSS lists Shorts as ordinary videos; duration or the /shorts/ URL needs videos.list at 1 quota unit per 50 ids), per-channel mute, watched state on device. v1 slice: import, merged list, Shorts filter, embedded player.
- **C2 Fire remote with the buttons.** Difference line: a Fire TV remote that has power, forward and every key back, reachable by Voice Control labels. Depth: network ADB pairing and key events, discovery on the LAN. Fair price up front against weekly subscriptions on the chart.
- **C3 Ticket calendar.** Difference line: every show near you on a date range, one list across Ticketmaster and SeatGeek. Depth: two APIs, venue dedupe, radius search.
- **C4 GoPro scrubber.** Difference line: preview and scrub clips on the camera over Wi-Fi, cut to a song length, without Quik. Depth: Open GoPro BLE wake and Wi-Fi join, HTTP media list and preview stream, cut by length.
- **C5 Internal RAW camera.** Difference line: ProRes RAW to the phone's own storage. Depth: AVFoundation RAW capture, thermal and storage budget. Exists only if the platform allows it.

## 4. Open

- Chair: does Apple's site-terms robot clause cover apps.apple.com pages read through store_http? `vh/` and `apple/` uncommitted until then. The zero count from 123075 stands only if that read is allowed.
- YouTube API Services terms on a subscriptions-only client (C1 Fit); Ticketmaster Discovery API terms (C3); Open GoPro camera list (C4); AVFoundation ProRes RAW internal (C5).

- Restore watch: re-read Fire TV and YouTube versions on 2026-10-31.

## Lesson

Big apps do not write their removals into release notes (0 of 100 here). The review is the only dated record, and the count is days old.
