## Method

- Charts: US legacy iTunes RSS, top paid and top grossing, genres Productivity 6007, Utilities 6002, Education 6017, Reference 6006, Photo and Video 6008, read 2026-10-24 16:51-16:53 ET through store_http.py. I asked for limit=200; the feeds answer at most 100 (97-100 rows each), so this is the top 100 of each chart, 991 chart rows, 990 ids.
- Lookup (US) of every id; kept first release 2026-05-01 or later. Ratings per day = userRatingCount / days since releaseDate, read 2026-10-24. Kept paid-up-front apps, and free apps only when they sit on a grossing chart (so they take money in app).
- Low reviews: tools/shelf_reviews.py --ids, 1-3 stars since 2026-05-01, storefronts us, gb, ie, ca (rev/ID.tsv). Hand-coded below; every count is from those files.
- sizing.py run on 8 entrants (sizing.md), default 1%-3% rating share. For paid apps the share is likely higher than 3%, so the implied sales are a ceiling.
