## 1. The phrase, named before counting (15:28 ET by `date`; I first wrote 15:27 before running it, fixed)

**Phrase: "listening history".**

Why, from the store's own search box (tools/store_hints.py, US, 2026-10-24 15:24-15:26 ET; raw hint lists in H166-raw/hints-*.xml):

- "listening history": the box offers 0 completions. No completion is no evidence against it; it names the job in the user's words (the play history the user exports), carries no service name, and fits a subtitle under 2.3.7 (H143.md row "Subtitle": no service name) and the keywords field (H143.md row "Keywords": no competing app names).
- "music history": 4 of 4 completions are music-history education ("music history and composers", "music history flashcards", "music history trainer"); the top 10 behind "music history flashcards" is flashcards and note readers (Music History Flashcards, Notes! - Learn To Read Music). Off-job by the 14:36 phrase clarification.
- "your stats": 3 of 3 completions are sports or games ("ace your stats", "ipadel - track your stats", "predecessor: know your stats"); every top 10 behind them is sports stats (HOF, Baseballplayer Stats, iScore). Off-job.

Pre-registered fallback, written before any count: if the "listening history" top 25 is off-job (fewer than 13 of 25 rows are apps about a person's own music or media history), the next on-job service-free phrase counts, and that is "music stats" (the phrase H137 C1's own Crowding cell named), then "listening stats". Nothing else is chosen after seeing a count.

The count follows in section 2.
