## Result: both rows unreachable by a permitted route

Both services' own terms forbid reading them with a script. I checked the terms after the first reads, not before. That was my mistake; the list below says what was fetched before I stopped.

### Letterboxd

- [Terms of Use](https://letterboxd.com/legal/terms-of-use/), "Last updated: October 2026". The Service is "any website, application, API, or content offered, owned or operated by Letterboxd Limited". Clause: "you must not employ any robot, spider, scraper, deep-link, or other automated data gathering or extraction tool, program, or algorithm to access, acquire, copy, or monitor any portion of the Service."
- [robots.txt](https://letterboxd.com/robots.txt) disallows ClaudeBot and 22 other named AI agents from the whole site. The `*` group allows /about/ and /settings/.
- [letterboxd.com/about/](https://letterboxd.com/about/): 403, Cloudflare "Just a moment" challenge. Stopped, not retried.
- [letterboxd.com/settings/data/](https://letterboxd.com/settings/data/): 200, page title "Sign in". No public text.
- [FAQ](https://letterboxd.com/about/faq/): 200, the word "export" does not appear.
- [support sitemap](https://support.letterboxd.com/hc/sitemap.xml) lists an article titled, by its URL slug, "Can I get a copy of my account data" (id 15179196880911). Not fetched. Its existence is a fact; its contents are not.
- help.letterboxd.com does not resolve.

### Goodreads

- [Terms of Use](https://www.goodreads.com/about/terms), "revised on April 28, 2021". The license "does not include ... any use of data mining, robots, or similar data gathering and extraction tools."
- [help.goodreads.com robots.txt](https://help.goodreads.com/robots.txt) allows all, but robots.txt does not override the terms.
- [help index](https://help.goodreads.com/s/) (200) and its [sitemap](https://help.goodreads.com/s/sitemap.xml) (200) were fetched before the terms check. I did not follow the sub-sitemaps or fetch the export article.
