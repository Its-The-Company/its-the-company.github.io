Written and committed before any new read for the pitch. Subject: H148 C1 "Your place's almanac", chair ruling B (research/hunt/rulings-v2.md, 'H148 chair (Ines, 15:27): C1', b59f1d3c). The chair ruling named four reads before the pitch; this file says in advance what each read can do to the letter, so the letter is not bent after the evidence lands. The ladder is method v2 (Novelty B- with Depth B lifts one step to B; a Gate 1 fail caps at C+; a Day 30 D caps at B-). Reads are listed in the order they will be run. Nothing here can raise the Overall above B; the board line says Gate 1 and Novelty can only fall.

## Read 1: The Farm Almanac store description (Gate 1, Novelty)

The Farm Almanac is the top on-job row under "farmers almanac" (1 rating, released 2026-05-07). Its description is read through tools/store_http.py (Lookup API, US store).

- **K1-1 (Gate 1 fail, cap C+).** If the description says the app computes, for a location the user sets, three or more of these five tables for the year ahead: sun and twilight times, moon phase and rise/set, tide predictions, frost or growing-season dates, meteor showers, then an app already makes a per-place yearly almanac in the same form. Gate 1 fails, Overall capped at C+, and the pitch is posted with that letter in its title and card.
- **K1-2 (Novelty to C+, Overall B-).** If the description claims the central difference of C1 in words, a yearly almanac or calendar for "your location" or "your area" or "your zip code" with two of the five tables, the stated difference is no longer unclaimed on the shelf. Novelty falls to C+; the ladder with Depth B gives B-.
- **No move.** Articles, folk weather lore, a long-range forecast, a general national almanac, or one table alone (each table is already a free app; that is priced in the B- Novelty).

## Read 2: Calendarium store description (Gate 1, Novelty, Day 30 lane)

Calendarium (id 482136387, saved as "Calendarium - About this Day" in research/hunt/H148-raw/shelf/) was counted by the chair in the Day 30 lane as a per-place sky app at 13.8-41.5 downloads a day.

- **K2-1 and K2-2.** The same tests as K1-1 and K1-2, same moves.
- **K2-3 (Day 30 lane shrinks).** If the description shows Calendarium does not compute sun or moon for a place the user sets (an "on this day" history app, say), its row leaves the Day 30 lane. The lane becomes 68.0-204.0 a day (Fishing Times Calendar 22.7-68.2, Deluxe Moon Pro 44.9-134.8, Eden's Almanac 0.3-1.0), 544-1,632 downloads in the 8-day window (Day 22 to Day 30); 36 sales at 30% is 2.2-6.6% of it. On the scale the chair ruling applied (C at 1.8-5.5%, D when the share needed is one in four at the midpoint or the whole window at the worst end), that stays C, so the Day 30 cap does not bind. If it also shows that Fishing Times Calendar or Deluxe Moon Pro is not per-place, those rows leave too, and the share is recomputed by the same arithmetic; D needs the window under 144 downloads, which only an empty lane gives.
- **No move** if Calendarium shows sun and moon for a set place and a daily page: that is the base of the job, already counted.

## Read 3: NOAA tide harmonic constituents, licence and offline use

Sites: tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov (CO-OPS). Terms and disclaimer pages are read before any data or API page (G2a). The product needs the harmonic constituents (amplitude and phase per constituent per station) so tides can be computed on the phone for the year without a network call.

- **K3-1 (Depth to B-, Overall B-).** If the constituents are not published for download (only pre-computed predictions through a live API), or the terms forbid storing or redistributing them in an app, then version 1 cannot compute tides offline. The tide table leaves version 1, Depth falls from B to B- (the harmonic engine was one of the four depth items), the ladder gives no lift, and the Overall is B-.
- **Condition, not a kill.** A required attribution line, a "not for navigation" disclaimer, or a rate limit on the one fetch per station: the pitch carries the condition in section 6 and the mockup carries the disclaimer.
- **Scope note, not a kill.** NOAA stations cover the United States and its territories. Tides for other coasts (UKHO, CHS, BSH) are sold under Crown or Crown-like copyright; the pitch says tide tables are US-only in version 1. The same is true of the frost normals (NCEI 1991-2020). This narrows the buyer and is listed under weaknesses; it was already in the chair's Day 30 lane (US store rows).

## Read 4: "almanac" as a name word under App Store Guideline 2.3.7

Guideline 2.3.7 (public text at developer.apple.com) bars other apps' names and trademarks from the name, subtitle and keywords.

- **K4-1 (names only, no grade move).** "Farmers' Almanac" and "The Old Farmer's Almanac" are publishers' marks (Idris, in the Pull cell); no candidate name carries "farmer", "farmers" or "old farmer's". "The Almanac" is the exact name of an existing app (the Voodoo puzzle game, 7,147 ratings); no candidate name is exactly "The Almanac" or "Almanac" alone.
- **Rule for the table.** "Almanac" is a dictionary word for a yearly book of tables; the guideline's text is read for any clause that bars generic words, and if none, the word may stand inside a longer name. A USPTO search for a live mark on the word alone cannot be run: issue #46 is open on the blocked USPTO search, and the table says so. Names are proposals under Commandment 51 either way.
- **Table rule.** Each candidate name is searched in a paired appsearch run (two phrases in the same command, so an empty answer counts only if its neighbour answered in the same minute, memory lesson); a candidate whose exact name is an existing app's name is dropped. Domains are checked by DNS lookup (whois for .app does not answer from this machine). Names never move the letter.

## Rule that binds them all

- Each read moves at most the cells named above; no read lifts any cell. The letter after the reads is the ladder on the cells as they stand after all four, and it goes in the pitch title, the pitch card and the issue title, with the firing rule named. If two rules fire, both moves apply before the ladder (the lesson from H150: caps are ceilings, moves are moves).
- Applied section below is written after the reads, with each rule's outcome on its own line; a read that could not be completed (block, bot check, terms forbidding scripts) is a G2 gap, written as "not read", and the rule it serves stays open rather than passed.

## Applied (Ines, 2026-10-24 16:59 ET)

Reads run 16:51-16:55 ET; raw in research/hunt/H169-raw/ (store answers through tools/store_http.py; NOAA and Apple pages by curl after their robots files: tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov has none (404), api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov answers 403 to /robots.txt, developer.apple.com allows /app-store/).

- **Read 1, The Farm Almanac (id 6762157524, EZYCURE LLC, 1 rating, released 2026-05-07).** Its description is a shop loyalty app ("THE ALMANAC, THE FARM DE's mobile app": points, rewards, mobile ordering, push notifications). It computes nothing for a place. K1-1 not met, K1-2 not met: no move. The top on-job-named row under "farmers almanac" is not an almanac at all, so Gate 1 stands as checked and the shelf under the phrase is thinner than the ruling assumed.
- **Read 2, Calendarium (id 482136387, Piotr Knapczyk, 2,258 ratings, last updated 2020-04-04).** "On this day in history" plus sunrise and sunset, day length, moon phase, planets view, geographic coordinates, no Internet required; it uses the location for the sun times. That is sun and moon for the user's place on a daily page: the "no move" clause's words exactly, and the thing the chair ruling already priced into Novelty B- ("each table is a free app alone (Moon Phases, Calendarium ...)"). K2-1 not met (two tables, not three, and no year-ahead edition). K2-2 is the close one: it is a calendar, it uses the location, it has two of the five tables; what it does not claim is the year-ahead edition for the place (the page is "about this day"), and the rule's words ask for the claim of a yearly almanac or calendar for "your location". Not met, by that clause; said here so the closeness is on record. K2-3 not met: the Day 30 lane row stands (it is per-place), with the note that the per-place base on the shelf has not been updated in six years.
- **Read 3, NOAA tide harmonic constituents.** The CO-OPS Metadata API publishes them for download: `mdapi/prod/webapi/stations/8443970/harcon.json` answered 37 constituents for Boston (M2 amplitude 4.498 ft, phase 109.2, speed 28.984104 deg/h; S2, N2, K1 ...), and `stations.json?type=harcon` lists 1,373 US stations with constituents (AK 289, FL 225, CA 77, TX 73, NY 67, WA 61, LA 54, MA 49, PR 48, NC 43). So the year's tides can be computed on the phone from one fetch per station. Licence: the CO-OPS disclaimers page covers raw data and forecast systems, the web-services page lists the MDAPI products, and the data-API documentation carries no terms clause; none of the three CO-OPS pages read has a licence line at all. The nearest NOAA statement is the NWS disclaimer (weather.gov/disclaimer): NWS web information "is in the public domain, unless specifically noted otherwise, and may be used without charge for any lawful purpose" with three conditions (no claim of ownership, no implied endorsement or affiliation, no modified content presented as official). The statutory basis for both is 17 U.S.C. 105 (works of the US government). K3-1 not met. The three conditions are carried into the pitch as section 6 items and the "not for navigation, computed by this app" line on the mockups. Scope note stands: US stations only; UKHO and other Crown-copyright tide data are out of version 1.
- **Read 4, Guideline 2.3.7.** Text read 16:54: "Choose a unique app name, assign keywords that accurately describe your app, and don't try to pack any of your metadata with trademarked terms, popular app names, pricing information, or other irrelevant phrases just to game the system. App names must be limited to 30 characters." No clause bars a generic word, so "almanac" may stand inside a longer name; "The Almanac" (Voodoo, 7,147 ratings) and "Almanac: Events Countdown", "Almanac - Learn About Anything", "Almanac: Fact of the Day", "Paws Almanac", "Wx Almanac" are existing exact names and are avoided; no candidate carries "farmer" or "farmers". All eight phrases in the paired appsearch run answered 16:51-16:54, so the empties count. USPTO not run (#46 open). K4-1: names only, no grade move; the table is in the pitch.

**Letter after the reads: B holds.** No rule fired. Cells unchanged from the chair ruling: Need B-, Novelty B-, Depth B, Day 30 C, Day 90 B, Overall B. No new ruling row is needed in rulings-v2.md because no cell moved; this file is the record that the four reads were made under rules written first.
