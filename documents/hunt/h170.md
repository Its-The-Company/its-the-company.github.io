## 0. Rules written before the first search (17:10 ET)

- 40 phrases, 20 pairs, in research/hunt/H170-raw/phrases.txt; Investor subjects first (language, notetaking, organisation, walking, YouTube, data, movies and TV data), then the other named genres. No phrase is added or swapped after the searches.
- Top 10 = appsearch.py order, first 10 rows. An empty result counts as unchecked unless its pair-mate answered in the same call.
- Traffic per row, "now": the Search API's userRatingCountForCurrentVersion over days since currentVersionReleaseDate, used only where that count is below the lifetime count (the version reset its count), labelled "ver". Otherwise the lifetime average (ratings over days since release), labelled "life". One dated read only: a second read 24 h apart does not fit this session, so no row carries a measured 24 h delta.
- Phrase traffic = sum over the <span title="The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.">top 10</span> of ratings per day. <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Each lane has its own file.">Lane</span> downloads per day = that sum x 33 to 100 (sizing.py's 0.03 and 0.01 rating rates).
- Weakest top-5 row: the top-5 row with the lowest ratings per day among those that are old (current version before 2025-10-24), under 4.4 stars, or off-job; none of these, "none".
- Price: median price of the top 10. Free median: free plus unlock, conversion 2%-5% (window_share.py defaults); paid median: --paid.
- Keep test (the G26 <span title="A test written in advance. If it fires, we drop the idea.">kill</span>, read here so it cannot bend): run window_share.py --launch 22 --days 30 on the top-10 lane. Midpoint share = the mean of its best and worst shares. <span title="Our arithmetic estimate of what the idea would earn by day 30 (the $200 deadline) or day 90. It never raises a grade.">Day 30</span> band on the H135/H169 scale: C or better when the midpoint share is 15% or under. "For a top-5 share": the weakest top-5 row's own share of the phrase's ratings per day must also be at least the midpoint share (a listing that takes that slot gets that share). Both <span title="What keeps an idea below the top: the cap that applies to it now.">hold</span>: kept. Either fails: killed by G26, no <span title="One product idea inside a lane. C1 is the lane&#x27;s first idea.">concept</span> sought.
- Kept phrases only: the top 10's own low reviews by RSS (review_mine.py RSS mode) for the difference with depth.

## 1. Result (17:15 ET)

40 phrases read, 40 answered (20 paired calls, 17:10-17:12 ET; raw H170-raw/pairs/). 400 top-10 rows; 400 of 400 carry the lifetime average ("life"): no row's current-version count was below its lifetime count, so no row has a since-version rate and none a 24 h delta. Lifetime averages flatter old leaders (QR Reader for iPhone, 2010, 1,442,242 ratings, reads 239.6 a day).

- Midpoint share over 15% (Day 30 below C): 4 phrases, all small lanes (video summarizer 3.0 ratings a day, japanese dictionary 12.0, kanji 13.5, packing list 16.8).
- No weak top-5 row (none old, under 4.4 or off-job; hand-read, no off-job row found): 22 phrases. Every lane over 700 ratings a day is in this group or has its only weak row near 0 a day.
- Kept by both tests: 3: planner (212.12/day, midpoint 2.9%, weak top-5 Digital Planner – Task Journal (4.3, upd 2026-09-30, 9.10/d) = 4.3%); qr code reader (574.9/day, midpoint 1.1%, weak top-5 QR Code & Barcode Scanner ・ (4.6, upd 2023-02-13, 25.48/d) = 4.4%); trivia (481.49/day, midpoint 1.2%, weak top-5 Jeopardy! Here is the shape of it: where the traffic is, the top 5 is fresh and rated 4.6 or better; where a top-5 row is old or weak, the lane is too small to share in 8 days. Three phrases sit between.
