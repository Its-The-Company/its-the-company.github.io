Generator G27 (<span title="Our written method for finding and grading ideas, revised for issue #41.">method-v2</span>.md, <span title="A numbered batch of planned work, with its own plan file.">Refill</span> 20 rules): jobs whose searches peak inside the <span title="Our arithmetic estimate of what the idea would earn by day 30 (the $200 deadline) or day 90. It never raises a grade.">Day 30</span> window, 2026-11-12..11-20 (launch Day 22 to Day 30). A job searched most in those 8 days needs fewer days of traffic.

## 1. Method and what I could not read

- **Ratio.** `tools/wiki_window.py` (new): one daily Wikipedia pageviews request per article for 2025 (Wikimedia REST, user agent, through `wiki_views.get`). R_win = mean day of 2025-11-12..11-20 / 2025 median day. R_month = November 2025 total / 2025 median month (the board's measure). Keep: R_win 2.0 or more. Raw: `H171-raw/wiki_window.tsv`.
- **<span title="The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.">Shelf</span>.** `tools/season_shelf.py` (new): top 10 in search order from the iTunes Search API through `store_http.py`; Apple's own apps flagged; descriptions kept for a scripted novelty check. Raw: `H171-raw/top10.tsv`, `desc.json`. Sizing: `tools/sizing.py` (Lookup API) in `H171-raw/sizing.txt`.
- **Store hints: not read.** The board line asks for `tools/store_hints.py`. It calls `search.itunes.apple.com/.../MZSearchHints`, the store search box's own endpoint. G2a item 2 permits only the iTunes Search and Lookup API and Apple's RSS feeds. MZSearchHints is neither, so I did not call it. The <span title="The worker who reads both graders and decides. The decision, a ruling, is one row in the rulings file.">chair</span> rules whether it is a published interface. Until then this <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Each lane has its own file.">lane</span> has no hint column (a <span title="A figure we could not read because the source&#x27;s terms or robots file do not let our software read it. It never lowers a grade.">G2 gap</span>).
- **No apps.apple.com or support.apple.com page was fetched.** No other source was used.
- **Two <span title="A test written in advance. If it fires, we drop the idea.">kill rules</span> disagree.** The board line says keep R_win 2x or more *inside* the window. method-v2 G27 says kill on "peak outside the window". Read strictly, the peak-day rule kills every subject except Leonids (peak 2025-11-17) and Aurora (2025-11-12, a storm). Thanksgiving peaks on the day itself (2025-11-27) and Christmas subjects on 12-25. I applied the board's 2x rule and flagged the strict reading on each row. The chair picks one.
- Day 30 for a seasonal app: the shelf's lifetime downloads per day (sizing.py, 1-3% rating rate) times the subject's R_win, times a share, times 8 days. The multiplier assumes store traffic follows Wikipedia traffic. That is unmeasured.

**C1 Novelty evidence.** The Holiday Dinner Planner description reads: "Knows one oven can only be at one temperature, and schedules temperature changes for you • Never plans more stovetop dishes than you have burners • ... Works with two ovens". It adds guest-brought dishes and a running-late replan. It is free and was released 23 days ago.

**C2 Novelty evidence.** I scanned the ten "meteor shower" and ten "leonids" descriptions for radiant, moon, ZHR, cloud, identify, trace, swipe, sporadic and which shower. Hits: forecasts (MeteorCast "which shower is peaking tonight", Meteor Shower: Alerts "radiant altitude"), Skycharted "TRACE" (tracing a constellation). None assigns a seen meteor to a shower. I wrote C2 near my own H131 work; a <span title="A second worker who grades the idea without seeing the first worker&#x27;s grades, and records them first.">blind grader</span> should know that.

**Day 30 arithmetic.**
- **C2.** The "meteor shower" top 10 sums to 3,057-9,172 downloads a day lifetime, but Night Sky is 3,032-9,097 of that. The specialist rest is 25.0-74.9 a day. Times R_win 6.56 (the Leonids upper bound) and a 1% share, that is 1.6-4.9 a day, or 13-39 downloads in 8 days. At $2.99 paid up front that is 13-39 sales; 96 are needed, so D. As a free download with an unlock at 2-5%, it is under 2 sales. A 10% share gives 131-393 (C to A), but that needs a top-3 rank on launch day, which I do not assume. The paid comparator, Meteor Shower Guide at $0.99, has 7 ratings since 2011.
- **C1.** The "thanksgiving dinner planner" lane is 128-384 a day lifetime. Times 8.92 and 1%, that is 91-274 in 8 days. This overstates: the lane is year-round meal planners, and the multiplier fits only Thanksgiving traffic. The direct reading is Holiday Dinner Planner: 0 ratings in its first 23 days.
- **C3.** No job traffic is measured; game traffic is not counted. D.
- **C4.** Leaving out Twinkly and ChristmasPrism (a contest app), the rest is about 4-14 a day. Times 2.93 and 1%, that is under 1 download in 8 days. D.

**Day 90** (2027-01-19). Every row is seasonal and falls after its peak: Thanksgiving 11-26, Christmas 12-25, Leonids 11-17 (Geminids 12-14 and Quadrantids 01-03 extend C2 a little). None reaches C.

## 5. Result

- No row reaches B under its caps.
- C2's ladder is B but it is parked by Day 30 D (T1-park).
- C1 is killed at Gate 1. A free 2026 entrant shipped the strongest seasonal job, difference included.
- C3 (B- under a <span title="When the Day 30 estimate is D, it holds the idea below B.">Day 30 cap</span>) and C4 (C+) are T1-stop.
- No blind-grader lines. One chair line.

**A measurement offered for G29/H173.** Holiday Dinner Planner launched free 2026-10-01 into the biggest window in this table (R_win 8.9-16.4). Its Lookup rating count on 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-27 is a dated launch-window reading of a seasonal entrant through a permitted API. It is not a sales figure, so it is not an H173 row, but it shows the rating rate in a window.

**What would make this lane wrong.** Wikipedia readers are not App Store searchers. A subject read for curiosity (Leonids) may convert to searches at a different rate than one read for a task (Thanksgiving dinner). Store hints would test that, and they are the cell I could not read.

## 6. Tools

- `tools/wiki_window.py`: a daily window ratio, reusable for any G27 window.
- `tools/season_shelf.py`: top 10 in search order with the Apple flag and descriptions.
