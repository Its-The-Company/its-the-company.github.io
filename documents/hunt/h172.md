## 0. Rules written before the first fetch

- Charts: toppaidapplications limit=200 for genres 6000, 6007, 6002, 6017, 6006, 6008, 6027, 6026, 6010, 6004. Apple returned 97-100 rows per feed (the limit is a ceiling); feed update times are in data/fetch_log.txt (09:47-14:15 PT, 2026-10-24).
- Every row at $9.99+ is tabled with rank, ratings, average, release date and last version date from Lookup, and coded by hand for job, buyers' trade and Commandment 7 fit (section 1).
- Reviews: RSS page 1, most recent, up to 50 per app; 1-3 stars kept. Apps under 5 ratings were not fetched (said in the row).
- Concepts: the adjacent job a $9.99+ row does not do (from its own 1-3 star reviews), at a price a named paid app on the concept's phrase already charges. Phrases searched in pairs; top 10 = appsearch.py order.
- Day 30 per concept: window_share.py --launch 22 --days 30 --paid at the comparator's price, lane = top 10 of the main phrase sized at sizing.py's 0.01-0.03 rating rates. An app on the top 10 that does a different job is named and taken out, and both results are shown. Leaving D: midpoint share under 25% and worst end under 100% (H164 rule).
- Skipped for concepts (Commandment 7): finance, medical, legal, weapons, children under 13, and pseudo-science claims (Commandment 33 honesty).

## 1. What the charts hold (219 rows at $9.99+)

219 of 986 chart rows cost $9.99 or more (Business 26, Productivity 23, Utilities 23, Education 20, Reference 26, Photo and Video 32, Graphics and Design 25, Developer Tools 9, Navigation 18, Sports 17). 185 rows had 5+ ratings and were read by RSS; 162 had at least one 1-3 star review on page 1 (2,456 low reviews in all, files in data/rev/). 23 rows carry a Commandment 7 flag (finance, medical, legal, weapons, children, or adjacent) and were not used for concepts; 9 more are tied to hardware we cannot test (OBD, drones, radar detectors).

Three facts from the table:
1. High price sells where a trade must have the answer offline: pilots (Sporty's E6B 5,375 ratings, FAR/AIM, Prepware), electricians and linemen (Ugly's $12.99 1,510; Lineman's Reference $19.99 409), HVAC, machinists, pipefitters, film and camera crews. Most of these leaders are old (released 2009-2016) and several are stale (Piping DataBase XTREME last version 2015-04-23, Kuber 2021-04-08, GoSatWatch 2021-03-16, Video Delay Instant Replay Pro 2023-05-17, Scrivener 2023-09-29).
2. New entrants chart at $9.99-$29.99 with few ratings: 2026 releases on the $9.99+ rows include Very Good Camera (16 ratings, Photo rank 12), GitSync.md (16, Developer Tools 21), Dine Bizaad ($24.99, 16, Education 64), Lightframer Pro ($19.99, 6), Salt Shack Minerals ($19.99, 6). The paid chart is reachable at a high price with a small count.
3. But every adjacent phrase I searched has a recent free entrant: 16 of 16 phrases have at least one app released since 2025-10-24 in their top 10 (data/entrants.tsv; 1 to 6 per phrase). The high price fixes Day 30 on four of five lanes below; it does not fix Novelty.
