A price taken from a store page before G2a (H159-raw rows1.md, rows2.md, app_rows.py output) is "read against terms" and feeds no rule.

Our own planned price, where different, is pasted and does not decide. No paid comparator on the job at a price: the Day 30 cap stands (G28's kill, applied here).
- G13 (H173). These rules are written before H173 and hold whichever way it lands. If H173 rules "sizing undercounts by 2x or more", the R-readings below are run with its measured median rate in place of sizing.py's rate (the chair states the lane conversion in the chair line), and the thresholds do not move. If H173 rules "sizing confirmed" or gives no verdict, the readings run on sizing.py defaults. If it rules "overcounts", Day 30 can only fall.
- G14 (graders). A row whose caps all clear and whose ladder then reads B gets two blind graders before the chair rules it (T1-park; the board line's G5). One blind grade already on file counts as one of the two. A grade two steps under the author's ladder holds the row at B- whatever the other says (H164 G5).
- G15 (Novelty guard). Where a reading lists an app that already sells the row's difference line in its description, Novelty drops one step and the ladder is recomputed. Readings lower Novelty; they never raise it.
