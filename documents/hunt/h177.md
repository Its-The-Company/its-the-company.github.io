## Method (H163's, repeated)

- Charts: US legacy iTunes RSS, top paid and top grossing, through store_http.py, read 2026-10-24 17:50-17:53 ET: Lifestyle 6012, Navigation 6010, Weather 6001, Travel 6003, Books 6018, Sports 6004, Food and Drink 6023, Graphics and Design 6027, Developer Tools 6026, Health and Fitness 6013, Entertainment 6016, Music 6011. Asked limit=200; feeds answer 95-100 rows. 2,384 chart rows, 2,369 ids. Source form: `https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/toppaidapplications/limit=200/genre=6012/json`.
- Lookup (US) of every id (`https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?country=us&id=...`); kept first release 2026-05-01 or later. Ratings per day = userRatingCount / days since releaseDate, as of 2026-10-24. Paid up front, or free on a grossing chart.
- Low reviews: tools/shelf_reviews.py --ids (review_dump.py's customer-review RSS route), 1-3 stars since 2026-05-01, storefronts us, gb, ca, au, 19 entrants (rev/ID.tsv, no header: country, date, stars, version, title, body; counts in rev_counts.txt). TapCook, Wheelz, SlowRead, proRadar and Michigan Storm Chasers hand-coded in full; the other meal planners by keyword with the matching rows read by hand (marked).
- Shelf reads: phrases written in prereg.txt at 18:04 before any search; Search API limit 50, cut to top 25 (shelf.py). store_hints.py not used (ad5aedd1), so <span title="Can a buyer find it? Yes if the phrase is a store search suggestion or a top-10 app under it has over 100 ratings.">Pull</span> reads only the top-10 clause. No apps.apple.com or support.apple.com page fetched (G2a).
- Commandment 7 before Health and Fitness: fitness only. Glucera (blood sugar), FaceVital (health monitor), Tipple (alcohol) and FoodGauge (food health scanner) are recorded and not read further. Music: data-driven only; M8 Music Tracker, Reel 4-Track, Serato DJ, Music Creator and play_music_theory are creation tools and not read.

## Here is the shape of it

The money this quarter, on these twelve genres, goes the same way H163 found: free downloads that charge weekly after a long set-up. The biggest wave is meal planning (7 entrants on Food and Drink grossing, 3 in its top 6). Its buyers are households counting dollars, and they write that the price at the till is not the price on the screen, because the app plans a pepper and the shop sells three. What are they doing instead? Copying the list into Instacart and finding it doubled, or "just go to TikTok" (TapCook us #79).
