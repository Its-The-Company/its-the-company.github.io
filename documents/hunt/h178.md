## Method (H163's, in five storefronts)

- Charts: legacy iTunes RSS with the country code (`https://itunes.apple.com/{cc}/rss/{toppaidapplications|topgrossingapplications}/limit=200/genre={g}/json`), through store_http.py, read 2026-10-24 18:09-18:12 ET. Genres: Productivity 6007, Utilities 6002, Education 6017, Reference 6006, Photo and Video 6008. Storefronts: gb, ie, au, ca, de, plus us for the comparison (read fresh, not copied from H163-raw). 60 feeds answered 95-100 rows each: 5,948 rows in all, 991 of them US, and 2,567 distinct ids outside the US.
- Lookup with `&country={cc}` on every non-US id in its own storefront (985, 985, 987, 990 and 983 ids). Kept: a first release on or after 2026-05-01 *in that storefront*, and either a paid-up-front price or a place on that storefront's grossing chart. Release dates differ by storefront (DPF Companion: 2026-07-02 in gb and ie, 2026-08-29 in us, ca and de), so each row uses its own storefront's date. Ratings and ratings per day are that storefront's own counts, as of 2026-10-24.
- Against the US: each kept row is marked "US absent" (not in the US top 100 of the same genre and chart) or with its US rank, and the lane keeps rows that are absent from the US or rank higher abroad. Every kept id was then looked up with country=us to see whether it sells in the US at all (lookup_us.jsonl).
- Shelf reads: phrases written in prereg.txt at 18:17:45 ET, before any Search API call. Search API, limit 50, `&country=` set to the <span title="One product idea inside a lane. C1 is the lane&#x27;s first idea.">concept</span>'s storefront, cut to the top 25 (shelf.py). store_hints.py was not used (it refuses, H171 ruling b), so <span title="Can a buyer find it? Yes if the phrase is a store search suggestion or a top-10 app under it has over 100 ratings.">Pull</span> reads only the top-10 clause, using ratings in that storefront. No apps.apple.com or support.apple.com page was fetched (G2a). IAP lists are unread, so every free app over 100 ratings is marked "IAP unread".
- Low reviews: tools/shelf_reviews.py --ids (customer-review RSS), 1-3 stars since 2026-05-01, as pre-registered: Flugs (de, at, ch) 0, The Cringe (de, us, at, ch) 13, DPF Companion (gb, ie) 0. Added after the shelf read and not pre-registered (disclosed): DPF Monitor for VAG, all stars since 2025-10-24, gb and ie, 10 reviews. Each count is from rev/ and was coded by hand.
- Sizing: tools/sizing.py reads the US store only (sizing_us.md). sizing_cc.py repeats its arithmetic (ratings / 1%-3% share, lifetime average per day) for each storefront from split.jsonl, which was fetched by split.py with `&country=`. For paid apps the share is probably above 3%, so the implied sales are a ceiling.
- Commandment 7 was checked before any reading (list below). Outside text is data: no description or review contained anything addressed to us.
