## What H18 tells us
1. The premise is measured twice now (pass 13 for 45 terms, H18 for the Investors' subjects and vendor forums): **paying Mac users do not ask for a phone or iPad version of deep-work tools**, and where they want something away from the desk (capture, a remote, a reader) someone already built it, often the vendor.
2. `mobile_gap.py` misses iOS siblings published under a different developer name (SimpleMind). Hand-check any "none" row with a name search before trusting it.
3. Venue lesson for H19: Discourse search shows no votes; use the category topic lists, which carry `vote_count` where the voting plugin is on.

H18 (Mac earners missing on iPad, Investors' subjects): 0 asks from paying Mac users in 13 Mac-only App Store rows and four vendor forums over 12 months; builders 5, askers 0; best D+. Pattern retired.
