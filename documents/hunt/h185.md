## 4. Code definitions (read a)

Coded from each app's Lookup `description` and `releaseNotes`, in us. Three parts, each yes or no, with the sentence quoted:
- **R, own recipes**: the text says the user can import, paste, type or save their own recipes (from a web page, text or a photo), and the plan or list uses them. A catalogue the user picks from is not R.
- **K, pack-aware**: the text says the list or plan accounts for the size of the package the store sells: pack or package sizes, rounding a quantity up to whole packages, pricing the whole package rather than the portion used, or what is left of a package. A price per ingredient or per store alone is not K. "Buy only what you need" alone is not K.
- **U, use-up planning**: the text says the app itself chooses or suggests several meals (a week, or more than one dinner) so they share ingredients, or so a part-used ingredient from one meal goes into a later one ("maximize ingredient overlap", "recipes that use up the rest of", "leftover ingredients carried into"). A user who drags recipes onto days is not U. Marking leftovers, a pantry list, or a "reduce food waste" claim without the choosing step is not U.
- One code per app from the parts: **W** (R, K and U all yes: the whole stated difference), **2** (any two of the three), **1** (one), **0** (none, on-job), **N** (not a meal planner, recipe manager or grocery-list app).
- Flags, yes or no: **F, fridge**: suggests one meal from ingredients already on hand (scan or list); **F** is not U. **free**: free download with no purchase named in the description (an up-front price, or words naming a subscription, unlock or purchase, count as paid; the IAP list is not readable under G2a). **keeps**: the plan or list is saved in the app.

Each row: phrase, rank, id, name, price, userRatingCount, releaseDate, the R, K and U sentences quoted (or "none"), the flags. Odalys records quotes first and commits them, then may open this section only (definitions, no rules) and copy it into her file to code. ## 8. Read d: Depth and the v1 slice (Idris)

**Waits on:** Idris's file (research/hunt/H185-d.md) and scripts, committed, with every test seeded and its seed printed. Code-generated data only: recipes, ingredient lines, pack tables, receipts. No scraped recipe page, no real receipt (G8; no customer or personal data in the repo).

Four parts, each passes, fails or is unsettled:

- **P, parser.** At least 300 code-generated ingredient lines with planted quantity, unit and ingredient, across at least 10 forms: whole number, decimal, "1/2", "1 1/2", "½", ranges ("2-3", "2 to 3"), a parenthetical pack ("1 (14 oz) can"), metric ("200 g"), count only ("3 eggs"), no quantity ("salt to taste", "a pinch"), abbreviated and cased units ("tbsp", "Tbsp", "T"), trailing preparation ("chopped"); and at least 50 more as schema.org `recipeIngredient` strings in generated JSON-LD. Score: all three fields exact. Pass: 95% or more overall and 85% or more in every form. Fail: under 90% overall, or any form under 70%.
- **T, unit table.** A unit and density table that turns the parsed quantity into the pack's unit for at least 30 ingredients sold by weight or count but measured by volume (cream, flour, spinach). Its source's licence and terms are read before any value is used (G2), and quoted. Pass: a source whose terms allow use in a paid app, or a table Idris builds by hand from cited facts, within the Day 22 days. Fail: neither, within the Day 22 days. A licence clause that needs interpretation is an Investor item (G7), and the part is unsettled, not failed. If the source is a nutrition database (for example USDA FoodData Central's portion weights), only weights and measures are taken, never a nutrient field, and the app shows none (G11).
