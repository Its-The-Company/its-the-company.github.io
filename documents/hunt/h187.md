## 0. Rules written before the first search (18:46 ET, 2026-10-24)

  - New: 20 phrases in 10 appsearch.py pairs, research/hunt/H187-raw/phrases_new.txt, all on the Investor subjects (science/math, language, computer science, automation/organisation, notetaking, walking, YouTube, data visualisation, movie/TV data). One search each, this session.
  - Re-used: 30 H170 phrases on the same subjects, research/hunt/H187-raw/phrases_h170.txt, with H170's own top 10 (research/hunt/H170-raw/top10.tsv, searched 17:10 today) not searched again. H170's off-subject phrases (measure, photo editor, photo cleaner, video editor, trip planner, offline maps, flight tracker, weather radar, screen time) are out. Trivia is in only as H170's third keep, re-read as the ruling asks; it stays killed by Fit (no games) whatever it measures.
- Top 10 = appsearch.py order, first 10 rows. An empty result is unchecked unless its pair-mate answered in the same call.
- Candidate weak top-5 rows (the ruling's start): a top-5 row whose current version is before 2025-10-24, or under 4.4 stars, or off-job. Off-job is first flagged by name this session and confirmed or cleared from the Lookup description held in read 1's raw JSON, next session, before read 2 is looked at. A phrase with no candidate row is "none" and killed (G26 unchanged).
- Rates: two Lookup reads of every top-10 id of every phrase, through tools/g26_snapshot.py (store_http.py, its cache switched off so the count is Apple's at that moment). Read 2 is at least 24 h after read 1 (the script refuses under 24 h). Ratings per day = userRatingCount delta / elapsed days. Never a lifetime or since-version average. A row missing from either read, or with a negative delta, is unchecked; a phrase with more than 2 unchecked top-10 rows is unchecked as a whole.
- Phrase traffic = sum of the top 10's delta rates. Lane downloads per day = that sum x 33 to 100 (sizing.py's 0.03 and 0.01 rating rates).
- Weakest top-5 row = the candidate row with the lowest delta rate. A delta of 0 counts as 0 (one day is noisy at low rates; that noise is stated, not corrected, and no third read is used for the keep).
- Price: median price of the top 10 (read 1). Free median: free plus unlock, conversion 2%-5% (window_share.py defaults); paid median: --paid.
- Keep test, the G26 kill unchanged: window_share.py --launch 22 --days 30 on the top-10 lane. Midpoint share = mean of its best and worst shares; Day 30 C or better when the midpoint share is 15% or under; and the weakest top-5 row's own share of the phrase's delta traffic must be at least the midpoint share. Both hold: kept. Either fails: killed by G26, no concept sought.
- H170's three keeps (planner, qr code reader, trivia) go through the same test on delta rates; H170's lifetime-average verdicts are not carried over.
- Kept phrases only, next session: the top 10's own low reviews by RSS (review_mine.py RSS mode) for the difference with depth.
- Not used: apps.apple.com pages (G2a), store_hints.py (H171 ruling). Passive reads only.
