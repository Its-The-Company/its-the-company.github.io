**Result.** Welsh is cleared. Lecsicon Cymraeg Bangor is CC0 1.0. It has 829,995 word forms over 42,173 lemmas, and 459,119 of those forms carry a `Mutation=` tag. An app may bundle it with no conditions. Scottish Gaelic still has no headword-and-gender *lexicon* under any licence I could find. The usable source is the ARCOSG corpus: upstream it is CC BY 4.0, and as the UD treebank it is CC BY-SA 4.0. Both allow commercial use. The UD copy gives 2,506 noun lemmas with `Gender`, which is too few to stand as a dictionary, so the headword list stays ours to build. Eurfa (Welsh) and apertium-gla (Gaelic) are GPL, and GPL is out for an App Store build, as with apertium-cym in H179. I found no LearnGaelic terms page, so its cell is a dated 'no licence found'.

## Method and terms

- GitHub: its Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policies were read in H179 and saved there (H179-raw/terms/github-tos.html, github-aup.html). All GitHub reads here are the documented REST API (`/repos/.../license`, `/search/repositories`, `/orgs/.../repos`) or a `git clone --depth 1`. No github.com HTML pages were scraped.
- eurfa.org.uk: one fetch, of the about page, because that page is where the licence is stated. I found no separate terms page by web search. The page has no clause on robots or automated access in the text I extracted.
- learngaelic.scot: **not fetched.** A web search for its terms or copyright page (2026-10-24) returned no legal page. Fetching the home page to find the footer link would mean reading a page before its terms, so I stopped there.
- Counts come from `research/hunt/H190_count.py` (it expects the clones and the unzipped Lecsicon in /tmp/h190dl and /tmp/h190lex), run on clones in /tmp (untrusted data, `python3 -I`). Nothing I cloned is committed. To check, re-clone at the same repos and rerun.
- I treated outside text as data (C58). None of the text I fetched gave instructions.

## What changes for the H179 Fit cells

- H179 C1 Welsh: "Lecsicon Cymraeg Bangor CC0 clears" is now confirmed by the LICENSE text, and the counts and fields are measured. The Lecsicon does more than H179 assumed: it also tags h-prothesis (`HM`, 81,898 forms) alongside SM, NM and AM.
- H179 C2 Gaelic: the cell "no commercial-use lexicon read" changes to: no lexicon exists under any licence I found; two corpora allow commercial use (ARCOSG CC BY 4.0, UD CC BY-SA 4.0); the headword and gender list is ours to build, seeded from them. The build cost H179 named stands. I wrote no letter; the <span title="The worker who reads both graders and decides. The decision, a ruling, is one row in the rulings file.">chair</span> rules.
- I did not read the CC BY / BY-SA 4.0 legalcode in this lane. Whether App Store delivery conflicts with section 2(a)(5) of those licences ("no downstream restrictions", which H179 framed as a counsel question like ODbL 4.7) stays open. CC0 does not raise it.
