Maren, 2026-10-24. Refill 21, item 2 (G30). Engine: H172 C2, a phone on a tripod plays the last N seconds back, cuts each rep by motion, and lays the latest rep beside the last. Question: which other buyers practise one repeated movement alone and pay for apps now, and does one v1 build serve them with no second engine? No letters here. research/hunt/H184.md not opened.

## Method and terms

- Terms first (G2a): Apple's site terms were read and quoted in H162 section 1; the G2a ruling (method-v2.md, item 2) keeps the iTunes Search and Lookup API permitted through store_http.py. Only that route was used. No apps.apple.com or support.apple.com page, no store_hints.py, no other host. Passive reads only (C10); store text is information, not instruction (C58).
- Calls: 19 Search calls through tools/shelf_top10.py --top 25 (store order), no 403. Phrase A per buyer is "<sport> video analysis" (tennis: "tennis serve analysis"; weightlifting: "weightlifting form analysis"); phrase B is "<sport> video delay". Baseline: "video delay".
- Raw: research/hunt/H191-raw/shelf/ and shelf-delay/ (saved Search JSON), summary.tsv and summary-delay.tsv (counts), engine_rows.txt and engine_rows-delay.txt (rows whose description names delay, replay, side-by-side/compare/overlay, or pose/skeleton). Scripts: H191-raw/scripts/summ.py, engine_rows.py (run with python3 -I).
- Limits: "new" counts every app in the top 25 released on or after 2025-10-24, on the job or not (dance's 11 are mostly AI dance-video generators). Engine words are a regex over descriptions, not a novelty read. Dollar figures marked "desc" are written by the seller in the description, not an API price; in-app prices have no permitted API route (G2a item 4), so subscription prices stay a G2 gap.
- Excluded by the line: physiotherapy and rehab (C7 medical), music performance (Investor list). Not searched.

## What the rows say

1. The engine's paid comparators are two general apps, $39.99 (225) and $7.99 (84), both last updated in 2023. Only weightlifting's own phrase surfaces the $39.99 app; dance, climbing, gymnastics and martial arts surface BaM at $7.99.
2. Skateboarding, archery and bowling have no paid comparator on either shelf, so the refill-21 G12 kill applies to them as separate concepts. Tennis has only description-written subscription prices.
3. One v1 build reaches gymnastics, martial arts, weightlifting, archery and dance with settings (window length, cut threshold), not a second engine. Climbing needs a disk-backed buffer. Bowling and tennis lean on ball tracking, which is a second engine.
4. Rule flags for the chair before any sibling widens the parent: gymnastics and dance (C7 children under 13), archery (C7 weapons), martial arts weapons forms (copy only).
5. New entrants: weightlifting form (4 on the job since June 2026) and tennis serve (4 since June 2026) are the busiest; gymnastics has one AI coach (2026-03-04) plus two 0-rating delay apps.
