Wrenna, counter. 2026-10-24, 19:16-19:19 ET by `date`. The counter applies no rule: the codes below are reads of what each description names, and the chair applies K176-A3 (H176 section 4) and anything else.

> "Full-year forecast with every high and low for the next 12 months, plus jump to any date"
> Tide Times - Tide Charts (6813647654), Pro list, US Lookup description, read 2026-10-24.

Here is the shape of it. Two of the 19 apps in the three top 10s name a year view for a place, and both put it behind a subscription. Neither says the year view carries sun or moon times; both describe sun and moon on the day curve or the day page. None of the 20 apps I read names a printable edition.

## 0. Method

- Searches: `LIMIT=50 tools/appsearch.py "tide chart" "tides" "tide times"` (US, iTunes Search API through store_http), cut to the first 10 rows per phrase. Raw: `H195-raw/appsearch.txt`, `H195-raw/top10.txt`. 30 slots, 19 distinct apps.
- Lookups: ids taken from `top10.txt` by `cut` into `H195-raw/ids.txt` (never typed), then `tools/shelf_pull.py H195-raw lookup` (store_http). Raw: `H195-raw/lookup-<id>.json`.
- One extra outside the top 10: Tides PRO (1016528618), the H176 A3 cell, id taken by `grep` from H176.md line 167. It is not in any of the three top 10s and is not counted in the totals.
- Sentences: `H195-raw/sentences.py` splits each description into sentences and keeps any naming year, month, calendar, 365, sun or moon times, lunar or solunar, print, PDF, export, edition or almanac (`sentences.txt`). `sentences2.py` keeps horizon and price sentences (N days, week, table, subscription, Premium, Pro; `sentences2.txt`). The four descriptions with no keyword hit (Tide Chart USA, NOAA Tides, Tides - high and low, Tide Times $0.99) and Ocean Watch were read whole.
- Codes (`H195-raw/codes.tsv`, one row per app, with the horizon quote and the pay quote): **Y** the description names a view a year ahead (12 months or 365 days) for a station the user picks; **M** it names a month view or a horizon of about 30 days, and no year; **N** neither. Sun, moon and printable are y when the description names sunrise or sunset times, moon phase or moonrise times, and a print, PDF or export of the tables. Counts below come from a script over codes.tsv, and every horizon quote was checked as a substring of its raw description.
- IAP lists: the Lookup JSON carries no in-app purchase field for any of the 20 apps (a field-name check returned none). IAP lists are not readable within the store terms (G2a; no apps.apple.com HTML), so the pay column quotes only what the description itself says.

- Sun times named: 12 of 19. Moon phase or moon times named: 12 of 19. Printable edition, PDF or export named: 0 of 19 (and 0 for Tides PRO).
- Both Y apps gate the year: Tide Guide, "Widgets, complications, detailed charts, and future table data require an active Pro subscription."; Tide Times - Tide Charts lists the full year under "Pro adds:" and "Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, available weekly or yearly."
- Neither Y description places sun or moon in the year view. Tide Guide puts the moon in a separate "Lunar Calendar" ("Easily track the current lunar phase with a beautiful lunar phase calendar."). Tide Times - Tide Charts: "Sunrise, sunset and moon phase, with daylight shown on the tide curve" (the day curve).
- Tides PRO (extra, $3.99, 390 ratings): code Y on the words "Tide calendar for the next 365 days" and "High and Low tide times for the next 365 days". Its sun and moon sentences sit under the graph ("Interactive graph shows tide predictions, current time, Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise, Moonset times") and in a feature list without a view named ("Sunrise/Sunset, Moonrise/Moonset times"). The H176 A3 question (does the 365-day calendar carry sun and moon?) is not answered by the description, as H176 already found; this read adds nothing to that cell beyond the same words.
- Ocean Watch coded N on its words, but flagged: "Interactive Almanac so you can see the future and make your plans." names an almanac with no horizon. Tide Charts coded M on "the next 30 days" (Apple Watch), and its "pick a date on the calendar" names no horizon either. A hands-on read could move either.

## 2. What the counts leave open (for the chair, no rule applied)

- Whether any Y app's year view shows sun and moon times on the same page as the tides, and whether any lets a buyer print or export it. Descriptions cannot settle it. Screenshots in the Lookup JSON (`screenshotUrls`) were not fetched: their host's terms were not read in this lane.
- IAP prices for the two Y subscriptions and for Tide Alert, Tides Near Me and TidesPro (G2a gap).
- The two Y apps sit at opposite ends: Tide Guide has 10,226 ratings and an Apple Design Award finalist line; Tide Times - Tide Charts is four weeks old with 0 ratings, so its year view is new on the shelf this autumn.
