Lane purpose (lanes.tsv, refill 23): what buyers pay for now in Notion, GoodNotes and Obsidian templates and digital planners, and what an iPhone app would compute from their own entries. Money first: the paid good comes before the difference.

## 0. Rules written before the first fetch (19:34 ET, committed e0523b87 at 19:34:47; first fetch 19:35)

Refill-23 section 2 rules bind. `python3 rules/lookup.py 7` was run at 19:34 ET, before any subject below was named.

**Phrases (5, fixed by the board line; none added or swapped after the first search).** 'digital planner', 'notion templates', 'planner templates', 'study planner', 'goodnotes templates'.

**Sources, in this order, each site's terms and robots.txt read before its first fetch.**
1. Notion's template gallery (notion.com/templates), for paid template prices, only if Notion's terms of service and robots.txt both allow a scripted read of the gallery. If the terms forbid automated access to the site, the gallery is a G2 gap, recorded and not read. At most 3 gallery pages (a category listing per phrase family), saved trimmed.
2. No Etsy, Gumroad, Amazon, Goodreads or apps.apple.com HTML reads (board line, refill 23). Earlier committed lane facts (H146's Gumroad rows, read 14:10 ET by Maren under H146's rules) may be cited by row and file, never re-fetched; they are labelled "H146, cited".
3. GoodNotes' marketplace and Obsidian: their own sites' terms are read before any read; if no listing page with prices is permitted, the gap is recorded.
4. Store: one iTunes Search per phrase through store_http.py (`entity=software`, `country=us`, `limit=50`, cut to the API's first 25; H166 lesson), saved per phrase with its header, fields trimmed (id, name, price, ratings, release, current version date, seller, genre, first 1,500 characters of description). Descriptions come from the Search answer itself (a permitted interface, G2a item 2). An empty or refused answer is retried once at the end; no phrase is replaced.

**Codes per top-25 row (scripted where a word list can do it, hand-checked on every row the counts rest on).**
- On-job: the app makes, sells or fills planner or template pages, or plans a student's or a person's tasks and time. Off-job otherwise (a game, a note app with no planning, a wallpaper maker).
- Money (from the description only): Paid (price above 0), Sub/IAP (description names a subscription, trial, Premium, Pro or in-app purchase), Free-unread (none named; the IAP list is a G2a gap).
- Compute: the description names a computation on the buyer's own entries (a schedule built from deadlines or free time, totals or progress from entries, spaced review dates, a grade or workload forecast, a streak or rollover). A list, a calendar layout, stickers, a PDF page set or a template catalogue is not a computation.
- New: released 2025-10-24 or later.

**Keep rule (a candidate needs all three).**
- K1 money: a paid good for the job exists with a price read from a permitted page (source 1, 3 or 4) or cited from H146.
- K2 computation: the job, done in an app, computes something from the buyer's own entries that the paid template only lays out (the T1 answer: input, output).
- K3 fit: not a Commandment 7 area (budget and money planners, health, fitness, mood, sleep and meal planners are out before the read), not a game, no product for children under 13 (a study planner is written for high school and up), not crochet, knitting or music creation.

**Kill rules, in order (first that fires is the reason).**
1. Shelf has it: 3 or more of the phrase's top 10 name the same computation in their description (the difference is gone).
2. Layout only: the paid good sells looks or page sets and nothing the buyer enters is computed (a decorated PDF planner, a sticker pack).
3. Clone wave (store form, method-v2 Crowding, provisional): 10 or more of the top 25 are New and none of the New rows is paid (up-front price or a named subscription or IAP) with over 100 ratings. Recorded and caps at C+; it does not remove the row.

**Concepts.** At most three, from kept candidates only. Each gets the method-v2 author row (`| Concept | Route | Pull | Novelty | Depth | Gate 1 | Crowding | Fit | Day 30 | Day 90 | Overall | Why |`), author letters, Overall left to the chair; Day 30 from `tools/sizing.py` on the phrase's top 10 (US, low 0.01, high 0.03). The candidate and phrase tables carry no letters. No candidate kept, no concept: the lane says so and that counts toward refill 23's falsifier.

**What would make this lane wrong.** Template buyers may never search the App Store: they buy on Etsy and Gumroad and use the result inside Notion or GoodNotes, so a store phrase's top 10 may measure a different buyer. The phrases are the board's; a job the five phrases do not surface is missed.

## 1. Sources read and gaps (19:35-19:39 ET)

- **Store**: 5 Search calls answered at 19:35-19:36 ET (43 to 48 results each, top 25 kept), `H196-raw/search.py`, raw per phrase in `H196-raw/search/`. - **Notion template gallery: G2 gap, not read.** robots.txt (`H196-raw/terms/notion_robots.txt`, fetched 19:36) allows `/templates/` but disallows `/templates/search?query=*`. The terms could not be read: the Terms and Privacy page (`notion.so/notion/Terms-and-Privacy-28ffdd083dc3473e9c2da6ec011b58ac`) redirects to app.notion.com and serves a JavaScript shell with no terms text (a plain fetch and a page reader both returned only the title "Notion"); `notion.com/terms` and `/legal` gave the same shell or 401/404. Section 0 lets the gallery be read only if the terms allow it; terms unread is not terms allowing, so no gallery page was fetched. H146 already found that gallery pages hide sales and duplicate counts (`number_of_duplicates` -1).
- **GoodNotes marketplace and Obsidian: not read** (no terms read, so no fetch). GoodNotes' paid templates sit inside its app, and Obsidian has no paid template store of its own that I know of; both are gaps, not zeros.
- **Etsy, Gumroad: not read** (board line). Gumroad prices below are **H146, cited** (Maren, read 2026-10-24 14:10 ET, `research/hunt/H146.md` section 2), not re-fetched.
- **Day 30**: `tools/sizing.py --low 0.01 --high 0.03 --asof 2026-10-24` on the 'digital planner' top 10 (`H196-raw/sizing_dp.md`), seven Notion companions (`sizing_notion.md`) and three paid template comparators (`sizing_paid.md`); `tools/window_share.py` in `H196-raw/window_share.txt`.
