## Method
New tool `tools/gh_asks.py`: `search` (issue search with `is:issue created:>=SINCE sort:reactions-+1`), `repo` (licence, stars, archived), `comments` (each comment's author association, so OWNER/MEMBER/COLLABORATOR marks a maintainer answer). Eight title queries: "iOS app", "iPad", "iOS" + "mobile app", "iPhone app", "iOS version", "iOS port", "iOS support", "iOS client"; plus one OSM query across StreetComplete, Organic Maps and iD. Top 20-25 per query read by title; the top asks opened.

Totals in the window (issues whose titles match; mostly bug reports): "iOS app" 14,840; "iPad" 7,012; "iOS support" 2,320; "iPhone app" 1,250; "iOS version" 1,022; "iOS client" 907; "iOS" + "mobile app" 663; "iOS port" 445.

## What H20 tells us
1. GitHub reactions measure asks to maintainers, and in 2026 maintainers answer with their own clients within months (OpenCode in nine months; StreetComplete porting). A third party arrives second, against a free official app.
2. The biggest cluster is AI coding-agent phone clients (98 + 13 + 6 + 6 + 4 +1 in a year). That is real attention, but the vendors (Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenCode) own it and give it away.
3. Lemmy search is unusable for counting; next time read named communities' feeds instead.
