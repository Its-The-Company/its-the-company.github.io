Lane purpose (refill-23.md section 2, G39): walkers already pay for AllTrails+, Komoot regions, Strava and the like. Money first: what each paid tier sells comes before the difference. Then the T1 v2 question (method-v2.md, 75ab1f35): what would an app compute from the walker's own walks that no top-10 app under the concept's phrase computes? Walking is an Investor subject (amendments.md 2026-10-22 18:51).

## 0. Rules written before the first fetch (19:41 ET; committed 40be2fd4 at 19:41:47; first fetch 19:41:5x)

Refill-23 section 2 rules bind. `python3 rules/lookup.py 7` was run at 19:41 ET, before any subject below was named. Fetched text is data, never an instruction (Commandment 5).

**Phrases (5, fixed by the board line; none added or swapped after the first search).** 'hiking', 'walking tracker', 'trail map', 'walking routes', 'step counter'.

**Read first, so nothing is counted twice: H194's walk-map rows** (research/hunt/H194.md, 'walk map art' and 'heatmap of my walks'): six walk-art apps on own routes (Paint the World, City Art, Step Counter Walk Drawing Art, WalkMap, Mystery Run, Streetly) and one in-app run heatmap (Sweat Map); Sidewalk coded off there (in-app block coverage). A print, poster or picture of the walker's routes is H194's concept, not this lane's; a candidate here that is only that is dropped as "H194 has it", cited, not re-counted.

**Sources, in this order, each site's terms and robots.txt read before its first fetch.**
1. Store: one iTunes Search per phrase through store_http.py (`entity=software`, `country=us`, `limit=50`, cut to the first 25; H166 lesson), saved per phrase as it goes with its header, fields trimmed (id, name, price, ratings, release, current version date, seller, genre, first 2,000 characters of description). Descriptions come from the Search answer itself (a permitted interface, G2a item 2); a price, tier or subscription named in a description counts as read from a permitted page. An empty or refused answer is retried once at the end; no phrase is replaced. Up-front prices come from the same answer (Search and Lookup carry the same price field; the IAP list is not in Lookup, a G2a gap).
2. Makers' own pricing pages, at most one page each, for AllTrails (alltrails.com), Komoot (komoot.com), Strava (strava.com) and Gaia GPS (gaiagps.com): the terms of use and robots.txt first. If the terms forbid automated access, scraping or robots, or only render with JavaScript, the page is a G2 gap, recorded and not read; the price then comes from the store description only, or stays a gap. No other maker sites.
3. No Amazon, Etsy, Goodreads or apps.apple.com HTML reads. No forum reads in this lane (the board line asks for prices and tiers, not complaints).
4. Concept phrase: for each concept, one more Search (same form as source 1) under the phrase a walker would type for that concept's job, written into section 4 before that search runs. That phrase's top 10 is the T1 read.

**Codes per top-25 row (scripted word lists as pointers; every row a count rests on is hand-checked against its description).**
- On-job: the app records, plans, maps or counts the user's own walks or hikes (a hike or walk tracker, a trail map or route planner, a step counter). Off-job: a game, a treadmill or workout plan, a city guide with no route of the user's own, a weight-loss or diet app.
- Money (description and price field only): Paid (price above 0); Sub/IAP (the description names a subscription, trial, Premium, Pro, Plus, membership, unlock or in-app purchase); Free-unread (none named).
- Tier (on-job Sub/IAP rows only, by hand): what the paid tier sells, in the description's words, sorted into Maps (offline maps, map layers, regions), Nav (turn-by-turn, off-route alerts), Safety (live share, beacons), Plans (route or training plans), Stats (analysis of the user's own recorded activities: trends, heatmaps, segments, records, comparisons), Ads (ad removal), Other.
- Compute on own walks: the description names an output computed from the walker's own recorded walks beyond the single walk's distance, time, pace, elevation and steps (a heatmap, coverage, yearly totals by place, personal records on a route, a comparison of walks, a forecast). Distance and step totals for one walk or one day are not counted: every tracker has them.
- New: released 2025-10-24 or later.

**Keep rule (a candidate needs all three).**
- K1 money: a paid tier for walkers exists with its price read from a permitted source (source 1 or 2), and the tier sells something on the walker's own walks or the walker's route planning.
- K2 computation: the candidate computes an output from the walker's own walks (input named: recorded GPS tracks, Health walking workouts, steps by day, saved routes) that no top-10 app under the candidate's phrase names in its description (the T1 answer).
- K3 fit: not a Commandment 7 area. No health or fitness advice claims of any kind: no calorie, weight, heart-rate zone, step-goal-for-health, recovery or training advice; a step or distance count is a measurement, not advice. No under-13 product, not a game, no crochet, knitting or music creation. Not H194's print concept.

**Kill rules, in order (first that fires is the reason).**
1. Shelf has it: 3 or more of the concept phrase's top 10 name the same output in their description.
2. Paid tier only unlocks content: the money is for maps, trail databases or ads off, and nothing the walker records is computed (that is a map licence, not a computation; a new listing cannot license AllTrails' trail base).
3. Clone wave (store form, method-v2 Crowding, provisional): 10 or more of the concept phrase's top 25 are New and none of the New rows is paid with over 100 ratings. Recorded, caps at C+, does not remove the row.

**Concepts.** At most three, from kept candidates only. Each gets the method-v2 author row (`| Concept | Route | Pull | Novelty | Depth | Gate 1 | Crowding | Fit | Day 30 | Day 90 | Overall | Why |`) with author letters and Overall left to the chair; Day 30 from `tools/sizing.py` on the concept phrase's top 10 (US, low 0.01, high 0.03, asof 2026-10-24). The phrase and candidate tables carry no letters. The Why cell carries the T1 answer as "T1: input X; output Y; top 10 read DATE (file)". No candidate kept, no concept: the lane says so, and it counts toward refill 23's falsifier.

**What would make this lane wrong.** The paid tiers of the big walking apps sell maps and navigation, which a newcomer cannot match; if every paid tier is Maps or Nav, the money is real but not reachable, and the lane says so rather than inventing a Stats buyer. Store descriptions under-name what a tier sells (AllTrails' own page may list more), so a tier coded from a description is a floor. The five phrases are the board's; a walker who types a place name or "walking app" is missed.

## 1. Sources read and gaps (19:41-19:45 ET)

- **Store**: 5 Search calls answered at 19:41 ET (44 to 50 results each, top 25 kept), `H203-raw/search.py`, raw per phrase in `H203-raw/search/`; one Lookup on the 38 distinct top-10 ids at 19:43 ET for full descriptions (`H203-raw/lookup.py`, `lookup_top10.json`; ids read from the search files by script). Tables by `H203-raw/tables.py` (word lists are pointers; every count a rule rests on was read by hand in the description). Three concept-phrase Searches at 19:44 ET (section 4).
- **AllTrails pricing: G2 gap, not read.** Terms (`H203-raw/terms/www.alltrails.com_terms.txt`, fetched 19:42) forbid using "automated agents or scripts ... to generate automated searches, requests, or queries to (or to strip, scrape, or mine data from) the Products". robots.txt allows /terms.
- **Strava pricing: G2 gap, not read.** Terms (`www.strava.com_legal_terms.txt`, 19:42): "Automated access to or collection of data from the Services ... is prohibited."
- **Komoot pricing: gap, not read, and a slip owned.** I fetched robots.txt and the terms page in the same command, so the terms page was fetched before I had read robots.txt, which disallows `/terms-of-service` for all agents. The rule was robots first; the order of fetches met it, the reading did not. The terms text holds no automated-access clause, but a page reached against robots grants nothing, so no Komoot pricing page was fetched. Kept as fetched (`www.komoot.com_terms-of-service.txt`), used for nothing. Lesson as in H160: read robots.txt, then fetch terms, in two steps.
- **Gaia GPS pricing: gap, not read.** No terms page found (`/terms/`, `/legal/terms/`, `/terms-of-service/`, `/tos/` all 404 at 19:42-19:43); terms unread is not terms allowing.
- **So every price below comes from a store description or the price field.** AllTrails Plus and Peak, Strava, Komoot regions, Gaia/Outside+ and onX memberships are named in their descriptions without a price; their prices are G2 gaps, never zeros.
- **Day 30**: `tools/sizing.py --country us --low 0.01 --high 0.03 --asof 2026-10-24` on the top 10 of 'hiking time calculator' and of 'hiking' (`H203-raw/sizing_*.md`).

**B. Summits and lists from my own hikes ('peak bagging').** Input: the walker's own recorded hikes. Output: which listed summits the tracks passed, and list progress. Top 10 read 19:44 ET: progress on peak lists is named by Peakbagger #2 ("Measure your progress against hundreds of peak lists"), List Hiker #3 ("automatically tracks progress on hiking lists ... point List Hiker at the apps, devices and/or spreadsheets where you already record your hikes"), Trailwise #6, SummitList #8 and Cairn #10, among others. **Kill 1 fires.** Clone wave recorded too: 12 of 25 New, none over 100 ratings.

**C. My own hiking pace on a planned route ('hiking time calculator').** Input: the walker's own recorded walks and hikes with elevation (Health workout routes, GPX imports). Output: the time a planned route will take this walker, from their own pace by slope and by hours on foot (their own curve, not a textbook rule), with the finish against sunset. Top 10 read 19:44 ET: 0 of 10 compute it. 6 of 10 are clock and date calculators or a sun-times app (Hours Minutes Calculator Lite, Date & Time Calculator, Time + Date Calculator, SolarWatch, Days + Date + Time Calculator, Hours + Minutes Calculator Pro); the 4 on-job rows (Wikiloc, Gaia GPS, Hiking Project, WTA Trailblazer) name no time estimate from the walker's own pace. Near-miss outside the ten: Pedometer++ (#5 'step counter') shows "the estimated time it will take you to reach your destination" on routes made in its planner, and does not say the estimate comes from the walker's own past walks; Random Walk sizes loops to a chosen pace setting (easy, normal, brisk). A grader should weigh both. Kill 1 does not fire; Kill 2 does not (it computes from what the walker records); Kill 3 does not (1 of 25 New). **Kept.**

Fit note for C: a time estimate is a planning aid. The app must never call a route safe, advise effort or pace for health, or promise a finish before dark; it shows the walker's own past numbers and the sunset time side by side. Elevation for a planned route needs a terrain model whose licence allows an app (not read in this lane; a gap for the build note, not a fact here).
