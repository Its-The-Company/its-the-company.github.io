## 0. Rules, written before the first candidate fetch

### 0a. Terms and robots (G2a), read 2026-10-24 22:01-22:04 ET, copies in `H205-raw/terms/`

- `commons.wikimedia.org/robots.txt` (`terms/robots.txt`, 491 lines). The `User-agent: *` group allows `/w/api.php?action=mobileview&`, `/w/load.php?`, `/api/rest_v1/?doc` and the sitemap, then **disallows `/w/`, `/api/`, `/trap/` and `/wiki/Special:`**. `/w/api.php` (the Commons API) is under `/w/`. Nothing bars `/wiki/File:...` or `/wiki/Category:...`.
- `upload.wikimedia.org/robots.txt` (`terms/upload_robots.txt`): `User-agent: *`, `Disallow: /wikipedia/commons/archive/` only. Current originals and transcodes are not under `/archive/`.
- Wikimedia Foundation Terms of Use (`terms/foundation_terms_of_use.html`): bars "automated uses of the Project Websites that are abusive or disruptive of the services, violate acceptable usage policies where available, or have not been approved by the Wikimedia community"; for the APIs it incorporates the User-Agent Policy, the Robot Policy and API:Etiquette by reference.
- Wikitech Robot policy (`terms/wikitech_robot_policy.html`): "Honor every directive in our robots.txt file"; "If you need the HTML content of pages use ... the /wiki/Article_name URL"; website reads under 10 concurrent and 20 a second; Action API rules (unauthenticated: concurrency 1, under 5 a second); media (upload.wikimedia.org): concurrency at most 2, 25 Mbps, "prefer thumbnails to downloading of originals if possible"; 5xx: pause 15 minutes. User-Agent policy (`terms/foundation_user_agent_policy.html`): a descriptive User-Agent with contact information.

Ruling I apply (Ines's robots ruling, rulings-v2.md row "robots (Ines, 19:52): paths": a host's `*` disallow bars the path pending the Investors; doubt goes to the stricter reading). The Robot policy both documents the Action API for unauthenticated programs and tells robots to honor every robots.txt directive, and robots.txt disallows `/w/`: a real doubt, so **no `/w/api.php` call and no `Special:Search` in this lane**. Route instead, all on allowed paths and recommended by the Robot policy: category pages and File pages through `/wiki/...` (serial, one request at a time, 1 s apart, gzip, the company User-Agent), video files from `upload.wikimedia.org` (one download at a time, a standard transcode where one exists rather than the original).

Deviation from the board line, disclosed: the line says the licence is "read through the Commons API". The API is barred by the reading above, so the licence is read by script from the File page's licence template (the `licensetpl_short`, `licensetpl_link` and `licensetpl_attr` spans the templates emit, the same fields the API's extmetadata serves) and the upload dates from the page's file-history table. If the chair reads robots.txt the other way, nothing here is lost: the page fields are the API fields.

A second category list, named now in `commons/extra_cats.txt` (strength-training demonstrations, sports training, sports science, yoga, martial arts, capoeira, athletics, balance challenges, juggling, parkour, bodybuilding, treadmills, gymnastics training, trampolines, blacksmithing, woodcutting, rowing, boxing, skipping, weight training), is read the same way and merged; the whole pool is then shuffled once with seed 205. Also found and fixed before any download: the first parser read no licence (Commons escapes the underscore in `licensetpl_short` as `&#95;`) and no original URL (the href starts with `https:`); re-parsed from the saved pages, no refetch.
- Amendment 2, 22:30-22:31 ET, still before any download or sheet: the merged pool is 532 passing clips (683 pages read, 151 rejected on upload date; `commons/find.log`), most of them team sport, races, newsreels, martial arts bouts and gymnastics routines, so 24 sheets in seed order would show perhaps two rep clips. `bin/select.py` keeps the seed-205 order and sheets only clips whose title, description or category names carry a repeated-movement keyword (list in the script and `commons/queue.json`; metadata only, no frame seen); its first list also had "exercise", "yoga", "jump", "punch", "kick", "rep", "step" and "gymnastics training", which pulled in floor routines, meditation and long-jump finals, and was tightened once (v2) before any download. Queue: 33 clips, all under 80 MB and 1,200 s. All 33 are sheeted (the 24-sheet stop is lifted to the whole queue); the keep rule in the viewing paragraph is unchanged.
- File filter, by script: extension .webm, .ogv, .ogg or .mp4; licence template short name matching `CC0`, `Public domain`, `PD`, `CC BY x.y` or `CC BY-SA x.y` (a file with several templates passes if one of them matches; GFDL-only fails); earliest file-history date before 2022-01-01; a transcode or original of at most 80 MB.
- Order: passing files shuffled with seed 205; downloaded and sheeted in that order; viewing stops when at least 8 clips are kept with 40 or more true reps in all, or after 24 sheeted clips, whichever comes first. Every sheeted clip gets a kept/rejected line with the reason.
- Viewing rule, applied to the code-made contact sheet (one frame every 0.2 s, all frames of the clip, viewed before any cut runs): keep when (i) one adult is the only person moving and no child appears in any frame (anyone who could be under 18: leave the clip out); (ii) the camera is fixed: background edges hold their place across the whole sheet (any pan, zoom or handheld drift visible between frames: reject); (iii) the person repeats one movement at least 3 times and I can place each rep's start to one sheet frame. A clip that fails any one is rejected with that reason; "if in doubt, leave it out" (Commandment 9 and the child rule).
- Truth: for each kept clip, the start time of every true rep (seconds, the sheet frame's time) and the end time of the last rep. Rep start = the first sheet frame in which the body leaves the rest position of the cycle. For continuous cycling with no rest (rope skipping, jumping jacks), rep start = the frame at the same phase each cycle (the lowest point), stated per clip. Stressors seen (passers-by, light steps, loose clothing, a far subject) are noted per clip. Truth lives in `H205-raw/truth.json`; the sheet manifest (sheet file names, frame times, SHA-256) in `H205-raw/sheets.json`. The sheets and clips are footage and stay out of git at `/Users/thecompany/h205-footage/` (clip SHA-256 in `log/assets.md`); the truth and manifest are committed before the cut runs on any clip.

### 0c. The cut and its settings, frozen now

`cut_reps` from `tests/h184_rep_test.py` at 6b98f1a8 (the H184-d commit), imported unchanged: `win=5, hi_k=0.30, lo_k=0.12, merge=6, min_len=10`, `ABS_HI=0.6, ABS_LO=0.25` grey levels. Input: every decoded frame of the clip at its native frame rate, converted to grey and resized to 48x48 (area interpolation, float), the size the floor was set for in D3. No tuning on any clip; the primary table is this run.

One secondary read, declared here before any clip is seen or run: the same cut with the frame-unit settings scaled to the clip's frame rate (`win, merge, min_len` multiplied by fps/30 and rounded, floor 1), to show whether frame rate alone explains misses at 50 or 60 fps. Declared, not tuned; reported beside the primary, never in its place.

### 0d. Metrics

A cut "hits" a true rep when its start lies within 0.5 s of the rep's start (\|cut start - rep start\| <= 0.5 s); cuts and reps pair one to one, nearest first. Per clip and pooled: (a) hit share = reps with a hit / true reps; (b) false cuts over true reps = cuts that hit no rep / true reps. Also shown, secondary: cut count vs true count, and the median start error of hits.

### 0e. Memory and software

Frames are decoded one at a time with OpenCV (opencv-python-headless 5.0.0, already in log/software.md) and reduced at once to a 48x48 float32 array and a 192 px sheet thumbnail; no full-resolution frames are kept. No new software.
