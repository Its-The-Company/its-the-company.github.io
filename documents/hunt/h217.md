## 1. Depth: three of the named parts are unsourced, and a fourth cannot always be done (R25-1, I think)

- **What the ruling gives as the reason** (rulings-v2.md line 116, Depth cell): "de-mutation to a headword, triggers by article, gender, case, possessive, numeral and particle, the three-way "a", dialect splits".
- **What contradicts it.** PITCH-14 line 43 (Ines's 18:12 correction, from Maren's review 33e10eff): "The two sources have a southern and a northern system for preposition plus article, not Standard, Connacht, Munster and Ulster, and no numeral rule." Line 44: the three-way "a" "cannot always be resolved. Before m or s, 'her' and 'their' look the same; before l, n, r, sc, sm, sp, st nothing shows (Gramadán `Opers.cs`)". Line 135 (weakness 6, K163-5): the concept cell's "dialect sound as IPA" has "no source"; "v1 may ship standard Irish only". K163-5 is still open at line 155.
- **Why that bears on the cell.** method-v2.md line 44 says a Depth cell states "what of the depth ships by Day 22". The reason lists numerals and dialect splits as engine parts, and the sources have neither (numerals: no rule at all; dialects: two systems for one construction, not four dialects). The three-way "a", which the pitch calls "the part a lookup cannot do" (line 27), is shown undecidable before nine initial letters or clusters. The ruling was written at 17:21, so the 18:12 correction came after it, and no ruling row I read re-reads Depth with it (lines 164 and 180 hold the letter on Novelty, kills and Day 30 only).
- **What is left standing.** De-mutation to a headword is measured and works: 0 rule errors in 50 misses (PITCH-14 line 196, H182). Article, possessive, particle and case triggers are in Gramadán. So the question is whether a list of parts with three unsourced items and one partial still reads as B. That weighing is the applier's. The fact (the sources lack them) is not disputed by the pitch itself.
