### 2. Fit

**The reason.** "pass, provisional: ... import, rep cut and synced side by side can be proven on the Simulator with code-generated frames (Idris)."

**What contradicts it.** The rep cut passed on code-drawn frames and missed on real motion with the same committed settings. `H184-d.md` line 5: "Rep cut by motion works on code-drawn frames (20 of 20 clips exact, 240 of 240 reps ...) and fails as expected on a 20-frame hold at the top (0 of 20)"; line 7: "Synthetic results show the logic is right. They do not show that it works on a person. Passed is not the same as correct." `research/hunt/H205.md` line 69: on 3 real clips, 10 true reps, "hit share 0.70", "false cuts over true reps 1.30"; line 74: "Every burpee and sprawl is cut in two or more." Ines's own chair finding (H205.md line 88): "a motion-gap cut splits any rep with a pause in it ... so a v1 rep counter needs a cycle model ... or one-tap marking." So the rep cut is not something the Simulator can prove: it proved the logic and the real result disagreed. The fact for the reason's word "proven" is the Simulator pass, and that pass did not hold on a person.

**Weight, stated against myself.** H205's counts are below its floor (8 clips, 40 reps) and the lane is "open (G2), counts recorded only" (rulings-v2.md line 218).
