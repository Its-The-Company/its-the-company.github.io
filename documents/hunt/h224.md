## Point 1. Day 90

- **Reason the ruling gives.** rulings-v2.md line 24, Day 90 cell: "H124 s5: H125 Geminids right >=97.8% to sigma 30, 80% named at 5.6 deg; compass path 1-3 deg". That reason is the naming gate; the money under the cell is H113.md line 67 and `H113-raw/window-share.txt` line 4: 0.4%-1.4% of 4,080-12,247 lane downloads in 68 days.
- **What contradicts it.** The lane is NightCap + MeteorActive + Slow Shutter Cam (`sizing-meteor.txt` lines 5-7). Line 6 lists MeteorActive at "Free", and H113.md line 60 reads the "meteor shower" top 10, MeteorActive first, as "All of them forecast or map the sky; 0 of 10 catch meteors". A free forecaster's downloads are not buyers of a $4.99 catcher's job, by the lane's own reading.
- **The number without it.** NightCap + Slow Shutter Cam: 0.6%-2.3% of 2,475-7,419 downloads. NightCap alone (the one entrant H113.md line 64 shows selling the job): 1.1%-4.1% of 1,428-4,277.
- **Weight, not fact (R25-2 if the applier reads it so).** NightCap's own reviews mention meteors in 9 of 161 low and 12 of 179 happy (H113.md line 64), so even NightCap-only counts buyers of other jobs; Slow Shutter Cam has no meteor read on file (`descs-shutter.txt` is the shutter-count lane, not this app).

- **Reason the ruling gives.** rulings-v2.md line 24, Novelty "B (both)"; H113.md line 60: "Novelty B rests on naming the shower. Catching alone would be B- (the leader catches already)."
- **The facts.** H124.md line 134 (chair): "the IMO rule misnames about 20% of sporadics at every sigma". H128.md line 3: the classic detectors run at 21% and 38% precision on a clear moonless night (5.8 and 2.0 false an hour); 80% or more is published only after a CNN trained on a labelled archive we do not have.

On the only published phone-free figures, most grid entries from a classic detector are not meteors, and one sporadic in five carries a wrong shower name. Neither fact says another app names showers, so the reason's fact ("no entrant names the shower") stands; this is a point about how much the difference shows, which R25-2 writes as a doubt.
