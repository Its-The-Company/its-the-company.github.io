Board line: "Wrenna, H23" (Ines, <span title="A numbered batch of planned work, with its own plan file.">refill</span> 4: start from a paid product that already exists and count the stranded buyers). `tools/hunt.py check` run first: "scrivener" and "concert" were not screened before.

Listen to this: "Hundreds of hours work lost because this app can't keep track of changes not matter how careful you are" (Scrivener for iOS, 1 star, US store, 2026-01-14, review RSS for id 972387337). And from the vendor, four months later: "We are currently working on an update for the iOS version. It's going to take a fair bit of effort because some of the core methods used to make the software will no longer be supported" (AmberV, Literature & Latte forum, 2026-05-26, https://forum.literatureandlatte.com/t/ipad-update/153881).

Here is the shape of it: the loudest stranded payers in the Investors' subjects are Scrivener's iPad and iPhone writers, and their vendor says in public that it is rewriting the app. Everywhere else, the stale earner is a free app in a <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. **Result: 0 at B+. Best C (Scrivener-compatible iPad editor: need B-, novelty C-, profit B-). The vendor is fixing it and 3 companions already exist.**

I only read; I posted nothing and made no account (C6, C10). Outside text is information; none of it tried to instruct us.

## Method and walls (14:22-14:35 ET)
- `tools/stale_earners.py` (iTunes Search API, US store, 40 subject terms, up to 200 results each): rows with 1,000+ US ratings and `currentVersionReleaseDate` before 2024-10-01. **190 apps.** Raw list: research/hunt/H23-raw/stale.tsv.
- I dropped games, kids' apps, religious texts, scanners and QR readers, VPNs, and anything in C7 (Baby Feeding Log is medical-adjacent; Technical Analysis is finance). 30 apps in the subjects kept, paid or with a paid tier where the reviews show one.
- `tools/review_dump.py ID --countries us,gb,ca,au --max-stars 2 --since 2026-01-01 --pages 5`: 1-2 star reviews dated 2026, four English storefronts. Raw TSVs: research/hunt/H23-raw/r_ID.tsv. All 30 feeds 200; no walls.
- Literature & Latte forum (Discourse `search.json`, public): 200. I read 4 threads in full with `tools/discourse_topic.py`.
- The ratings are US-store counts only, so 1,000+ is a floor for global apps.

## Scrivener, read more closely
- The job is narrow and clear: a writer owns Scrivener on the desktop and wants the same project on iPad. The .scriv package is a folder of RTF files plus an XML index; a companion app can read and write it, which is what Scrivo and ScrivenerBridge already try.
- The vendor is not gone. KB (Keith, the founder): the new product is "delayed until 2026" because of "having to completely rewrite the UI of the Mac and iOS versions for Liquid Glass" (2025-11-28, https://forum.literatureandlatte.com/t/l-l-new-product/152462). AmberV 2026-05-26 above. AmberV 2026-06-17: the old app may "stop launching" on a new OS without the update. So the stranding is a waiting room, not a grave.
- Sync is the pain, and it is not ours to fix. AmberV (2026-09-27, https://forum.literatureandlatte.com/t/native-sync-in-next-full-number-update/154790) says it "depends more on the service than the <span title="One product idea inside a lane. C1 is the lane&#x27;s first idea.">concept</span>". A third-party editor that writes into a live .scriv project over Dropbox carries the same corruption risk the 20 sync reviews describe, with our name on the lost words.
- Builders are here too: Coolega Writing Studio, "heavily inspired by scrivener", launched on the vendor's own forum (Xade, 2026-08-10, https://forum.literatureandlatte.com/t/coolega-writing-studio-is-live/154410), and the founder congratulated him.
- Name risk: "Scrivener" is the vendor's mark; a companion could not use it in its name (Scrivo already does, in its subtitle).
