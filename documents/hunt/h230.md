Idris, 2026-10-25. Brief: research/hunt/refill-27.md section 3 L27-1; board line "Idris, P2, H230". Counts only; the applier (Odalys) reads them against the fail and hold lines. I am not H163's author and I write no letter here.

## 0. Rules, written and committed before any fetch or script (03:21 ET)

**What L27-1 asks, and how I read it.** At least 300 modern Irish sentences with at least 1,000 fada vowels, licence-clear, rendered by code in three fonts whose licences allow it; seven conditions; Vision text recognition, accurate level, H182's language settings; fada recall on words located correctly, false fadas per vowel, word accuracy, per condition. Fail on the combined condition: fada recall under 90% or false fadas over 2% of vowels. No photograph and no generative model (C9).

**Source.** The Irish Wikipedia dump already on disk from H182 (`H182-raw/dl_wiki/gawiki-latest-pages-articles.xml.bz2`, dated 2026-10-01; not re-downloaded). Before the sentence script runs, this session reads `https://dumps.wikimedia.org/robots.txt` and `https://dumps.wikimedia.org/legal.html` through `tools/terms_guard.fetch` (one request each, company User-Agent) and saves both to `H230-raw/terms/`. If either refuses the use, I stop and say so. Expected licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 / GFDL for the text; test input only, titles and revision ids kept for attribution. "Modern Irish" = present-day Wikipedia prose (Caighdeán spelling); no pre-reform text is sought.

**Sentence rule (`H230-raw/bin/sentences.py`).** Articles in namespace 0, not redirects, shuffled with seed 230; articles used by H182 (`coverage_articles.json`) or H200 (`texts.json`) dropped. Text cleaned by H200's cleaner (imported from `H200-raw/bin/texts.py` logic, copied, not changed). Prose lines only (same filter: at least 40 characters, letters, digits, space and `. , ; : ' ( ) -` only). Sentences split after a full stop followed by a space and a capital. A sentence is kept if it is 40 to 140 characters, ends with a full stop, has at least 6 words, has no digit, and has at least one fada (a e i o u with acute, either case). At most one sentence per article (the first that passes). Sentences are taken in shuffled order until both 300 sentences and 1,000 fada vowels are reached, then the set is closed. No sentence is read by me or by Vision before the set is closed.

**Font rule (`H230-raw/bin/fonts.swift`).** Eligible: an installed, upright, regular-weight face whose own name table licence field (nameID 13) or licence URL (nameID 14) names the SIL Open Font License, and which maps every character of the closed sentence set with no fallback. Families sorted by name; chosen: the first eligible serif family (Core Text stylistic class oldstyle, transitional, modern, clarendon, slab or freeform serif), the first eligible sans family (sans-serif class), and the first eligible family left of any class. If a class has none, the next family by name fills the place, disclosed. The three are logged in log/assets.md (test fixtures only, never shipped). The renderer stops if any glyph run reports a font other than the one asked for.

**Render (`H230-raw/bin/render.swift`, code only).** Grey 8-bit images 1080 px wide x 1440 px high ("1080 px scale": all pixel sizes below are at this width), white ground, black text, 36 px type, 54 px leading, margins 60 px, so 960 px text width. Each sentence starts a new line and wraps at spaces (Core Text framesetter, no hyphenation). Sentences fill pages in set order; a page closes when the next sentence would not fit. Every page is rendered in each of the three fonts (pagination per font, since widths differ). Truth is the sentence text itself; no transcription by eye.

**Seven conditions** (order of operations as a camera: warp, then blur, then noise, then compression; lossless PNG unless a JPEG quality is named):
1. clean.
2. persp15: camera rotation 15 degrees about an in-plane axis at a seeded random angle, homography K R K^-1, f = 1440 px, principal point at centre, scaled down (never up) to fit with 40 px margin, outside the page 0.6 grey (H200's warp).
3. persp30: the same at 30 degrees.
4. blur: Gaussian, sigma 1.5 px.
5. jpeg40: JPEG quality 40 (ImageIO lossy quality 0.40).
6. lowlight: my reading of "noise sigma 12 on 8-bit with gamma 0.6": each value v in [0,1] becomes v^0.6 (the usual definition, out = in^gamma), then 255 v' + N(0, 12), rounded and clipped to 0-255.
7. combined: 15 degrees, blur sigma 1.0, noise sigma 8 (no gamma), JPEG quality 60.
Seed per image = 230000 + 100 x page + 10 x font + condition, SplitMix64 (H200's generator); every drawn axis angle is saved in `params.json`. The images are not committed except one sample page per condition; the scripts and seeds rebuild the rest.

**Vision (`H230-raw/bin/ocr.swift`, `swiftc` command line, macOS Vision, no app target, no Simulator).** `VNRecognizeTextRequest`, `.accurate`, revision 3, the settings H182 used: config A, `recognitionLanguages = ["es-ES"]`, `usesLanguageCorrection = false`; config B, automatic language with correction on. H182's third setting (en-US, correction off) gave text identical to es-ES on all 12 pages and H200 found the same, so it is not rerun (disclosed). **Config A decides the fail test** (it is the config H182 and H200 scored); B is reported beside it, and if the two disagree on fail or hold both are stated and the applier rules.

**Scoring (`H230-raw/bin/score.py`), fixed now.** Text NFC, casefolded. Words split on white space, edge punctuation `. , ; : ( ) '` stripped; base form = word with all combining marks removed. Each truth sentence is aligned on its own to the page's OCR word stream (Vision lines joined in returned order) by semi-global edit distance on base forms (substitution cost 0 when base forms are equal, else 1; insertion and deletion 1; ties prefer the diagonal), so line order and fada errors cannot move the location.
- *Located correctly*: a truth word aligned to an OCR word with the same base form.
- *Fada recall*: in located words, truth fada vowels that the OCR word has as the same accented letter at the same position / truth fada vowels in located words. Also reported on all words (unlocated words count as misses), so the located-only figure cannot flatter.
- *False fadas per vowel*: in located words, truth bare vowels (a e i o u) that the OCR word carries with an acute / truth bare vowels in located words. Any other mark on a bare vowel (grave, diaeresis, tilde) is counted apart as "other mark" and reported, not added to the fail figure.
- *Word accuracy*: truth words aligned to an OCR word equal to it exactly (after casefold and edge punctuation) / all truth words.
Pooled over the three fonts per condition and config; per font also given. Fail/hold is read on the pooled combined row, config A, against L27-1's two bounds; nothing else is graded here.

**Stops.** A terms or robots refusal stops the lane. A font fallback stops the render. Fewer than three eligible fonts: I stop and report, rather than use a font whose licence I have not read in its own file.

## 1. What was done (03:22-03:34 ET)

- **Terms first.** `dumps.wikimedia.org/robots.txt` returned 404 through `tools/terms_guard.fetch` (no rules; saved as `H230-raw/terms/robots.txt` with the error text). `legal.html` read and saved, byte-identical to H200's 2026-10-24 copy: text under GFDL and CC BY-SA 4.0, "used for any purpose (including commercial purposes!)". The dump itself was not fetched again (on disk from H182).
- **Sentences** (`bin/sentences.py`, as section 0): 71 titles from H182/H200 excluded, 64,413 articles shuffled with seed 230, closed at **300 sentences, 1,263 fada vowels** (`sentences.json`, with title and revision id each, for attribution). Wikipedia prose carries proper nouns in other languages (e.g. "Beaune-la-Rollande", "Yorkshire"); they stay, as section 0 set no filter for them.
- **Fonts** (`bin/fonts.swift`, `fonts_eligible.json`): 12 installed faces state the SIL OFL 1.1 in their own name table; 3 cover every character of the set: **Mukta Mahee** (sans, class 8), **STIX Two Math** and **STIX Two Text** (both oldstyle serif, class 1). The rule therefore chose all three, and they are the only ones it could choose. **Disclosed:** STIX Two Math's Latin letters follow STIX Two Text's design, so the set has two letter designs, not three; per-font rows are below so the applier can weigh that. Logged in log/assets.md.
- **Renders** (`bin/render.swift`): 73 pages (Mukta 23, STIX Math 25, STIX Text 25) x 7 conditions = 511 images, 151 MB, kept in /tmp, not committed; one page per condition in `H230-raw/samples/` (STIX Two Text page 0, 2.2 MB). Each image read back for size and type after writing; no font fallback stop fired; every sentence fully set. I viewed the clean, low-light, 30 degree and combined images of Mukta page 3 at 720 px before scoring. **Deviation, disclosed:** Core Text also breaks lines after a hyphen, not only at spaces as section 0 said; a hyphenated word split across lines is unlocated (seen: "Beaune-la-Rollande", "nua-aois", "h-chruthacha"), which lowers word accuracy slightly and does not touch fada recall on located words.
- **Vision** (`bin/ocr.swift`, swiftc, macOS 27 Vision, accurate, revision 3; 16 processes, 8 min 17 s): 511 images x 2 configs; raw output in `H230-raw/ocr/`. **Scores** (`bin/score.py`, rule as section 0): `scores.json`, rows pooled and per font, plus per image.

## 4. What this does not show

- These are code renders with a model of damage, not photographs (C9): a phone at a street sign at dusk, glare, motion blur, curved pages, small type and real sensors are unread. Refill 27 section 8 says this already; the hold wording it names is "closed on code-degraded renders; real photographs unread (C9)".
- Two letter designs, not three (section 1). No Gaelic type, no signage typeface, no handwriting.
- 36 px type on a 1080 px page is large and clean; the result says nothing about small print.
- Truth is the source text, so no transcription error; Wikipedia text may carry its own spelling slips, which count against neither side since the render shows what the text says.
- Turn budget: 10 given; this lane took about 20 tool turns (over, disclosed).
