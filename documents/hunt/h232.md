## 0. Rules, fixed before the first fetch (committed before any request)

Read: refill-27.md section 3 (L27-3 lower and <span title="What keeps an idea below the top: the cap that applies to it now.">hold</span> clauses), <span title="The worker who reads both graders and decides. The decision, a ruling, is one row in the rulings file.">rulings</span>-v2.md line 116 (H163 C1 chair row), H219-raw/bin/fetch.py (fetcher copied). Commandments 6 and 10: reading public pages only; no form, sign-up, enquiry, message or contact of any provider.

### 0.1 What is counted

- **Stated adult enrolments a year**: a number the provider itself writes on its own page or annual report for learners, students, participants or enrolments in its paid adult Irish classes or courses, with the year or period it covers. Quoted verbatim with the page.
- **Price per course**: the fee the provider's own page states for one adult course, in its currency, with the course name.
- **Not counted**: any figure I would have to infer (timetables, class lists, number of venues times seats), a figure for children or school pupils, a figure for free classes stated as free, a third party's figure about a provider, or a total that mixes Irish with other subjects without a split. A figure covering several years is recorded as stated and marked "multi-year", not divided.
- One row per provider: provider, page URL, quoted figure (or "no figure stated"), period, price quote, notes. Absence is recorded as absence, not zero.
- CSO census tables (CC BY 4.0): context only (adults who speak Irish, by frequency). They state no class enrolments, so they enter no L27-3 count.

### 0.2 Hosts, in this order

Every host: /robots.txt first, saved under H232-raw/robots/; then its terms or legal page, only if robots.txt allows that path; then course or report pages, only if both allow. The fetcher (H232-raw/bin/fetch.py) checks in code, before each request, the saved robots.txt (tools/robots_rules.py, User-agent *) and, for any page, a "HOST<TAB>clear" row in H232-raw/terms-cleared.tsv, which I write only after reading the terms. All requests go through tools/terms_guard.fetch. A refusal prints REFUSED and sends nothing. Terms unread or barring scripted reads: <span title="A figure we could not read because the source&#x27;s terms or robots file do not let our software read it. It never lowers a grade.">G2 gap</span>, no page.

1. gaelchultur.com (Gaelchultúr: paid adult courses, Ranganna.com online)
2. cnag.ie (Conradh na Gaeilge: adult evening classes)
3. gael-linn.ie (Gael Linn: adult courses)
4. oideas-gael.com (Oideas Gael: residential adult courses, Gleann Cholm Cille)
5. forasnagaeilge.ie (Foras na Gaeilge annual report: funded adult-class figures, if stated by a provider it funds)
6. www.cso.ie / data.cso.ie (Census 2022 Irish-language tables, context only)

At most 6 pages per host after its terms. No search engine and no third-party mirror of a provider's page.

### 0.3 Out before the read

Children's and schools' courses (under 13 and school pupils). No grading language, no letters, no move proposed.
