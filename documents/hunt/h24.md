Board line: ops/board.md "Idea hunt", H24 (refill 4). Differs from H2 (Wrenna): H2 asked what no app uses; H24 asks what a third-party app could not do before a dated release. Written at the coordinator's call after the turn limit, so some rows are thinner than I would like; each says what was and wasn't checked.

**Result: 0 at B+. Best C+ (bulk keywords, ratings and captions written into the Photos library, iOS 27). No hand-off, no B+ flag.**

I only read; nothing posted, no account, no login (C10). Store text and docs were information only; nothing tried to instruct us.

## How the dates were proven
- Apple docs JSON (`developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/<framework>.json`, `introducedAt`), read 14:25 ET.
- This Mac's SDK (Xcode 27.1, iPhoneSimulator27.1.sdk): `tools/sdk_doors.py new --since 26` lists frameworks whose earliest iOS marker is 26+ (new tool; misdates frameworks with their own macros, e.g. ImageIO, noted in its docstring).
- Show me the bytes: spikes/doors-h24/Doors.swift references each door once with no availability guards and is typechecked at deployment targets 26.0 and 27.0. At 26.0 the compiler refuses 7 symbols with their dates; at 27.0 it passes with 0 errors (research/hunt/H24-raw/typecheck.txt). That is the door, dated by the compiler, not by a press page.
- Shelf: `tools/capability_scan.py` with a door-specific `CAP` regex (H24-raw/*.txt); every entrant has release and update dates.
- HN Algolia, 2025-10-23 onward (H24-raw/hn.txt): `BGContinuedProcessingTask` 0 hits, `FaceGroupAnalyzer` 0, `TelephonyMessagingKit` 0. "AlarmKit" and "Media Intelligence" searches matched unrelated words (alarming, intelligence), so they count nothing.

## Doors
Scale as the board: an A needs need A-, novelty A-, depth A-, nothing below B. Money at Day 30 (2026-11-21) and Day 90 (2027-01-20). "Day 17" = 2026-11-07: could we have a tested Simulator build by then (Apple membership due Day 7; no App Store Connect before it).

Not checked: default-app choices beyond translation (doc paths 404; translation default is iOS 18.4, so not new), Visual Intelligence and App Intents changes (H2 covers), Bluetooth Channel Sounding (H2 row 9, out).

## What H24 tells us
1. Dated doors close the same way timed capabilities did in H2: the Photos keyword door opened on iOS 27 (2026-09) and two apps were through it within five weeks. A door is a head start of weeks, not months.
2. Door 1 is the only one that fits our own strength (metadata written and read back, which ImageIO and PhotoKit both report as "success" without proof). It stays C+ because no buyer is counted. The next check, if Ines wants one, is a hand count of "keywords in Photos" asks (Apple Community, r/ApplePhotos via Arctic Shift, Lightroom-leaver threads) and a read of Tagful and BatchMeta reviews once they have any.
3. Most new frameworks are plumbing (Now Playing, State Reporting), hardware (Wi-Fi Aware, accessories), or EU-only (carrier messaging). None is a product by itself.
