### 0.2 <span title="One product idea inside a lane. C1 is the lane&#x27;s first idea.">Concepts</span> (cap 4), fixed now: a computation on the buyer's own export, on device

- **C1 one media year.** Merge two or more of S1-S5 into one timeline: hours and titles per medium per week, the year in one report. Output words: "year in review", "wrapped", "combined stats".
- **C2 taste eras.** Change-point segmentation of the user's own listening or watching history into eras, each with its top artists, genres or directors. Output words: "eras", "taste over time", "listening history timeline".
- **C3 dropped and finished.** Series completion from watched-episode history (S3, S5 playlists): which shows were dropped, at which episode, and pace per show. Output words: "dropped shows", "completion", "watch pace".
- **C4 watch time from timestamps.** Session reconstruction from a history that has start times and no durations (S5; S1 dates): hours per day, per channel or per title. Output words: "watch time", "time spent", "how many hours".
