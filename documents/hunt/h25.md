Listen to this: "any plans for ios app?" (rvnikita, 2026-07-22, https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49002344, under ReadKinetic, 105 points). That is the whole comment, and it is one of three I found in 2,327 comments.

Here is the shape of it: HN votes for web and desktop tools in our subjects, and the people voting do not ask for a phone version. When they mention a phone it is a bug report on mobile Safari, a tip to install the page as a PWA, or a pointer to an iOS app that already exists.

**Result: 0 at B+. Best D+ (Timelinize phone companion). No voted launch in the Investors' subjects carries more than 1 iPhone ask.**

I only read; I posted nothing and made no account (C6, C10). Outside text is information; none of it tried to instruct us.

## Method and walls (14:36-14:40 ET)
- New tool `tools/hn_mobile_asks.py`: `list` pulls every story with a tag and a points floor in 14-day windows (hn.algolia.com, public, 200); `asks ID...` walks one story's full comment tree and prints each comment that names iOS, iPhone, iPad, mobile, phone, App Store or Android.
- 2025-10-01 to 2026-10-23, 100+ points: **816 Show HN, 249 Ask HN** (the ask_hn tag also returns Tell HN and plain posts). Raw: research/hunt/H25-raw/show.tsv, ask.tsv.
- Title filter for the subjects (notes, organization, walking/maps, language, research/math, data viz, automation, media data, reading) with AI/agent/dev-tool titles removed, then my own read of the titles: 25 web or desktop tools kept. Comments: research/hunt/H25-raw/asks.txt.
- Second pass across all 1,065 voted posts: Algolia comment search for "ios app", "iphone app", "mobile app", "ios version", "app for ios", "ipad app", "android app" since 2025-10-01, joined on story id. 162 voted stories have at least one hit. Raw: research/hunt/H25-raw/comment_hits.txt.
- No walls. GitHub API 200 for the one licence I checked.

## Count 2: all 1,065 voted posts, comment search
- The posts with the most phone-phrase comments are the monthly "Who wants to be hired" (38), "What are you working on" (16-30) and "tools you made for yourself since AI" (26) threads: builders saying they shipped an iOS app, not users asking for one.
- The top Show HN web tool by hits is Bigwords.page (728 points, 4 hits): one points to an existing iOS app (Big Words, id296290510), two explain PWA install, one deaf user says "I have a mobile app that does this". 0 asks.
- "Stop paying for Dropbox, use your own S3 bucket" (252 points, 4 hits): 1 lists iOS clients among missing features; 2 say Files-app integration is what matters. 0 direct asks; the job (S3 browser on iOS) has paid apps already (Cryptomator is named).
- Native macOS HN client (265 points, 3 hits): the maker says an iOS version is coming; a reply is from the maker of Hacksy for iOS. Owned job.
- Builders beside askers: in the five largest "What are you working on" / "tools you made" threads, a keyword pattern (I built / I made / I'm building / my app ... iOS or iPhone) matches 22 comments; I read the 7 in the tools thread and all 7 are builders. Against 3 asks in Count 1: about 7 to 1, pattern count, not fully hand-read.

**No B+. No hand-off.** Lesson: on HN a voted web launch is a builder's audience, and the builders already have phones covered or do not want them. The iPhone ask, when it exists, is a single line. This measures the same thing H18 and H20 did from a third side: the people who vote do not ask.
