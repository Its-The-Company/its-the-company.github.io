### 0.3 Sources and bars (R31-1, packet)

- Allowed: each rival's own website (pricing, plans, help pages naming the paid tier); the developer's own public revenue or paid-user statements on its own host.

Letterboxd and Goodreads hosts (H245 0.3, H167). www.last.fm pages other than those H245 read (its terms bar "any automated use of the system, such as scripts", H245 section 1). trakt.tv (redirect refused, H245).
- URL finding: WebSearch may be used after this commit only to locate a host's terms page and pricing page; a snippet is never evidence and is not quoted. Queries are logged in H250-raw/urls.tsv.
- Every request goes through terms_guard.fetch, in H250-raw/bin/gate.py, which calls rules_first.require() and must pass tools/lane_fetch_check.py.
- Per host, in order: /robots.txt (saved), the terms page (saved as text), then at most two pricing or help paths, only if robots allows the path and the terms are cleared.
- Terms cleared: at least 1,500 characters of text (TERMS_FLOOR) and 0 automation hits (robot, automat, scrap, spider, crawl, data mining, harvest); or the hits weighed by hand from the saved text, with the sentences quoted in section 1 and a row in H250-raw/terms/weighed.tsv, before any other page on that host. A redirect to another host is refused, body discarded. A clause barring commercial visits or any script use is not cleared.
