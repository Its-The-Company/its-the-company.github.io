H41 is the home machine cycle listener (Ines). The question: can a phone-side detector find a machine's on/off cycles in a recording, offline, to the section 3 bar? Feasibility check only; no product is built (#23, 2026-10-23 17:33).

## 0. Sources, queries, <span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">caps</span> (committed 2026-10-25 05:04 ET, before any fetch)

- **Recordings.** Wikimedia Commons audio files (File: namespace), read through the Commons API (commons.wikimedia.org/w/api.php), licence from each file's own extmetadata (LicenseShortName, Artist) and quoted per file. Only CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA. NC, ND, unclear origin, or "AI"/"generated"/"synthesized" in the description: out. Second source only if Commons gives too few: Freesound, after its robots and terms are read through tools/terms_guard.py; if its terms bar scripted reads or downloads need a login, it is out. ESC-50: out (section 3).
- **Queries (Commons, filetype:audio).** refrigerator; fridge; compressor; boiler; furnace; heat pump; air conditioner; sump pump; water pump; well pump; dehumidifier. At most 11 searches, 20 results each, 40 file reads, 12 downloads, each under 8 MB; total raw audio kept out of git (H253-raw/audio/ is gitignored) if over 3 MB.
- **Kinds.** Furnace or boiler, heat pump or air conditioner, sump or well pump, fridge compressor; section 3 needs 5 recordings, 3 kinds, 20 cycles.
- **Labels first.** Cycle start/stop marked from a code-drawn spectrogram and RMS envelope (bin/spectro.py, numpy only), committed in labels.tsv before the detector is written.
- **Detector.** Swift package, test target only, Accelerate. Method: short-time energy in bands (Hann-windowed FFT, Oppenheim and Schafer), adaptive noise floor by running minimum statistics (Martin 2001, "Noise power spectral density estimation based on optimal smoothing and minimum statistics", IEEE TSAP 9(5)), hysteresis thresholds with minimum on/off durations (double-threshold endpoint detection, Rabiner and Sambur 1975, BSTJ 54(2)). No generative model, no third-party package.
- **Pass (section 3).** Start and stop each within 5 s of the label on at least 90 percent of cycles; at most 1 false cycle per hour of recording. One bug-fix pass allowed.
- **Stop rule.** If fewer than 5 recordings / 3 kinds / 20 cycles exist under these licences, the lane stops "not finished: too few licensed recordings", no move, Open list.
- Turn budget 10.
