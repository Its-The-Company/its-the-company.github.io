### 0.2 Concepts, fixed now (cap 4): computation on the buyer's own input, output

- **K1 (F1) survey effort and richness from my own notebook.** Input: the user's own dated visit lists (site, date, minutes, species and counts), typed or imported from a CSV. Algorithm: per site, a species accumulation curve over visits, a Chao1 / ICE richness estimate with its interval, and an effort-standardised count (per hour, per visit) so seasons and observers compare. Output: per-site sheet "you have probably seen N of about M species here; the next visit most likely adds X".
- **K2 (F2) walk programme builder.** Input: the club's own route list (length, grade, start point) and each leader's dates free. Algorithm: constraint scheduling over a season: one leader per walk, no route repeated inside its spacing, a grade mix per month, leaders' load balanced, start points rotated. Output: a printable programme and per-leader calendar, re-solved when one leader drops out.
- **K3 (F3) tutor's review planner across students.** Input: the tutor's own word lists per student, with the lesson each word was taught and how the student did. Algorithm: per student, a spaced-review schedule (half-life regression per item) folded into the tutor's lesson calendar, with each lesson's review list capped to fit its minutes. Output: "Tuesday 18:00, Ana: these 12 words, these 3 new".
- **K4 (F4) shelf plan for my collection.** Input: the user's own catalogue (title, sort key, width or count per item) and shelf lengths. Algorithm: ordered bin packing (keep the sort order, fill shelves, leave a growth gap per section), and the moves list from the current shelving to the new one (fewest moves). Output: a shelf-by-shelf plan and a move list.
