- **Window.** Target local dates 2026-09-21 to 2026-10-18 (28 days). Day 1 for date D comes from the cycle issued D-1 12Z; day 3 from D-3 12Z. So 30 issue days per product (2026-09-18 to 2026-10-17).

Backfill = 30 issue files for one station; one day = one file. Whole-file figure is the measured one; a Range-read path is reported only if measured.

- **<span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">Caps</span>.** 3 stations, 2 products, 60 fetches (terms_locate fetches counted in), turn budget 10.
