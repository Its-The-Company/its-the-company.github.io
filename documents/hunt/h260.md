## 0. Sources, datasets, computations, codes (committed before any fetch)

**What a dataset must be.** A US federal work (17 U.S.C. 105) from NOAA, USGS, NASA, NPS, FAA or Census, published as downloadable files (not a keyed API), whose public-domain status is stated on the agency's own page, read and quoted in section 1. Each is joined to a phone sensor or the buyer's own input; the output must be one no rival's own page claims.

**The ten datasets (fixed, this order).** Size is read in section 2 from the agency's own listing (Content-Length or listing size); nothing over 10 MB is downloaded, and nothing downloaded is committed except files under 1 MB.

## 3. Concepts (cap 4, from P datasets only)

- **C1 Raindrop path (D3, D10).** Stand anywhere, or replay a walk: the app traces where rain falling there flows, stream by stream to the sea, offline, and marks each watershed divide the walk crossed. Size: national WBD 2.78 GB zipped; a phone needs HUC12 polygons and a flow network simplified per region, size unread. Rival: River Runner, a free website (memory, unverified); no priced app read (#53).
- **C2 Bedrock under your photos (D4, D10).** Reads the GPS of the buyer's photo library and walks; gives the rock unit and its age under each photo and a timeline of the library by bedrock age. Size: SGMC 435.9 MB zipped, simplification unread. Rival: Rockd, free location geology (memory, unverified), not known to read a photo library.
- **C3 Magnetic residual map (D1).** Records the magnetometer on a walk or around a room and maps the residual against the WMM's expected field there and then: where the compass can be trusted, where buried iron or wiring bends it. Size: small (coefficient file). Rival: compass and "metal detector" apps show raw microtesla (memory, unverified); none known to subtract the model. The hard part is the phone's own hard- and soft-iron offset, which the residual must not confuse with the place.
- **C4 Named places walked (D10).** Each walk's list of named features passed (summits, gaps, streams, springs), with a lifetime count. Size: 0.0-2.7 MB per state zipped. Rival: Wandrer counts streets, priced (memory, unverified).
