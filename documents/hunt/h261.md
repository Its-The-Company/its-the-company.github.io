## 0. Rules, written before the first fetch

`python3 rules/lookup.py 7` was run in this session at 05:47 ET, before any article was named. Kept out: health, sleep, diet and body measures (medical); Benford-style fraud checks and anything priced in money (finance); no child or school use. Commandment 6: every sketch runs on the device on the reader's own input; nothing is uploaded.

### 0.1 Order and stop rule

1. Terms first. `tools/terms_locate.py` on wikimedia.org and foundation.wikimedia.org; the Terms of Use and the User-Agent rule are quoted from the pages it finds, or from pages linked from the REST API's own documentation page (a link followed, never a guessed path). Live robots through terms_guard for every host before its first page.
2. If wikimedia.org's live robots bars /api/rest_v1/, or the terms are unfound, unread (under the 1,500-character floor), or carry a clause barring automated reading: stop with a G2 line. No pageview call, no seeds.
3. Else pageview calls: `/api/rest_v1/metrics/pageviews/per-article/en.wikipedia/all-access/user/<title>/daily/20251025/20261024`, one per article, in the order of 0.2, with our User-Agent naming the company and a contact URL, 1.5 s apart.
4. Rival pages last, with fetches left, only on hosts whose terms terms_locate finds and that carry no automation bar.

### 0.2 Articles (18 of the 40 <span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">cap</span>) and sketches (8; 4 concepts at most go forward)

### 0.3 Seed rule (fixed now)

- Per article: median daily views and the coefficient of variation (CV) of daily views over the 365 days.
- An article is "high and steady" if its median is 1,000 or more and its CV is 0.6 or less.
- A sketch goes forward if one or more of its articles is high and steady; at most 4, by the highest median among its steady articles. Ties to the earlier sketch.
- A sketch that goes forward is a concept in section 2 with its pageviews beside it, the computation, and a priced rival's own page or "no priced rival found" (a <span title="Our arithmetic estimate of what the idea would earn by day 30 (the $200 deadline) or day 90. It never raises a grade.">Day 30</span> doubt, not a pass).

### 0.4 Hosts (no new host after this commit)

wikimedia.org, foundation.wikimedia.org, meta.wikimedia.org (only if the docs link there), and the four rival hosts in 0.2. No store, HN, GitHub-demand or Reddit request.
