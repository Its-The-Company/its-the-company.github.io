Order per host: lane_preflight (robots live, then tools/terms_locate.py; no guessed path). A host whose terms are unfound is a gap: no rulebook page read. Where terms are read and carry no bar on automated reads, one rulebook page is fetched, a page linked from the host's own sitemap or terms-located pages (not a guessed path); where none is linked, the rulebook is unread. Fetches counted by script in H268-raw/fetch-log.tsv; stop at 25.

Codes: R = rulebook computation quoted from the body's own page; M = hand or spreadsheet method shown on that permitted page; U = unread (terms gap, refusal, cap, or no linked page); T = terms bar automated reads. Each <span title="One product idea inside a lane.
