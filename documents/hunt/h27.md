## Why they are absent
Two reasons, and every row is one of them.
1. **iOS forbids the job.** Tasker, MacroDroid, Automate, KWGT, KLWP, FolderSync, Button Mapper, Pano Scrobbler, StayFree's raw stats. These are paid on Android because Android lets an app reach the system. On iOS the constraint makes the option impossible, and I will not draw a version that quietly works around it.
2. **iOS allows it and the shelf is full.** Readers, read-aloud, maps, trackers, podcasts, habits, notes. The Android developer stayed home; iOS developers already sell the job.

## Result
No concept at B+ in both letters. No hero drawn (one hero only for a B+). Best: field map (C- / C+); widget maker sells best (D- / B-) and is not novel. The lane gives one finding: a paid Android power app with 10,000+ ratings is absent from iOS either because iOS forbids the job or because iOS developers already sell it. I found no third case in 145 rows plus 48 hand-picked pages. The lane is closed for me; reopening it needs a new door (see Idris's H24) that turns a type 1 row into a buildable one.
