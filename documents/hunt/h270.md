### 0.2 Concepts (cap 4), fixed before any read

- **C1 Dial Layout.** Phone GPS gives latitude and longitude; compass and level give the wall's declination and inclination (or a horizontal plate). Output: hour lines with longitude and equation-of-time correction, a full-size printable template tiled on A4 or Letter, and the hour lines drawn over the camera view of the wall. Buyer: hobby dial makers, garden and house owners.
- **C2 Glass Cut List.** The buyer's own pattern (a drawn cartoon, or a photo of the opening with one measured edge) becomes pieces by colour, sheet nesting with a waste figure, lead came or copper foil length, solder estimate, and a full-size cartoon tiled for print. Buyer: hobby stained-glass makers.
- **C3 Signature Imposer.** The buyer's own PDF becomes imposed sheets for folio, quarto or octavo signatures at a chosen paper size, with creep, blank padding, sewing-station templates and duplex order for a home printer. Buyer: hobby bookbinders and zine makers.
- **C4 Frame Cut Sheet.** The buyer's own span, pitch, timber sizes and joint choices become rafter, brace and post lengths with layout marks, a board-foot takeoff and stick weight. No load or code figures. Buyer: hobby and small-shop timber framers.

Data: each computation runs from published formulas (spherical astronomy, geometry), not a dataset, so no data licence applies; the lane says so per concept rather than quoting one.
