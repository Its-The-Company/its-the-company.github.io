### 0.1 Trades considered (<span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">cap</span> 8)

1. Arborists (trunk diameter at breast height and tree height, then species tables)
2. Tile and flooring estimators (room area, then layout, waste and box tables)
3. Picture framers (artwork size, then mat, moulding and glass cut sheet)
4. Sign makers (viewing distance at the site, then letter-height legibility tables)
5. Home inspectors: out, section 0 line 2 (code pass/fail).
6. Painters (wall area, then coverage-per-gallon tables): folded into C2's shape, no own concept.
7. Fence and deck builders (run length, then post spacing and board tables): no concept, same shape as C2.
8. Theatre set builders (flats and platforms): no published table found in my own knowledge to name before a read; no concept.

### 0.2 Concepts (cap 4), fixed before any read

Each names buyer, computation on the buyer's own input, data, and a rival own-page price to read.

- **C1 Tree card (arborists).** Buyer: a working arborist or tree surveyor. Input: LiDAR depth plus camera frame of a trunk at 1.37 m and of the whole tree. Computation: DBH from the fitted trunk circle, height by angle and range, crown spread; then a published species table (allometric volume or biomass, i-Tree class) for the card. Rival own page: Arboreal Forest (arboreal.se) pricing.
- **C2 Floor take-off (tile and flooring).** Buyer: a flooring or tile installer quoting a job. Input: a LiDAR room scan. Computation: net area by room, tile layout from a chosen start line, cuts, waste by pattern and box counts from a waste table. Rivals' own pages: magicplan (magicplan.app) pricing; Measure Square (measuresquare.com) pricing.
- **C3 Frame cut sheet (picture framers).** Buyer: a small framing shop or a home framer. Input: a photo of the artwork with LiDAR range for scale. Computation: artwork size, mat window and borders, moulding length with rabbet and mitre allowance, glass and backing sizes. Rival own page: FrameReady (frameready.com) pricing.
- **C4 Letter height from where you stand (sign makers).** Buyer: a sign maker at a client's site. Input: LiDAR or camera range from the viewer's spot to the sign face. Computation: viewing distance and angle, then letter height from a published legibility table. Table source: United States Sign Council Foundation (usscfoundation.org), licence to quote. Priced rival: none named before the read.
