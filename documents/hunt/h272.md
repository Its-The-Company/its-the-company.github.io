### 0.3 <span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">Caps</span> and fetch counting

- 5 collections (LoC, AIC, the Met, Rijksmuseum, Smithsonian), 3 concepts, 20 fetches.
- On the 20: every locate fetch (tools/terms_locate.py via tools/lane_preflight.py), every terms or licence page, every data call. Not on the 20: the one live robots.txt GET per host per ET day that terms_guard makes (disclosed in section 1, one line per host).
- Locate budget: preflight runs host by host in the table's order after www.loc.gov. When locate fetches reach 8, the hosts not yet located are "not located: lane cap" (G2) and get no other fetch.
- Every request goes through terms_guard.fetch in H272-raw/bin/gate.py, which calls rules_first.require("H272") and must pass tools/lane_fetch_check.py. Company User-Agent. At least 2 s between requests, 7 s on loc.gov. A redirect to another host is refused and its body discarded.
- URL finding for licence pages, API endpoints and rivals: links on a fetched page first; else WebSearch, logged in H272-raw/urls.tsv. A snippet is never evidence and is not quoted. Terms pages only through terms_locate (ruling 05:07), or the H193 reuse above.
- Terms cleared as in H250 0.3: text at or above TERMS_FLOOR with the automation sentences weighed by hand and quoted in section 1. A clause barring scripted access or commercial reuse of the metadata is not cleared; that host's data is G2.

### 0.4 Codes (per collection, from its own pages and a sample of at most 10 records)

- L: licence of the metadata, quoted. `CC0` / `PD` (no known restrictions, US government) / `mixed` (per-record rights) / `unread`.
- G: place in the record. `coord` (latitude and longitude) / `text` (a place name) / `none`. With the sample fill: n of k records carrying it.
- D: date in the record. `day` / `year` / `range` / `none`, with fill n of k.
- K: key needed for the API: `no` / `yes (sign-up, barred)`.
- A concept is only built on a field whose fill is at least 5 of 10 in the lane's own sample, or 5 of the records the sample returned if fewer.

### 0.5 Seeds, written before any read (a seed with a failed field test makes no concept)

- S1 (walk): records with coordinates near the buyer (LoC: Historic American Buildings Survey and similar, if their records carry coordinates). Field test: G = coord in at least 5 of 10.
- S2 (place): objects made in the places the buyer has been (the buyer's own photo locations or a typed list of trips), at the Met or AIC. Field test: G = text or coord with at least 5 of 10, and a public-domain flag on the record.
- S3 (date): records dated to a day (LoC dated photographs or newspapers), joined to the buyer's own dates. Field test: D = day in at least 5 of 10.
- Each concept row names: the buyer; the computation on the buyer's own input (T1 v2 question); the data and its licence quoted from its own page; a priced rival's own page or "no priced rival found" (a <span title="Our arithmetic estimate of what the idea would earn by day 30 (the $200 deadline) or day 90.
