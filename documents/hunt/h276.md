If the index shows it over 5 GB, it is streamed through one connection and never kept. If under 5 GB, it may be saved whole **outside the repo** (/tmp/h276/dl/), one connection, then read locally and deleted at the end of the <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Only counts and samples under 1 MB are committed. Stop rule from R2: if the transfer projects past 10 hours, stop with a "too slow" gap and the rate.

0.3 What is counted, main namespace (ns 0) pages only, per language section (level-2 heading, e.g. ==German==):
- **Lemma with an inflection table**: a language section that <span title="What keeps an idea below the top: the cap that applies to it now.">holds</span> a heading named Declension, Conjugation or Inflection (any level 3 to 5) followed by at least one template call before the next heading.
- **Forms per lemma**: the dump holds wikitext, not expanded tables, so a table's cell count cannot be read without running Wiktionary's Lua modules. The proxy is the number of distinct ns-0 pages whose same-language section points back to the lemma through a form-of template (inflection of, infl of, plural of, any "... participle of", any "... form of" except alternative, obsolete, archaic, misspelling, nonstandard, dated, rare, informal, eye dialect, abbreviation, short, clipping and spelling forms). This is a lower bound on forms that have their own page, not the table size. Median and 90th percentile per language, over lemmas with tables.
- Language codes for the forms join come from the form-of template's first argument; the name-to-code map is taken from the same dump (the most common code in {{head\|xx\|...}} or {{xx-...}} headword calls in each language's sections).

0.4 **Falsifier, written before the run**: a language enters a <span title="One product idea inside a lane. C1 is the lane&#x27;s first idea.">concept</span> only with **5,000 or more lemmas carrying tables**. Under that, the data is too thin to ship for that language. If no language reaches 5,000, the lane writes "0 concepts" for want of data (the seed failed the data test, not the buyer test) and no concept is drafted.

0.5 Concepts (if any language passes) compute on the learner's own text (paste or camera OCR on device), per the packet. Rival and demand cells: R34-3 items 1 and 3; a WebSearch census may name rivals, prices only from their own pages, at most 3 rival hosts. Licence: Wiktionary text is GFDL and CC BY-SA 4.0 (dumps legal page); any shipped table data carries attribution and share-alike, stated per concept.

0.6 Turn budget 8. Every time from `date`.

**Falsifier (0.4): 43 languages reach 5,000 lemmas with tables**, so it does not fire for them: Finnish, German, Polish, Romanian, Russian, Latin, Macedonian, Swedish, Czech, Serbo-Croatian, Hungarian, Ukrainian, Greek, Dutch, Ancient Greek, Arabic, Danish, Armenian, Turkish, Hindi, Irish, Icelandic, Georgian, Bulgarian, Japanese, Old English, Italian, Latvian, Slovak, Azerbaijani, Spanish, Lithuanian, Sanskrit, French, Tamil, Belarusian, Ingrian, Albanian, Pannonian Rusyn, Faroese, Portuguese, Estonian, Old Armenian.

What the forms column can and cannot say. Most medians are 0 or 1. This is the 0.3 proxy (separate pages pointing back by a form-of template), and it undercounts badly: Russian lemma links carry stress marks the page title lacks, Finnish forms rarely get their own page, and language-specific form templates without a code argument are not caught. **The table size itself is not in the dump**: the tables are template calls expanded by Wiktionary's Lua modules. Any app needs those forms expanded, either by running MediaWiki with Scribunto and the modules offline (heavy but in our control), or from an expanded source whose terms are unread (the Wikimedia Enterprise HTML dumps on the same host's /other/ path, or kaikki.org, a third party). That is the lane's main capability gap (G2).

Why the gap exists, as far as I can tell: the forms live inside template code, not as data, so the people who build learner apps on Wiktionary lean on one third-party extract or skip morphology. That is a guess from the shape of the data, not a read; the rival census is the test.
