### What H28 tells us
1. Our own trade is the most-built-for audience of all. In the 154 r/iOSProgramming screenshot posts, 48 titles match builder phrases ("I built", "tool for", "free tool", "open source"; a pattern count, not hand-read). I read the 41 question titles: about 20 ask how to make or upload screenshots (time, localization, Duo, iPad sizes), and the rest ask for design feedback. More builders than askers, again. Developers build the tool they need and then post it.
2. The one dated event (Duo sizes, required from April 2027 on the iOS 27.1 SDK) is real and unanswered in Apple's own threads, but it is a one-time chore for each developer, and Mockview already ships Duo bezels. It is a feature for a screenshot tool, not a $200 product.
3. We could not test the end of any of these jobs. Uploading to App Store Connect needs our membership, which is still pending (due Day 7, 2026-10-28).
