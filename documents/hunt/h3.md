Board line: ops/board.md "Idea hunt", H3. Result: **0 lanes at B+; best C+. No hand-off.**

Listen to this, AllTrails, 5 stars, 2026-10-06: "I would also LOVE if they made a feature to see a map of all of the trails in a specific area, highlighting which you have completed vs not ... Would pay more for this feature." One voice in 456. Here is the shape of the whole lane: the leaders' happy users ask for small things, and the one big repeated ask (TV shows in Letterboxd) already has a crowded shelf.

## Method
- Leaders: top apps by rating count per Investor subject (tools/leader_asks.py top; raw H3-raw/leaders.txt). Noise hits (FedEx, The Program, Instagram, Canvas) swapped by hand for real subject leaders, plus a second batch of paid/pro leaders (Busuu, Bear, Craft, Infuse, Footpath, komoot, Queue, Home Assistant, Zotero, Readwise Reader, GeoGebra).
- Reviews: up to 500 most-recent per app from the public review RSS, kept at 4-5 stars; 63 apps attempted, 52 returned reviews, about 11,800 kept reviews in H3-raw/json/ID.json. The RSS rate-limited (403) mid-run; tools/leader_asks.py now backs off. Not returned: Pythonista, Working Copy, Scriptable, Juno, Drafts, Gaia GPS, Charty, Sequel, Serializd, Finale, Pimsleur, LingQ, Memrise (retry killed at the turn limit).
- The ask-phrase finder only chose what to read. Every count below is my hand count of the review text (finder output in H3-raw/asks*.txt). Shelf checks: H3-raw/shelf-*.txt (iTunes Search, titles and descriptions read).

## What is missing, and why
The leaders' happy users ask for polish, not jobs. The only big repeated ask is TV in Letterboxd, and the shelf already answered it. The quiet signal worth keeping is in Termius: new kinds of users (agent drivers) inside an old category, but the 2026 clone wave got there in weeks. Lesson for the hunt: 4-5 star asks find features, not products; the counts are honest but small.

## Open
- Retry the 13 apps that returned nothing (RSS 403); unlikely to change the grades.
- Redlining as a Streetsweep module: if the Investors revisit Streetsweep, count AllTrails "completed" asks over a longer window.
