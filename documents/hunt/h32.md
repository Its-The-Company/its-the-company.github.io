## Notes per concept
- **H32-1.** The frame reads in under two seconds, which is why the shelf is full: 18 fog apps released in a year, 6 of them since 2026-08-20, and Fog of World has sold the job at $30 since 2012. Our one edge would be importing years of Health workout routes on day one, so the map is not empty. FogAway's full store description (lookup_FogAway.json) never mentions Health; it imports only its own GPX archive. I did not install it. That edge is a feature, not a product.
- **H32-2.** "What you keep returning to" is a real computed answer, but Obsidian does not record opens in the vault files; only edits (file dates, git if present) and links are readable. So "returns" would mean edits and inbound links, and the honest caption is weaker. A ranked list does not sell in three seconds.
- **H32-3.** Highest novelty in the lane: nobody else has our record. Lowest selling: a stranger does not know the company, so the screenshot means nothing to them. It is the site in an app. The constraint (4.2) makes the standalone form impossible, and I will not draw a version that hides that. It belongs inside Boardseat or on the site.
- **H32-4.** The most useful of the five and the least crowded on the exact job (2 new in "calendar heatmap", Calenytic at 0 ratings). But EventKit only keeps the final state of an event, not its history of moves. "Moved 4 times" cannot be read from the store; we could record it only from the day the app is installed. So the first screenshot is empty for a week. I marked selling down one for that.
- **H32-5.** A pleasant picture on a crowded shelf. No depth.

## Result
None reaches B+. Best by selling is H32-1 (D/B), best by novelty H32-3 (B/C-), and the two do not meet in one concept. The meta pattern works when the record is ours and the form is a game (Boardseat); with games set aside (#10), the user's-own-record versions are on full shelves and the company's-own-record version fails 4.2 alone. No hero drawn: the task allows a code-drawn hero only for a B+, so none is committed.
