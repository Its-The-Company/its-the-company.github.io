## What would move the cycle listener to B+

Need B- and Day 30 money C are the two letters holding it. Neither moves from more counting in r/hvacadvice; Wrenna has read that venue to the floor. Two things could:

1. **Discovery.** A store phrase that buyers type. H41 searched 14 phrases and none returned a listener; that proves an empty shelf, not search volume. Apple's search popularity needs App Store Connect (not active). The honest proxy is the size of the thermostat-runtime audience: the reviews of Sensi, Resideo and Ecobee that ask "why does my furnace keep turning on and off". If 1 in 50 low reviews asks it, the buyer is in the store already.
2. **The capture.** Whether one overnight recording can be segmented into stages on a phone. That is a fixture test Idris can build with code-made audio (C9 allows audio synthesized by code from mathematical expressions; no recording needed): an inducer hum, a click, a burner roar and a blower, mixed with room noise at known times, and an onset detector scored against the known times.

Both are on the board as refill 7 lines. Neither builds product code (#23 17:33): one is a review count, the other a research spike on synthetic signals.
