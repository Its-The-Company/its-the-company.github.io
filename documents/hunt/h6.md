Board line: ops/board.md "Idea hunt", H6. Gigs and listings that sell a computed deliverable (a design, plan, layout, schedule, conversion, check) on Fiverr, PeoplePerHour and the Freelancer.com API, plus vendor design services like Rain Bird's (H5). Keep only deliverables an on-device algorithm could produce; <span title="The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.">shelf</span> check by category phrase; every entrant dated.

Listen to this: "I don't use either a Mac or a Windows PC as I constantly travel for work and only have my iPhone and iPad ... it's only really about getting it into the right format for a paperback." (Scrivener forum, 2024-12-14, https://forum.literatureandlatte.com/t/compiling-for-self-publishing-without-a-computer/144635). And this: "I want to try CMYK process but I don't have access to photoshop for CMYK separation. I know I can't separate channels in procreate" (r/SCREENPRINTING, 2026-09-16, https://reddit.com/r/SCREENPRINTING/comments/1whtr46/). Here is the shape of it: people do pay strangers for computed deliverables, often and cheaply, and the biggest one (book interior formatting, 2,160 buyer reviews on three gigs) has had four new formatting tools in 2026, two of them on iPad. Where the iOS shelf is still empty (screen-print separations) the buyers are desktop shops and the web/desktop builders arrived first.

**Result: no <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Each lane has its own file.">lane</span> at B+. Best C+ twice (iPad print-interior typesetter; iPad screen-print separations). No hand-off.**

## Method
- `python3 tools/hunt.py check TERM` before screening, 2026-10-23 about 12:10 ET. NOT screened: color separation, screen print, book format, typeset, KDP, nutrition label, paint by number, string art, handwriting font, font, audiobook, ACX, kitchen layout, vectoriz, mesh, lego, patent, resume, wiring diagram, planogram, packing, pallet, dieline, packaging, spine. Already screened and skipped as lanes: floor plan (pass12), seating (pass6/7), sprinkler, Manual J, solar (H5), garden (pass13), timetable (synthesis), subtitle (H2), brick model (pass8), pattern grading and sewing (pass6/8), nutrition only as a Vision capability (H2).
- Fiverr: new `tools/gig_scan.py` reads the gig cards Fiverr embeds in its public search page. "rev" is the gig's own buyer-review count (`buying_review_rating_count`), so it is a floor on orders. The counts are lifetime and **undated**. Prices are the cheapest package ("starting at"). Raw output: research/hunt/H6-raw/fiverr-1.txt, fiverr-2.txt, fiverr.jsonl.
- PeoplePerHour: `gig_scan.py --pph` reads the /services/QUERY page (the ?q= form ignores the query). PPH shows seller-wide reviews, not sales per offer, so it gives supply and price only. Raw: H6-raw/pph-1.txt.
- Freelancer.com: `tools/job_market.py fetch --query`, 1 page each (377 active projects across 5 queries). I hand-read titles; descriptions only for the 6 book rows. Raw: H6-raw/freelancer-titles.txt.
- Shelf: `tools/appsearch.py` (iTunes Search, US), 34 phrases; descriptions read via the Lookup API for 17 possible entrants (H6-raw/descriptions-book.txt, shelf-1..5.txt).
- Demand reads: Scrivener's Discourse forum (`tools/discourse_asks.py`, 8 queries, 5 threads opened: H6-raw/scrivener-*.txt); r/SCREENPRINTING through the Arctic Shift archive API, 4 queries since 2025-10-23, 117 posts listed, titles and excerpts hand-read (H6-raw/arctic-screenprinting.txt). Reddit itself is 403 for us; not bypassed.

## Lane 1: iPad print-interior typesetter ("Vellum on iPad")
Who: self-published authors who write on an iPad (Scrivener iOS, Ulysses, Pages) and need a KDP/IngramSpark paperback PDF (trim, gutter by page count, mirrored margins, running heads, drop <span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">caps</span>, widows and orphans) plus an EPUB.
- <span title="How many people show, in their own words, that they want this.">Need</span>, counted: three Fiverr gigs carry 2,160 buyer reviews at $25 starting (undated, lifetime); PPH offers $20-130; 3 of 99 Freelancer titles. The iPad-specific need: 1 thread in two years on the Scrivener forum (the quote above); queries "kdp ipad", "print ipad kdp", "paperback ipad compile" since 2025-10-23 returned 0 topics. Free tools cover the basic job: Reedsy Studio "Typeset to PDF" free (https://reedsy.com/write-a-book), Kindle Create free (Mac/Windows).
- Incumbents: Vellum **$199.99** ebook tier, macOS 13+ only (https://vellum.pub/); Atticus **$147** one-time, a PWA "on all devices with a browser" (https://www.atticus.io/), so it already reaches an iPad.
- Entrants (dated): Novelium (iOS/iPad, 2026-06-26, 20 ratings, "Format manuscripts, prepare exports"); **Alhora** (iOS/iPad, 2026-10-09, 0 ratings: "Choose typography and page styles, customise chapter openings ... Preview your book as an ebook or printed edition ... EPUB and PDF exports and book checks"); Galley EPUB editor (2026-07-29, EPUB only); desktop Deckle (forum thread 2026-07-23, Typst engine, $107 early access / $197), Lacuna (thread 2026-06-02), OpusWriter (thread 2026-09-25, Electron). Forum: https://forum.literatureandlatte.com/t/deckle/154299, /t/lacuna-software/153952, /t/opuswriter/154784.
- Grades: need B- (paid human demand is real; the iPad buyer is 1 ask), novelty C+ (Alhora shipped 14 days ago), depth A- (Knuth-Plass line breaking, KDP preflight, EPUB3), money <span title="Our arithmetic estimate of what the idea would earn by day 30 (the $200 deadline) or day 90. It never raises a grade.">Day 30</span> C+ / Day 90 B- (about 8 sales at $29.99 make $200 net, but discovery against free Reedsy and a new Alhora). **Overall C+.**

## Lane 2: iPad screen-print separations
Who: artists who draw shirts in Procreate and small or garage screen-print shops: spot, index and simulated-process separations, halftone films at angle and LPI, white underbase with choke, printed to film by AirPrint.
- Need, hand-read on r/SCREENPRINTING since 2025-10-23 (117 posts listed, titles and excerpts read; deduplicated): **13 posts ask for separation software or an alternative** (e.g. AccuRIP thread 2026-08-06, 35 comments; "Ultra seps V3 ... $399 which seems very expensive" 2025-12-29; "how comes there hasn't been a new app that ... make it a one time purchase" 2026-02-06), **14 ask how to do a separation**, **3 offer to pay a human** (2026-03-24, 2026-06-25, 2026-09-30). iPad/Procreate users in that set: **2 read** (2026-03-05 "iPad Pro with procreate", 2026-09-16 the quote above); 3 more Procreate posts not opened. Fiverr: top four gigs 540 reviews at $5-10.
- Supply: iOS shelf empty for 6 phrases (colour separation, screen printing separation, spot color separation, simulated process, underbase, procreate screen print); halftone toys exist (Real Halftone 2, 2022; Pressed: Risograph & Halftone, 2026-06-30). But **7 web/desktop builders posted in the same subreddit in 12 months**: InkSplit (web, 2025-11-11), LCDuotone (free, 2025-12-24), an Illustrator separation plug-in beta (2026-02-11), Reveal open-source Photoshop plug-in (2026-03-07), another Photoshop plug-in (2026-05-26), an AI separation tool (2026-07-08), a free separation and mock-up web app (2026-09-15). Builders 7 to software askers 13.
- Grades: need B- (overall) but C for iPad, novelty B (first on iPad; the method is crowded elsewhere), depth B+ (colour-space clustering to ink set, halftone screens without moire, underbase choke and trapping), money Day 30 C / Day 90 C+ (shops run Adobe because clients send .ai files, thread 2026-10-16). **Overall C+.**

## What I learned
- Fiverr's public search page carries per-gig review counts in JSON; one session gave 13 queries before a 403. Stop there; spread queries across sessions.
- Lifetime gig reviews are undated: they prove people pay, not that they pay now. Pair each with a dated forum count before grading need above B-.
- The biggest paid deliverables (formatting, labels, fonts, vectors) are exactly where the 2026 clone wave already landed on iOS. Paid-to-a-human demand is visible to every builder; it does not find a gap by itself.
