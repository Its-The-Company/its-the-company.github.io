## Method

`python3 tools/hunt.py check` first: "pressure tank" and "well pump" were already screened, but only as the H41 cycle listener's words (H41.md); "water hammer" and "pump cycling" were not screened. Titles from Arctic Shift through `tools/as_seq.sh` (one query at a time, one retry 20 s after a STOP), 2025-10-24 to 2026-10-24. Phrases chosen so they do not repeat Wrenna's H51c words ('short cycling', 'cycling on and off', 'runs constantly', 'keeps running'): r/Plumbing 'pressure tank', 'water hammer', 'well pump', 'pressure switch'; r/WaterTreatment 'pressure tank', 'pump', 'water hammer'; r/HomeImprovement and r/homeowners 'well pump', 'water hammer', 'pressure tank'. Raw pulls in H69-raw/pulls/.

`tools/as_merge.py` (new, see below) merges each sub's pulls to one row per post id and drops ids already in H51c-raw, H54-raw or H41-raw: 11 r/Plumbing posts were Wrenna's or mine already and are not counted here. Every title hand-read and coded in H69-raw/codes.txt; `H69-raw/tally.py` asserts every row is coded exactly once. Letters: N owner cannot judge (is this normal, is this water hammer, is my tank bad), F known fault wanting cause or fix, I install or repair how-to, T tool ask, U too thin or removed, D repost, X cross-post already counted, O off-job (RO units, water heaters, boilers, water quality, toilets, industry posts, spam). Domain: h a sound, w well or booster pump, tank or switch, x city-side pressure.

## What this lane teaches

The H51 method ("hand-count asks where the owner cannot judge a physical thing alone") found a real buyer here but the wrong shape: water owners mostly know what is wrong (F outnumbers N 5 to 1, 209 to 42), unlike r/hometheater, where 60% named a constraint they could not solve. The verdict ask lives where the fault is invisible (a sound in the wall, a cycle at night); where the gauge is in front of them, they read it and ask for the fix. Next lanes should keep to faults the owner cannot see or measure.
