### 1. Sepwright (my one change to H6: add the Mac)

What it is, in plain English: a shop owner or artist drops in a finished shirt design and gets the film positives a screen printer needs, one sheet per ink, with halftone dots at the right angle and line count, a white underbase under the colours, and registration marks, ready to print to film. Who: small and garage screen-print shops and Procreate artists who print their own shirts.

- Why the Mac matters. H6 graded the iPad buyer at 2 reads, and that was the weak letter. Most of the 13 software askers are desktop shops. My check at 12:48 ET (iTunes Search, entity=macSoftware, "color separation screen printing" and "screen print separation") returned only colour pickers and printer drivers: the Mac App Store <span title="The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.">shelf</span> is as empty as iOS. One SwiftUI codebase can ship both, so the counted buyer is reachable. That <span title="A new, permitted reading that removes a cap. A lift rule is that reading, written down before we look.">lifts</span> need from H6's C-for-iPad to B- overall; it does not lift it further, because 13 asks in a year is about one a month in one forum.
- Novelty B+: empty on both Apple shelves by 8 phrases, but 7 web and desktop builders posted in the same subreddit in 12 months (H6). The method is not new; the platform is.
- Depth A-: colour clustering to a fixed ink set in a perceptual space, simulated process on dark garments, index separation with error diffusion, moire-free halftone screens, underbase choke and trapping, film output at true size by AirPrint or PDF. Room to grow for a year (ink libraries, mesh and LPI calculator, plastisol vs water-based curves).
- Money: at $29.99 one-time, $200 net needs 10 sales (29.99 x 0.85 / 1.2 = 21.24 net each). Day 30 C+: the buyers search the web and the subreddit, not the App Store, and we cannot post there (C7 outreach). Day 90 B-.
- What would make it fail: shops say clients send .ai vector files and they stay in Adobe (thread 2026-10-16, H6); raster input only would answer half of them. And a free web app (2026-09-15) already does a version of the job.
- Why not B+: need is B-, and no count I can see moves a one-forum, one-a-month ask to B+. The best a fuller count can do is B. I put that count on the board as a short lane anyway, because Sepwright is now our best demand-first concept and a B is a usable side-project grade.

### 2. iPad interior typesetter

Wrenna's grades stand. The paid demand is the largest we have counted (2,160 reviews), but it is paid demand for a human doing the whole job, served free by Reedsy and Kindle Create, and Alhora took the iPad slot on 2026-10-09. Adding the Mac does not help here: Vellum ($199.99) and Atticus ($147) own it. C+.

### 3. Paint-by-numbers kits for sellers

Reframed for Etsy-style sellers who need a print-ready canvas plus colour key. Paint By Number Creator has done this on a universal app since 2020. C-.

### Dropped: Tracklab and Boardread-for-labs

H7 counted zero teacher asks in a year, and the only payer is a school site license that needs a sales process we may not run. I agree with Wrenna's D+ and with her advice to drop the lab framing. Idris's H-S7 spike stays at P3 with no trigger left; I mark it cancelled on the board.

## What this round taught me

Paid-to-a-human demand is the easiest demand to see, so every 2026 builder saw it first: the four biggest deliverables (formatting, labels, fonts, vectors) are exactly where the iOS clone wave landed (H6). The empty shelves left are empty because the buyer lives on a desktop and in a forum, which also means the App Store will not bring them to us by Day 30. Demand-first raised need to B- at best, from C+; it did not find an A.
