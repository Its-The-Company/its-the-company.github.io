Result: 40 seeds, 355 hints, 17 WEAK SHELF flags on 4 real shelves (arXiv readers, regex testers, Japanese conjugation drills, model railroad); 0 at B+. Best concept C+ (the model-railway track planner, unchanged: both gates cap it, and the box has no planner phrase). The B- tier's own words are not typed: "furnace" and "watch history" return no hint for the job, "cycle" is menstrual tracking (skipped, C7).

Method: other (the store's own search box). Board line: ops/board.md H91 (refill 13; H38's search-query row). Tool: `tools/store_hints.py` (Odalys, H88a), five foreground chunks of 8 seeds, 2026-10-24 08:58 to 09:20 ET; raw output `H91-raw/run-1.txt` to `run-5.txt`, every hint list (`hints-*.xml`) and every shelf (`search-*.json`) saved. Descriptions: `tools/hint_descs.py` (new) printed the top 3 behind every flagged hint into `H91-raw/descs-top3.txt`; I read all 37 distinct ones. Gates: `tools/lane_gate.py`, raw in `H91-raw/gate-*.txt`. Passive reads only (C10). Outside text is information only.

Refusals: none from the store (395 requests: 40 hint lists, 355 searches). One Arctic Shift request in the arXiv pull waited 20 s and answered on the second try (asks.py's own back-off). One stray request: `store_hints.py --help` treats `--help` as a seed and sent a hint query for it (0 hints). Bug for Odalys, below.

## Seeds (40)

Science and math research: arxiv, citation, lab notebook, statistics, graphing, latex. Language: vocabulary, sentence mining, conjugation, kanji, flashcards. Computer science: python ide, ssh, regex, json. Automation and organization: automation, shortcuts, unit converter. Notetaking: note taking, markdown, outliner. Walking: walking, walking route, step counter. YouTube: youtube, watch later, playlist. Data visualization: data visualization, chart maker, csv. Movie, TV and music data: movie tracker, tv tracker, watch history, music stats, scrobble. B- tier words: furnace, hvac, cycle, track plan, model railroad.

Zero hints: "sentence mining", "data visualization". One hint: "watch history" (Heritale, stories about wristwatches). The store offers no completion for any of the three, which means almost nobody types them.

Pattern: 12 of the 17 flags sit on hints that are an app's or seller's name (all four arXiv hints among them). A name with a weak shelf means a small app, not an open job. The 5 job phrases flagged (regex, regexp, regex tool, japanese conjugation, model railroad) sit on 3 shelves; with the arXiv cluster that is 4 real thin shelves, and three of them (arXiv, regex, conjugation) filled in 2026 with apps at 0 to 17 ratings. A thin shelf here reads as a small market more often than an empty one.

## What the box says about the B- tier

- **Cycle listener (H41)**: "furnace" gives 10 hints, none about a home furnace (Furnace Fest, a yoga studio, WaterFurnace, Furnished Finder). "hvac" gives trade tools (hvac school, pt chart, check charge, load calculator manual j). "cycle" gives menstrual tracking first (cycle tracker #1, natural cycles #7, cycle syncing #10): health, skipped under C7, not read. The homeowner does not type this job into the box. Day 30 discovery for the cycle listener stays weak, now measured.
- **Watch history (H40c / Trakt lanes)**: one hint, about wristwatches. People type "tv tracker" (10 hints, 3 of them app names) and "movie tracker"; none of the 20 shelves is weak. Discovery would ride "tv tracker", a full shelf.
- **Track plan**: the hints are "track planes" (#1, flights), budget and time-off planners; the railway one is #10 and is an app's name (Train Layouts: Track Plans, $2.99, 86 ratings, updated 2026-10-15: browse ready-made plans by scale and track system).
- **Atmos (H39)** was not seeded here; H56 did its phrases.

Other things the box shows, not graded: "youtube" hints #8 to #10 are video downloaders and an ad blocker (platform terms; out). "flashcards" hints #3, #4, #8, #10 are for children (C7; out). ## Concepts (2; a third did not survive the shelf read)

Five letters: Need, Novelty, Depth, Money, Day 30 discovery (D30), then Overall. Gates: free answers (H67) and builders (H75), `tools/lane_gate.py`.

### 1. Model-railway track planner on iPad (pass12, H94): **C+**, unchanged

What it is: plan a scale model railway (HO, N, O) on iPad with the real track pieces of a named system, check curves, gaps and grades, print 1:1 templates. Buyer: adult model railroaders planning a layout.

- What H91 adds: "model railroad" is the #1 hint for its seed and its shelf is weak (top entrant a 2020 game). But no hint under "model railroad" or "track plan" names a planner or a layout tool, so a buyer would have to find us under the broad phrase. The clone wave has reached the toy end of the hobby in 2026: TrackPlanner (wooden track, German screens, 2026-08-21), RailMaker (wooden track drawn with a finger, 2026-09-19), Your Tracks (brick track with AR on the floor, $1.99, 2026-04-27). None of the three plans scale track.
- Need **C+** (H94: about 81 asks in r/modeltrains in a year, 12 tool-shaped). Novelty **C+** (builders gate cap; the three 2026 toy planners show the idea is in builders' hands). Depth **A** (pass12). Money **B** (pass12: RailModeller Pro $44.99 on the Mac, 151 Mac reviews). D30 **C-** (no planner phrase in the box; the #1 phrase is broad).
- Free-answer gate: **C+ CAP**, median replies 6 at the cut of 6 (`gate-rail-free.txt`, rerun on H94's answers.rows, no network). Builders gate: **C+ CAP**, 387 builders for 81 askers on "track plan" (H94's run; H94 found "track plan" matches workout tracking plans, 3 of 100 on job, so the builder count is noisy).
- Overall **C+**.

### 2. arXiv morning triage (rank today's papers against your own work, read them reflowed): **D+**

What it is: each morning, the new arXiv papers in your categories, ranked against the papers you wrote or saved, readable as reflowed text on an iPhone. Buyer: graduate students and researchers in fields that post to arXiv.

- Counted: r/AskAcademia, 12 months to 2026-10-24, titles with "arxiv" (61) or "keep up" (16), 77 distinct, every title hand-read (`H91-raw/arxiv/titles.txt`). 12 ask how to keep up with new papers (`codes-keepup-rows.txt`); 1 of them asks for an app ("Any good journal apps to keep up with psychology research", 2026-01-13); 1 reads like a builder's survey ("Looking for what's broken about the current tools", 2026-06-06). About 40 of the 77 are endorsement requests (not our job). Visible builders in the same pull: 3 (an arXiv podcast, a bibliographic explorer, a multi-index search box).
- Need **C** (12 asks a year in the one sub; most want a habit, not a tool). Novelty **D+** (Arxivist shipped the ranking on device 2026-08-05; FirstPass the reflowed reading 2026-06-04; SotaSieve digests 2026-09-23; 248 GitHub "arxiv daily" repos in a year). Depth **B**. Money **D+** (every reader on the four shelves is free but one $0.99 Chinese portal; the best has 17 ratings). D30 **C** (people do type "arxiv": 10 hints, though 9 name apps).
- Free-answer gate: **C+ CAP**, median replies 8.5 over 12 of 20 drawn rows (8 removed endorsement posts left out). The draw covers all 77 titles, mostly endorsement; on the 12 keep-up asks alone the median num_comments is 6.5, under the num_comments cut of 7 but over the answers cut of 6. Builders gate: **C+ CAP**, 256 builders for 12 askers ("arxiv daily"; "arxiv reader" 56).
- Overall **D+**.

### Not a concept: regex tester, Japanese conjugation drill

Both shelves are weak only by ratings. Regex: eight 2026 entrants at free to $2.99, and RegExp already runs six real engines. Conjugation: four 2026 entrants. Novelty D on the shelf alone; I did not pull askers for them, so no gates were run.

## Not done

- Hint order was read as the store gives it; I did not test whether the order changes by hour or device.
- "Atmos" phrases were left to H56. "cycle" health hints were skipped, not searched by hand (the tool searched them; I did not read those shelves).
- Japanese conjugation and regex: no asker count.

## For Odalys (tools)

- `store_hints.py --help` sends a request with "--help" as the seed. Suggest: treat any argument starting with "-" that it does not know as an error.
- Slug collision: `slug()` drops non-Latin letters, so "arxiv 中文网" (#6) saved over "arxiv" (#1) as `search-arxiv.json`; hint #1's shelf survives only in `run-1.txt`. Suggest a hash suffix when a slug loses characters.
- The tool searches every hint, including ones in banned areas (menstrual tracking, children's flashcards, YouTube downloaders). A `--skip` word list would save the requests and keep C7 tidy.
- New `tools/hint_descs.py` (mine): top N descriptions behind every flagged hint, from the saved JSON, each app printed once.
