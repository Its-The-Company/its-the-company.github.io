## Method

- Tools: the 11 the board line names plus 9 more (racquet string tension, radar gun, light meter, clinometer, tree height, window tint meter, timegrapher, stud finder, wood moisture meter). 20 rows below. Five more were pulled but are not in the 20 (3D printer belt meter, bat detector, thermal camera, golf launch monitor, golf rangefinder); a note on them sits under the table.
- Price and buyer count: one web search per tool (WebSearch, US results), one page fetch where the count mattered. **Most price and count figures below are from search summaries, not pages I read**, and are marked "(ss)". Only the Mantel TM-1 page was read directly (94 reviews, 4.6/5). Zoro answered 403 and pocketradar.com 429 (Retry-After 60); I stopped at each, no retry (C6). The buyer count is a retailer's review or rating count, which undercounts buyers; it is a floor, not a size.
- Phone error: Apple's documentation or a published paper, or 'unknown'. Where I have only my own arithmetic or a synthetic check, it is labelled as mine, not as a source.
- Synthetic check: `H92-raw/freq_check.swift` (a script, not an app): decaying stiff-string partials plus white noise at 20, 10 and 0 dB SNR, 48 kHz, 60 trials per row, seed 92; Hann window, DFT grid over +/-20% of the pitch, parabolic refinement. Re-run this session with `swiftc -O`: output identical byte for byte to `freq_check.txt`. What it does not test: the real iPhone microphone, room noise that is not white, a spoke crossing that shortens the vibrating length.
- Shelf: `tools/shelf_gap.py PHRASE --since 2025-10-24 --save` per phrase (`H92-raw/shelf_gap*.txt`, JSON in `shelf_gap/` and `shelf-json.tar.gz`), then `H92-raw/shelf_list.py` for a dated list per phrase (`shelf_list.txt`, `ios_shelf.txt`). **Serious entrant** (rule fixed before reading, written in shelf_list.py): the title or description contains every word of the phrase AND 100 or more US ratings. The rule overcounts where games or forecast apps share the words, so the table gives the rule count and, where they differ, the count by hand.
- Builders: App Store entrants released since 2025-10-24 whose title names the phrase, plus GitHub repos created since then with the phrase in name or description (shelf_gap). GitHub counts for hardware words (tachometer, soil moisture, anemometer) are mostly Arduino and ESP32 projects, not phone apps.
- Gates (H67 free answer, H75 builders per asker): **not run on a pull of this <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Each lane has its own file.">lane</span>**; for spoke tension the H52 pull gives the H75 result (fails). This is a capability-first lane with no demand pull of its own, so there is no asker count to divide by and no thread sample to code. That is a shortfall against the refill-13 instruction; see the last section.

## <span title="One product idea inside a lane. C1 is the lane&#x27;s first idea.">Concepts</span> (the three kept tools)

All three are the same engine: a pitch from a pluck or a tap, then a physical model to the quantity the owner wants. The engine is easy to test on the Simulator from files; the model is where the work and the error live.

### 1. Spoke tension by ear (wheel builders and home mechanics)

Pluck each spoke near the rim; the app hears the pitch, takes the spoke's free length and gauge (and butting), and gives tension in kgf with the TM-1-style balance map per side. Physics: T = 4 mu L^2 f^2, so frequency error doubles into tension and length error doubles too. My synthetic check says the microphone stage is not the problem (0.07% tension p95 at 0 dB SNR); the free length is (+/-1.4% at +/-2 mm). Crossing spokes touch and shorten the vibrating length, which no synthetic test covers. Shelf: 0 serious, but three new apps between 2026-07-14 and 2026-07-22 and ten GitHub repos in the year (two use the microphone), so the idea is in many heads at once and none has buyers yet (all at 0 ratings).

### 2. Motor and engine RPM by sound (small engines, shop motors, turntables)

<span title="What keeps an idea below the top: the cap that applies to it now.">Hold</span> the phone near a running motor; the app finds the hum's fundamental, asks for the number of cylinders or poles, and gives RPM. Synthetic hum gives 0.005 to 0.023% p95, inside the +/-0.05% the optical tools state, but a real engine's firing order and harmonics decide which partial is the fundamental, and that is not tested. The buyers pay $19 for the optical tool (Neiko, ss), so the price ceiling is low. Shelf: 1 to 2 serious (a 2015 strobe app and a turntable app); three new acoustic tachometers in July 2026.

### 3. Racquet string-bed tension by tap (club players and stringers)

Tap the string bed; the app hears the pitch and, with head size and string, gives stiffness and the loss since stringing. The pitch stage is fine on synthetic taps (0.07% p95 at 0 dB); the hard part is pitch to DT or to tension, which RacquetTune itself calls a different thing from the machine setting. RacquetTune has sold at $2.99 since 2010 with 482 US ratings: proof that a few people pay, and proof that the market stayed small. Six new apps name the phrase since May 2026.

## What would make me wrong

- Buyer counts are search summaries for 19 of 20 tools. A tool with a large, verified count that I priced from a summary could move a Need letter; none of the kept three would leave D on Novelty, because their shelves are dated, not summarised.
- The phone errors for the kept three are mine and synthetic. A real recording on a real wheel could be worse (crossings, room noise) or the length error could be removed by measuring it from the rim and hub dimensions; either changes Depth, not the shelf.
- The serious-entrant rule is a cut I fixed before reading. It counts games under "paint color match" and forecasts under "anemometer"; I corrected those by hand. It may undercount a serious app that uses another phrase (for example a "wheel truing" app); I searched only the tool's own phrase.
- I did not run the H67 and H75 gates on a pull of my own. For spoke tension H52 already has one (29 askers, 7 GitHub builders: fails H75). For the tachometer and racquet concepts there is no asker count; a pull from r/smallengines or r/10s would be needed, and with 3 and 6 store entrants in a year it would need many askers to pass.
