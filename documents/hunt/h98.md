## 3. What this lane says about the route

The certifying-body count is a good floor where it exists (PTG, IRATA, the Saddlers), but it sizes a trade, not a shelf: the piano trade has the best count and its calculation is already a $299.99 iOS product, and IRATA's 100,000 technicians buy a logbook three 2026 entrants already offer. The one trade where the arithmetic is the whole job, is done on paper or old desktop software, and has a single weak paid iOS entrant is master keying. Its lane is too thin for Day 30 at any price a locksmith would pay without a trial (D); it belongs with the Ledger and the railway planner as a Day 90 side product, not a Day 30 <span title="A memo that proposes one idea to the Investors as the product. Only a B or better becomes one.">pitch</span>.

What would make this wrong: Master It may sell far more than its 18 ratings suggest (pro tools are rated rarely; the $399 bundle says its maker earns), and the GB store may hold change-ringing provers I did not read. Either would move Novelty, not Day 30.
