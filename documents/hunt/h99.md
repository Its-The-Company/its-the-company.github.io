Result: the venue is thin for us. Owners of home and hobby gadgets hate their companion apps for connection faults (990 of 2,994 one- and two-star US reviews by pattern, 33%) and subscriptions (156, 5%; Anova alone 90 of 200). They rarely ask for history or a judgment over time (28 of 2,994, 0.9%, every one hand-read) or for export (1). One device stands out. Moen Flo has 12 of its newest 200 low reviews in four months asking for the water-use history a 2026 update took away. Moen publishes no API, so no route reaches that data. Up to 3 concepts below, author cells only; the chair rules the letter.

Listen to this, Moen Flo, 2026-08-21, 2 stars: "The old version of the app includes Historical usage by fixture... often helpful when I found a slow leaking toilet. Now that data is completely missing in the app." And Anova, 2025-02-19, 1 star: "No logging or history. These are very obvious basics that should be included, but instead ignored to try to squeeze you for a subscription instead."

## 1. How the 15 were chosen, and where the board's premise broke

- `tools/appsearch.py` over 104 terms: the board's device classes, maker names and generic phrases (raw: H99-raw/search1.txt, search2.txt, 14:00-14:15 ET). Rows kept: maker companion apps for home or hobby devices with 5,000 or more US ratings.
- **Only 3 maker apps meet the board's cut (over 5,000 ratings, under 3.5):** LotusLanternX 2.5, DJI Go4 3.0, Ember 3.3. The named classes sit high. Meater 4.6, ThermoPro (TempPro) 4.7, ThermoWorks 4.8, Traeger 4.8, recteq 4.8, Masterbuilt 4.7, Weber iGrill 4.7, Rachio 4.9, B-hyve 4.7, Rain Bird 4.7, Hydrawise 4.7, Automower Connect 4.5, Ambient Weather 4.6. SensorPush, Ecowitt, Netatmo, Worx Landroid, Segway Navimow, Yarbo and Mammotion returned no maker app at 5,000+ under their names. Prompted in-app ratings likely lift these averages (my reading, not measured).
- So I took the **15 lowest-rated** maker apps at 5,000+ in the sweep, which reaches up to 4.4. Excluded: Apple Home 2.2 (an OS app, not a device maker), third-party LED and TV remotes, the Blink EV-charging network, and non-device apps that matched a term. Weather Underground (3.9) is a weather network that personal stations upload to. Its low reviews read as forecast users, not station owners, so it is a weak row.
- Reviews: `tools/review_dump.py ID --countries us --max-stars 2` (store_http throttle; no 403 or 429 in this lane). The newest 200 per app. La Crosse View had only 194 in the US feed's 500-review window. US only, so non-US owners are not in these counts.

## 2. Coding

Letters (one per review; priority H > E > S > K > O): **H** wants history or a judgment over time; **E** wants export; **S** subscription anger; **K** connection fault (pairing, Wi-Fi, offline; not ours to fix); **O** other (crashes, login, UI redesign, false alerts, hardware).
- `research/hunt/H99-raw/propose.py` proposes a letter by pattern. I hand-read every review it proposed as H or E (102 rows) and set each one's letter in `overrides.tsv` (50 of them not O). The pattern was loose ("last week", "photography", "log in"). 28 of the proposed rows are true H and 1 is a true E.
- Seeded check (`sample_check.py`, seed 9901): 5 rows per app that were not proposed as H or E, 75 rows hand-read. **0 missed H or E.** K/S/O agreement 68 of 75 (91%): 4 O should be K, 1 O should be S, 2 K should be O. So treat K and S as pattern counts within about ±10%.
- Tally by `tools/code_reviews.py tally codes.txt --letters HEKSO`. The `--letters` option is new in this lane (Tenet 7); the default output is unchanged.

Totals: 2,994 reviews; H 28, E 1, S 156, K 990, O 1,819. Builder counts for one-word names are word matches I did not read (dji, anova, arlo, govee, lifx and nanoleaf collide with other things). They count as builders only, never as a route (board line). "None found" means I checked one likely developer URL plus the store text I had. It is not a full search of the maker's site.

Here is the shape of it:
- **History wants come from metered utilities and room sensors, never from lights or gadgets.** Water (Moen 12), temperature and humidity (Govee 4, La Crosse 3, Inkbird 2 + 1 export), HVAC and energy (TCC 2, Rheem 2). Lights, mugs, LED strips and drones: 0 or 1.
- **Most of the history wants are about something taken away**, not something never offered. Moen's fixture history went in a 2026 app update, and 9 of the 12 say so. Govee removed VPD graphing (2026-10-18) and graphs stopped for one owner's H5100s (2026-04-17). Reviewers say the data still exists on Moen's web portal. A maker can put a removed feature back in one update, so this is a fragile gap.
- **The wrong-judgment complaint is bigger than the wants-judgment one, and it is the maker's own algorithm.** In my 5-row Moen sample, 3 of 5 are false "fast water flow" or shutoff alerts (livestock drinking, a toilet flush, lawn watering). I coded these O as the board's letters ask. They point to the same job, "tell me what my water is doing", done badly.
- **Subscription anger concentrates on one maker that publishes an API.** Anova: 90 of 200 S, against 0-31 everywhere else. 2025-05-26: "There is a $24/year ... subscription and without this you cannot even receive a low water alert." 2026-03-10: "they charge a $10 a year subscription for it. But it won't connect!!!"

## 5. What was lost, slips, not done

- No store 403 or 429 in this lane. Total store wait was about 50 minutes for 15 review dumps plus a 15-phrase `shelf_gap.py` run that shared the throttle.
- The board's selection cut (over 5,000 ratings, under 3.5) yields 3 maker apps. I relaxed it to the 15 lowest at 5,000+ (up to 4.4) and say so here.
- US only. Per month uses the US low-review flow, so DJI Go4's 200 span six years and its rate means little.
- Not done: top 10 by hand under "anova" and "moen flo"; Anova's developer terms; Honeywell's locked developer site; `sizing.py` (Day 30 and Day 90 are hand reads, marked not computed); a hand read of the one-word builder lists.
- Over the turn budget: about 40 turns of 30. Most of the extra went to waits for the shared store throttle.
- Raw: research/hunt/H99-raw/ (codes, overrides, rank, search lists, shelf_gap text, scripts; 124 KB). The review dumps (1.9 MB) and shelf_gap JSON (10 MB) are in /tmp/wr-h99/, kept out of git.
