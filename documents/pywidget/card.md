**Four new looks for the app, side by side** (T-5861)

Mockups of four directions for the app's look. Nothing in the app changes until you choose; Paper (light) and Studio (dark) stay in force meanwhile.



Every screen in every look, light and dark: the full page).

**1. Watercolor (warm).** A soft wash of pigment behind headers, cards and the first screen a new user sees; the editor and Settings stay plain. Colour lives in the wash, with a terracotta accent unlike iOS's standard blue. Cool and muted palettes are drawn too. About 2.5 weeks (1.5 to 4), including the Builder as a measured drawing board. **Motion is extra:** a slowly drifting wash adds about 1 week (0.5 to 2), mostly testing speed and battery on a phone.

**2. Blueprint.** The app as the plan of what you build: blue ink, a fine grid, outlines instead of shadows, red pencil on the block you're changing, true measures in the Builder. Its dark mode is deep blue, not today's neutral grey. About 2 weeks (1.25 to 3.5).

**3. Risograph print.** Two see-through inks, teal and fluorescent pink, printed over each other, with halftone dots and faint paper grain. Printed offsets replace shadows. About 2.5 weeks (1.5 to 4).

**4. Swiss editorial.** A strict four-column grid, large titles, one red accent, no shadows. Square cards, and a few things moved onto the grid, make it read least like a standard iPhone app. About 1.5 weeks (1 to 2.5).

**What the costs mean.** Weeks of one engineer's work: my first guesses, not yet checked by Idris. The look's work sits on the plan's path for the interface, so each week of it is likely to move the release (Monday, July 12, 2027) by up to about a week: to about July 22 (Swiss), July 26 (blueprint), July 29 (risograph, or a still watercolor) or August 5 (moving watercolor).

**The moving wash.** The page is built but not yet on the site: the publishing rule has no place for it and removes scripts from mockups. Reply **"do it"** and the company lets this one page run its script on the site (today the site removes the scripts from every mockup it publishes; only its own pages, such as Home and the ideas library, run scripts), so it opens on a phone at https://its-the-company.github.io/documents/new-look/ . Or open `documents/new-look/index.html` from the repository in Safari on a Mac. Stills meanwhile:

 

**My recommendation: watercolor, warm, with the Builder as a measured drawing board.** It has the most personality without looking like a stock app, keeps the working screens calm, and answers both your ideas in one look. Build the wash still first; add the drift once it's tried on a real phone.

Reply with the direction you'd like (or a mix), and anything to change.
