## Which v1 widgets need outside data

Of the 12 in ADR-001 3.1, four need data from outside the device: sunrise and sunset (a computation), weather (conditional), the GitHub repository status, and the quote of the day (bundled text). The other eight use only on-device APIs, and each has a short row at the end.

## For the L16 policy (the v1 part)

1. Every premade widget that goes online names its source in its manifest (brief L16): `api.met.no`, `api.github.com`. The others declare "none".
2. Keys: v1's premade widgets need none. GitHub's token is optional and the user's own, Keychain only; ADR-001 C2 already lists `keychain` for this.
3. Credit lines render on the widget where the licence asks (MET Norway, CC BY 4.0).
4. Every request sends a User-Agent naming the app and the company site (MET Norway and NWS require it). No personal email in it.
5. No paid API, no new account, no server in v1. The MET Norway proxy (at scale) and any WeatherKit tier above the allowance are the Investors' call (C50).

## Browser-read list (developer.apple.com and apple.com, person only, G2a)

1. WeatherKit: the monthly call allowance with the membership, the paid tiers, and the attribution rules for widgets.
2. Whether Apple Weather data a user gets through Shortcuts may be shown in a widget the user builds in a third-party app, and in our screenshots.
3. CLGeocoder use limits (rate and caching) for the "distance to a saved place" setup.

## Brief correction

None. The brief says "Official APIs only" and "Users supply their own API keys"; both hold. ADR-001's weather condition ("a data source with commercial-use terms and no cost") is met by MET Norway, with the volume ceiling above.
