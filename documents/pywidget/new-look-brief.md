Jeremy, on #325 at 11:29 AM on 11-22, you wrote that Paper was a good starting point but the app should be re-envisioned for a "visually distinct, aesthetic, and polished layout", and that you'd send some direction and inspiration once the pre-comparison work was done. That work is done (the comparison started at 7:02 PM on 11-22), so this issue collects your direction in one place.

**What we need from you:** whatever direction and inspiration you'd like to give, as a reply here, in any form and at any length. Nothing else is waiting on this, and the app keeps its current look until you approve a new one.

## 2. What helps most

Any of these, in any mix. Short answers are fine.

- **Apps, sites or objects you like, and why.** Any app, website, product, poster, book or room. The "why" matters more than the name: what is it about the type, the colour, the spacing or the feel?
- **Mood words.** For example calm, playful, bold, technical, warm or crisp. Words you *don't* want help just as much.
- **Colours and type you love or hate.** Specific colours, colour families, typefaces, or "nothing like this".
- **What falls short in Paper today.** What feels plain, dated, too quiet, too busy or not like PyWidget. Point at any of the screens above.
- **Light and dark.** Whether dark mode should be its own look or the same look in the dark.
- **Pictures** are welcome. Paste them into a reply here.

## 3. The limits we work within

- **Mockups are drawn in code only**: SwiftUI (the app's own code) or HTML and CSS. No AI-generated images, now or in the app.
- **Your pictures are reference only.** We take ideas from them (mood, colour, spacing, type) and never copy anyone's design, artwork, icons or layout. Any typeface we use must be one the app is licensed to ship. A paid font would come to you for approval first, like any other purchase.
- **Nothing changes until you say "approve".** A new direction replaces the visual direction you approved on #291 only when you approve it. Until then Paper (light) and Studio (dark) stay the app's look, and app work goes on in them.
- **The layout you chose on #291 stays unless you say otherwise.** That means the Builder with the widget on top and the chosen block ringed, the plain-words run card, and the three start buttons. The new look changes how things look, not where they are. If you'd like the layout re-envisioned too, say so and the mockups will try new arrangements.
- **Paper and Studio stay as themes.** Under #291, people can choose prebuilt themes, so Paper and Studio can stay on as choices. The new look would become the default.

## 4. What comes back, and when

- **Three directions**, each a code-drawn mockup of the **same three screens**: Home, the editor with the Builder, and the widget gallery. Each is shown in light and dark on iPhone, with real widgets on the screens. Drawing the same screens three ways makes them easy to compare side by side.
- **When:** about three days after your reply. For example, a reply on Tuesday 11-24 would mean mockups by about Friday 11-27. They come back as a new issue, linked here, where you pick one, mix parts, or ask for another round.

**The reply that settles it:** your direction and inspiration, in any form. If you'd rather we try first, reply "go ahead" and we'll draw three directions from your #325 words alone.
