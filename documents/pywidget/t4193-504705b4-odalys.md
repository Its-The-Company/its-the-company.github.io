- What is true: on 2026-11-02 `ops/state.md:4` did say "release Tue 2026-12-15" (4bccf762b5), so the rows were true

  out of date.
- Fix: the next T-0337 session (the line is open for R3 and R4) adds a dated line under the T-0337 section that
  works the two dates out again from the current target, and tells Ines in the same note. Board line: none (Low;
  T-0337 is already open and owns it).

### L2 (Low): the memo's note gives a lag range that no saved page backs

- Where: memo :129 "the lag is about two to six weeks" (also CORRECTIONS.md:391 "about two to six weeks").
- The saved page gives the rule and one example (approved 02-10, adjusted from 03-14, 32 days). The range depends on

  CORRECTIONS row says the fiscal calendar was not read, but the memo's note does not.
- Fix: say "about two to seven weeks, Apple's fiscal calendar not read" in the next T-0337 session's dated line, or
  drop the range and keep the quote. Board line: none (Low).
