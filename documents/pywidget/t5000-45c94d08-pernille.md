## Verdict: PASS with findings

H1 is fixed as I asked. No transit family or state draws a count-down any more: medium, small, large and rectangular
show clock times only, the inline reads "B 7:42, D 7:44", the VoiceOver label has clock times only, and checklist item
2 now tells the Day phases build "no relative text, no timer text" with the reason for each. Maren chose clock times
over timer text and gives the reason I would have: "0:00" for a train that left four minutes ago is also false. render.py
now draws SwiftUI's under-an-hour form, which is what the app draws (TreeView.swift:758-763 draws
`Text(d, style: Self.style(style))`, and `style("relative")` is `.relative` at line 801), and the probe's five points
are pinned in the test. The redrawn sheets reproduce byte for byte and still have 0 layout faults.

The findings are outside the transit frames: the bundled Sun premade has the count-up fault the page now names for Sky
(M1), and the test and fmt_rel have small gaps (L1, L2).

## Findings

### M1 (Medium): the bundled Sun premade counts up after sunrise and sunset for up to 15 minutes

showcase.md now says, for Sky, that relative text "counts up once its date has passed", so "the build needs an entry at
each event". That is right, and the app's shipping Sun premade already has the fault. widgets/premade/sun.py draws
"in" plus `ui.Text(nxt, style="relative")` (lines 150, 166, 183) and its `plan` (line 202) gives entries on the quarter
hour only. Sunset is not on a quarter hour: with sunset at 6:00:19 PM, the 6:00 PM entry still has `nxt` = sunset and
holds until 6:15, so from 6:00:19 it reads "Sunset in 0 min, 1 sec", then "in 14 min, 40 sec" by 6:15, counting up.
The same at sunrise. Fix: add entries at sunrise and sunset (and dusk, if it is ever the next event) to `plan`, as the
page asks of Sky. progress.py's "left" text is safe (its date is midnight, a quarter hour) and github.py's is a past
date, where counting up is the meaning. clocks.py draws no relative text.

### L1 (Low): the test misses two count-down forms and one fmt_rel form

(a) "no timer text" looks for `"kind": "timer"` and `timerInterval`, not for date text in style "timer" or "offset",
both of which count by themselves (`_LIVE_DATE_STYLES`). With tree 01 regenerated, `ui.Text(arr[0], style="timer")`
in the rows passes 37 of 37 (mutants C', D'), and that is the very form checklist item 2 rules out. Check that no
date text in the transit documents has a style in `pywidget_ui._LIVE_DATE_STYLES`. (b) Nothing pins the zero unit:
SwiftUI draws "2 hr, 0 min", and fmt_rel rightly draws "34 min, 0 sec", but a change dropping it survives (A3). One
more point, a whole minute ahead or behind, would pin it.

### L2 (Low): fmt_rel's month form, render.py's offset form, and two sheets drawn with the old strings

None of this is on a sheet of this commit. (a) Over about a month SwiftUI draws "1 mth, 21 days"; fmt_rel draws
"53 days, 0 hr". (b) `.offset` is single-unit, in words, with the sign the other way: "−3 minutes" for a date 4
minutes ahead, "+1 minute" for 65 s past; render.py line 354 draws "+3 min, 59 sec". No tree in trees/, gallery/trees/
or showcase/trees/ uses a date over a month away or the offset style, so no sheet is wrong today, but the next one
that does will measure the wrong string. (c) The 12-premade and gallery sheets (drawn off main, design-previews) were
not redrawn: Progress now measures "9 hr, 54 min" where its sheet says "9 hr, 55 min", and gallery 15 (Script
output) "34 min, 0 sec" where it says "34 min". Redraw both sets once, and note (a) and (b) in fmt_rel's docstring or
draw them.

  entry date.

  PM) and sheet 08's "39 min, 59 sec" are the strings SwiftUI draws at those times. Today's count-up risk is covered by
  the Evening entry at 5:00 PM where Evening is a transit phase, and gap 4 already names the case where it is not.
- Today's VoiceOver label now names "5:00 PM", not a count, so it does not go stale.
