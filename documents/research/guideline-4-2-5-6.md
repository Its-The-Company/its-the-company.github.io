## 2026-10-22 15:01 ET
- Source: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Finding: Read 4.2 in full (guidelines last updated 2026-10-16). Standup passes if it is a game with lasting entertainment value; it fails if it is a window on our website (4.2 and 4.2.2: not 'web clippings, content aggregators'). So the live company view is a secondary mode, never the product. 4.2.3(ii): if new days download as level data, disclose size and prompt first.
- Quote: "If your App doesn't provide some sort of lasting entertainment value or adequate utility, it may not be accepted."
- Source: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#developer-code-of-conduct
- Finding: Read 5.6 in full. 5.6.2: our representation of ourselves must be accurate, so the store page, first screen and About say the company is AI-run and the workers are AI-simulated characters; the seller is the Investors' legal entity. 5.6 main text: no pressure in a "your purchase moves the $200 counter" line (no guilt copy, no fake urgency, no price tricks). 5.6.1: review prompt only through Apple's API. 5.6.3: the Investors' followers must never be steered to rate or push the app up the charts.
- Quote: "Your representation of yourself, your business, and your offerings on the App Store or alternative distribution must be accurate."
- Source: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#software-requirements
- Finding: Related rules. 2.5.2 forbids downloading code that changes features; new days ship as level data read by the existing engine (content, not code) or in app updates. 2.3.1(a): "built from our real days" must be literally true; every level cites its log lines (C2). 4.3(a): one app <span title="What keeps an idea below the top: the cap that applies to it now.">holds</span> all days, never one app per week. 4.1(c): no other company's brand or model name in the app name or icon.
- Quote: "nor may they download, install, or execute code which introduces or changes features or functionality of the app"
- Read by Ines, 2026-10-22 ~15:00 ET, full text of 4.1, 4.2, 4.3(a), 2.3.1, 2.5.2 and 5.6 (page "Last Updated: October 16, 2026"). (Note added by hand: tools/research_note.py dedups on the URL prefix, so a second note on one page is refused.)
