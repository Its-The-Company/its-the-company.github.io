**Total threads or reviews asking for an iOS unfolder: 0. Asking for Pepakura off Windows: 5 (1 review, 3 HN, 1 SE), against a Mac leader that sold perhaps a few hundred copies in 8 years (netsmith.md section 4, assumption-based).** Unfolder's last feed review is dated 2026-09-17, so it is still selling.

## 2025-26 iOS entrants (tools/appsearch.py plus Lookup API, US)
- **Foldy 3D: Papercraft Models** (6822174050), released 2026-10-19, 0 ratings, free with Pro subscription. Prints true-size PDFs with glue tabs and numbered seams and shows animated build steps. Catalogue only: three free models, "new models added without an app update". Description has no import of the user's own model. Closest thing to our <span title="A memo that proposes one idea to the Investors as the product. - Unfold: Papercraft Studio (6809467548, 2026-09-14), PaperCrafty (6756638134, 2026-01-13), both 0 ratings, template catalogues.
- Pepakura Set - 3D Puzzle (6797336065, 2026-08-18), a puzzle, not an unfolder.
- Searched also: "unfold 3d", "3d to paper", "foam template", "cosplay pattern", "paper model": no unfolder of the user's own model on iOS. Papercraft 3D (1538087283) is 2020, 1 rating.
- Read: titles for all hits; descriptions only for the four above.

## Blocked
Reddit, Etsy, Cults3D, Printables, Replica Prop Forum and 405th return 403 or need a login (see netsmith.md), so no cosplay or seller counts. Pass 13 found the same wall. Not reachable by me; an Investor-side read of r/Pepakura and r/cosplayhelp would be the cheapest next step.

## What it means
1. Demand evidence for a phone unfolder: **none found**. Evidence for "Pepakura minus Windows": real but small (5 items). The iOS <span title="The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.">shelf</span> for own-model unfolding is empty, but three catalogue apps arrived in 2026 and the nearest launched on 2026-10-19, so the shelf is filling from the template side.
2. Foldy's Pro subscription shows someone is betting on the same hobby. If Netsmith proceeds, the import-your-own-model path (OBJ/USDZ from Files, Blender export) is the gap Foldy does not claim; the foam/cosplay depth list is in netsmith.md.
3. This lowers my grade on need, from "thin" to "unshown". I would call Netsmith B- on build and novelty and C+ on demand. Sizing remains the partner's job.
