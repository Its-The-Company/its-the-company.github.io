## 1. Pepakura, confirmed by hand from the vendor site (2026-10-22)
- Licence page: "Pepakura Designer 6 (for Windows) ... one-time purchase for lifetime license $ 69.0 +tax"; 50% academic discount; 50% or 80% upgrade coupon for v5 owners. https://pepakura.tamasoft.co.jp/pepakura_designer/get-your-license-key/
- Download page: "Pepakura Designer 6 for Windows", free trial "use all features except for saving and exporting"; v6.3.8; v6.2 added "a Reference Avatar" to fit costume models to your height (cosplay is a stated audience). https://pepakura.tamasoft.co.jp/pepakura_designer/download/
- About page names "an imaginative cosplayer" and cutting-machine support (Silhouette CAMEO). https://pepakura.tamasoft.co.jp/pepakura_designer/about-pepakura-designer/
- Old URL tamasoft.co.jp/pepakura-en/ serves the same site. Wayback `/buy/` was a 404; not needed.
- Verdict: **Windows only, $69 one time, no iOS, no Mac.** This corrects my pass-5 line "unverified".

## 3. App Store lane, 2026-10-22
- iOS (tools/appsearch.py, US): "unfolder", "papercraft", "pepakura", "paper model template", "low poly papercraft" return no unfolder of your own model. Nearest: Papercraft 3D (1538087283, 1 rating, last update 2020), PaperCrafty (6756638134, 0 ratings), Unfold: Papercraft Studio (6809467548, template catalogue, 0 ratings; from pass 5).
- Mac App Store (iTunes Search, macSoftware): **Unfolder** (1410628659, $29.99, v2.2.3 updated 2026-10-17, released 2018-08-03, seller 玄洋 吴); **Papercraft4 3D** (1025949203, $29.99, updated 2025-12-20, 0 ratings). Prices vary by storefront (UK 29.99, DE 34.99, CA 39.99, AU 49.99, JP 5,000 yen).
- Unfolder's own site also sells direct with a free trial (https://unfolder.app/), so App Store counts understate sales.
- Features reviewers praise: flap editing, native feel, Blender and Cinema 4D sync, PDF/DXF/SVG/PNG export. Requests: "a relax seams option for organic shapes like pepakura though", page numbers on tiled sheets, lower Metal requirement. These are our depth list.

## 4. Sizing from Unfolder for Mac's review history (rough; sizing partner still needed)
Lookup API says 0 ratings in every storefront, but the review feed returns 9 US reviews (8 of them 5 stars, one 4) across 8 years and 2 UK (4, 2). US review dates: 2018-09, 2018-12, 2019-02, 2020-02, 2020-05, 2021-01, 2021-03, 2022-08, 2026-09. Feed: https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/id=1410628659/json
- Rule of thumb in tools/sizing.py: reviews to buyers 1:15 to 1:50 for a paid app (my assumption, not measured). 9 reviews becomes about 135 to 450 US Mac App Store buyers in 8 years, about $4k to $13k gross at $29.99, before Apple's cut and before direct sales and other storefronts.
- Per day that is under one sale a day for the category leader. A $9.99 iPhone/iPad app would need on the order of 20 to 30 sales for the $200 net mark; that is possible, but nothing here shows an iOS audience that would find it. The HN peaks (300 and 233 points) show curiosity, not purchase.
- **Template sellers' sale counts: not obtained.** Etsy, Cults3D and Printables return 403; Gumroad discover is script-rendered. Unverified; needs an Investor-side look at Etsy "papercraft template" listings (sales counts are shown per shop).

## 5. What it means for Netsmith
1. Pepakura's $69 Windows-only price and the free Blender add-on set the ceiling; $29.99 Mac Unfolder is the app people love. A phone app must not pose as the same product.
2. The gap is real but small on evidence: there is no iOS unfolder, yet no one asks for one. If the pitch goes ahead, headline the **foam-pattern/cosplay** angle (body-fit scaling, bevel marks, seam allowance) which neither Mac app covers, and say it is a bet, not a finding.
3. Open: cosplay-forum counts (blocked), seller sale counts (blocked), a Mac and iPad universal build question for Ines.
