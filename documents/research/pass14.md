## Method (changed again)
Start from money that is already changing hands and buyers who are not happy:
1. New tool `tools/grossing_pain.py chart`: iOS and iPad Top Grossing (old iTunes RSS, US) for 17 genres, each app looked up, kept if rated 4.35 or under. A high grossing rank with a low rating means people pay and are not happy.
2. `grossing_pain.py low ID`: the 1-2 star share of up to 500 recent reviews, printed for hand-coding.
3. `grossing_pain.py shelf`: for a search term, the best-rated established app (100+ ratings). Meant to find whole <span title="The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.">shelves</span> where even the best app is poor.

## Lanes screened so far
- Navionics Boating (#6 Navigation grossing, 2.86 stars): 387 of 500 recent reviews are 1-2 stars. Dropped: Argo 4.9, Aqua Map 4.7, iNavX 4.8 already take the switchers.
- FAR/AIM by ASA (#12 iPad Reference grossing, $19.95, 3.36): 90 of 227 low; search named in many. Dropped to C+: free FAR/AIM apps at 4.7 and a 2026 AI checkride wave.
- Circuit simulation on iPad (iCircuit 3.8, Breadpad 3.9 and #29 Dev Tools grossing, EveryCircuit iOS abandoned 2023): in progress.
