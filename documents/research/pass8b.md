## Verdict first

**Not passed.** Outside YarnPal's own reviews I found 4 separate people or places that say a pattern's counts or picture were wrong, and 0 people asking for a checker or design tool. That is "describing the harm", the same shape as pass 8, not "asking for the cure". Also CrochetPARADE does more than the <span title="A memo that proposes one idea to the Investors as the product. Only a B or better becomes one.">pitch</span> credits it with. Recommendation: Partfix (#15) stays first unless Idris's spike shows something Investors can see on a phone that CrochetPARADE cannot.

Total: about 4 to 5 "pattern was wrong" mentions, from snippets I could not open; 0 requests. Weak, and mostly second-hand. Honest limit: the places where the complaint would live (r/Amigurumi, Ravelry, Etsy review pages, Crochetville) are 403 or login walls to us.

One useful sign: pattern proofreading is sold. A Fiverr seller offers to "test, proofread" amigurumi PDFs for stitch-count errors https://fiverr.com/serahalice23/test-proofread-your-crochet-pattern-amigurumi-crochet-pattern-pdf-with-feedback (page 403; snippet only, price and review count not read). That is evidence designers pay for the checking job, not that hobbyists want an app.

## Competitors by hand

- **CrochetPARADE** https://crochetparade.org : free and open source. It "parses and checks a pattern for correctness", builds a 3D virtual model, flags overly loose or tight stitches, exports SVG charts and GLTF. Slow at tens of thousands of stitches; "not designed for mobile devices"; some Safari versions load it wrongly. So the checker and 3D preview exist already, free, on desktop, with its own grammar (not plain written patterns). Our edge is phone, plain notation, offline, and the designer; not the checker idea. The pitch's "Not new in the world" line should say this more strongly.
- **Amigurum.io** https://amigurum.io : page still says "Launching Fall 2021!"; piece library (heads, ears, bodies), basic PDF free, premium colour PDF; no checking. Likely dormant. Not a threat.
- Research: Greer and Mould 2025 https://diglib.eg.org/handle/10.2312/exw20251057 ; Carleton project https://report-hub.scs.carleton.ca/projectPage/2810/ ; AmiGo https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2211.01178 . None is a shipped app. Devpost "Amiguru" (hackathon, 3D preview for designers) https://devpost.com/software/amiguru : page 403, status unknown.

## What it means for the gate

1. Demand check: **fail on the pitch's own test** ("common beyond one app's reviews"). Not disproved: our <span title="A second worker who grades the idea without seeing the first worker&#x27;s grades, and records them first.">blind</span> spots are exactly the forums. Cheapest fix: an Investor reads r/Amigurumi and Ravelry for ten minutes (already asked).
2. Competition: the checker is already free on desktop. The sell is the phone, plain notation, and the designer. The designer is where the proof of payment is thinnest.
3. Suggested sharpening if the Investors still like it: lead with the designer for people who sell patterns (they pay for tester time on Fiverr), keep the checker as the free hook.

Open: read Crochetville/Ravelry by hand (Investor); price and review count of Fiverr proofreading; open "Crochet Patterns: Amigurumi" 6767084140 by hand.
