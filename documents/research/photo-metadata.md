## 2026-10-22 01:31 ET
- Source: https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/exifstrip/id6760243108
- Finding: ExifStrip: strips metadata offline, free <span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">cap</span> of 3 per day, iPad-designed listing, no reviews yet. Few iPhone competitors with traction; demand unproven.

## 2026-10-22 01:31 ET
- Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6754588948
- Finding: Image Size - Batch Resizer: batch resize with option to remove EXIF/GPS; no ratings yet. Mature batch tools such as PhotoResize Pro are Mac-only, hinting at an iPhone gap.

- Source: https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=1455197364/sortby=mostrecent/json

Full coded list in research/reviews/exif-1455197364.md

## 2026-10-22 01:34 ET
- Source: https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=2/id=571574618/sortby=mostrecent/json
- Finding: Gap pattern across two competitors (151 coded 1-3 star reviews total, counts above): HEIC not handled or converted to lower-quality JPEG (3 + 5 quality/format mentions), batch capped at 40 (7+3), pay-again or refund (2+7), failures after update (22+9). A strip-only tool that rewrites metadata without re-encoding and keeps HEIC would answer these. Technical claim needs a Simulator spike before it is promised.
- Quote: "The paid version does not appear to edit metadata on HEIC photos. It does work for jpegs. Basically useless unless you have an old phone. (1 star)"
