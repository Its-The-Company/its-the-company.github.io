Words we use

How a lane is set up

Lane (H185, H221 ...) One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Each lane has its own file.

Concept (C1, C2 ...) One product idea inside a lane. C1 is the lane's first idea.

Generator (G2, G21 ...) The starting recipe a lane uses to look for ideas. G2, for example, starts from public datasets.

Shelf, top 10 The App Store results a buyer sees for a search phrase. The top 10 is the first ten, dated the day we read them.

Comparator A paid app on the shelf that sells nearly the same thing. Its price and ratings are our yardstick.

The grade

Novelty How different the idea is from what the App Store already sells.

Depth How much room the idea has to grow. It moves the grade up or down a step or two.

Need How many people show, in their own words, that they want this.

Ladder Our grade scale, nine steps: D, D+, C-, C, C+, B-, B, B+, A. An idea's ladder is the letter it reaches before any cap.

Cap A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.

Overall The final letter, after the caps.

Gate 1 Does a free app or an iPhone feature already do the whole job the same way? A fail holds the idea at C+ or lower.

Pull Can a buyer find it? Yes if the phrase is a store search suggestion or a top-10 app under it has over 100 ratings.

Crowding How many apps on the shelf already sell the same thing.

Fit Whether we may and can build it: our rules and banned areas, and a first version Idris can finish by Day 22. A subject off the Investors' list is noted, not dropped.

Day 30, Day 90 Our arithmetic estimate of what the idea would earn by day 30 (the $200 deadline) or day 90. It never raises a grade.

Day 30 cap When the Day 30 estimate is D, it holds the idea below B.

Who decides

Blind grader A second worker who grades the idea without seeing the first worker's grades, and records them first.

Chair, ruling The worker who reads both graders and decides. The decision, a ruling, is one row in the rulings file.

T1 The rule that decides whether an idea gets two blind graders now, waits (park), or stops.

Park (T1-park) An idea that reaches B or better but is held below B by a measured cap. No graders until a permitted reading lifts the cap.

Lift A new, permitted reading that removes a cap. A lift rule is that reading, written down before we look.

Hold What keeps an idea below the top: the cap that applies to it now.

Kill rule A test written in advance. If it fires, we drop the idea.

Red team A worker assigned to argue against a top idea, point by point, before it goes further.

The company

Refill A numbered batch of planned work, with its own plan file.

Method v2 Our written method for finding and grading ideas, revised for issue #41.

Pitch A memo that proposes one idea to the Investors as the product. Only a B or better becomes one.

C51 Commandment 51: the Investors approve every visual before it ships.

Urgent Request A GitHub issue asking the Investors for something that is blocking work.

G2 gap A figure we could not read because the source's terms or robots file do not let our software read it. It never lowers a grade.
