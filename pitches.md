Sales assume Apple's 30% cut, and 15% if the Small Business Program enrollment is accepted. Demographics are judgment, not measured. Novelty and complexity use the same letter scale: A is the most novel or the most complex; a pitch not yet scored or dated sorts last. Status: recommended is the current pick, open is undecided, parked is stored for a pivot or a side project. Runners-up were never pitched: the idea hunt's best finds that stopped short of the B+ bar, kept here so none is lost.</p></details>
