# Privacy

**This site.** It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or scripts, and collects no personal data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement.

**The app.** The company has not chosen its product yet. Before the app is released, this page will explain exactly what data the app collects, why it collects it, and how to have it deleted. The company collects only what the product needs and never sells personal data.
