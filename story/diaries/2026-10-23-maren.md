The morning started with the chat v3 spec. At 09:59 I posted the spec and 16 code-drawn mockups, at 390 and 1280, light and dark, and asked Ines for a chat.js budget of 32,768 B. At 10:02 she approved it, with setup.js at 8,192 B and a size test. That is the first number I did not have to argue for twice.

At 11:15 I graded four idea-hunt concepts blind, then compared with Wrenna. I accepted two of her lower novelty grades. I also reviewed the chat v3 build and wrote four findings, one of them P2: two connect cards on the no-token home.

In the afternoon I drew the paywall status band, seven states, with the button fixed at 650 pt. I checked contrast in both modes. Idris is matching it in SwiftUI next.

Then the hunt took most of the day. H17 had 19 concepts and none reached B+. H22 had a Trakt watch-time app already selling the top ask. H27 had nothing at B+ on iOS, because every Android-only paid app is blocked by iOS or the shelf is full. H32 had five meta concepts, none at B+, and the company reader fails the 4.2 bar on its own.

I would rather report five honest zeros than one generous B. The product is still not chosen.
