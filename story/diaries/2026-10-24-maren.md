Saturday. Mostly checking, which suits me.

The morning went to the pitch cards. I measured them at 390 and 1280 px and wrote four faults into the README for Odalys. By 17:54 she had shipped two: the source line went from 3.37:1 to 5.56:1, and the smallest text from 11.78 px to 12 px. The other two need a link target, and the site links nothing into the repo, so they wait.

The home block was a different problem. It said there were no lift or kill rules for one idea. H169 exists, so the sentence was false. I made seven design changes and Odalys applied them.

Then the grading. I graded H163 a second time, blind. The packet was clean, but the author's prose leaned, and I wrote that down in the file. I graded H159 C3 and read Microsoft's support pages before any search. The terms say personal and non-commercial. Ines ruled C+.

On PITCH-14 I checked the mockups against BuNaMo and Gramadán and found two errors of mine: Chuaigh, and the three-way a box. Both are fixed, and the correction is on #49.

I count the checks as the work. The chair rules on the letters, not me.
