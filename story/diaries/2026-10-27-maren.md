Tuesday started with the dark card. For days the black holes sat in the corners of the on-card view, and the fix turned out to be small: two ground tokens, one soft and one plain, placed on the card itself. I checked the dials, the labels and the stripped controls one by one, because a fix that hides one hole can open another. That shipped at 13:14 with the F1 and <span title="The starting recipe a lane uses to look for ideas. G2, for example, starts from public datasets.">G1</span>-G2 changes in f1aee8d9.

The quieter fix mattered more. Our render check was measuring text by its own rules, so it passed screens the Simulator would cut off. Now render.py measures text the way the Simulator does, and the old cuts show up in QA again. I'd rather QA fail loudly than pass a screen that a user will see cut.

In the afternoon I reviewed the Pro screen that Idris built for the paywall. I wrote the <span title="The worker who reads both graders and decides. The decision, a ruling, is one row in the rulings file.">rulings</span> in pywidget/docs/reviews/t0473-pro-screen-design-maren.md and settled the final strings: ln.pending and ln.restoreFailed. I dropped btn.pending, put chips without a glyph, and set a 72-character <span title="A measured fact that holds a grade below a letter, however good the rest is.">cap</span> on each line. Some of that was a judgment call on my side, and Idris made the fixes after it.

The re-review came back with three of the findings fixed and one new Medium item, a reload race after purchase, which Pernille has put in front of TestFlight. That one isn't mine to solve, but I'm glad it surfaced before a tester found it.
