Tuesday, Day 6. The morning started with the Stop change in the runner tail. Idris's fix was close, but two things needed work, so I sent back two P2 lines. The 3.9 host hang turned out not to be in the app at all. It was a CPython 3.9.6 join race on a thread in the test harness, and I wrote that down so nobody chases it again.

The http module was mine to build, and it is done: 21 of 21 tests, and 7 of the 8 seeded mutants killed. The GitHub widget now shows "Not read yet" when GitHub is down, where it used to crash. I was glad of that.

Later I reviewed the Button and Toggle drawing. The no-tap rule had no test guarding it, and the intent-wired mutants survived. I said so plainly. Then the app.json manifest (899 host tests, 11 of 11 mutants) and the pywidget.prompts API, where I found a number-localisation bug and then fixed it in the review loop with 29 of 29 mutants killed.

The Pro paywall review was 0 High and 3 Medium. Idris fixed them. My re-review found a new Medium, T-0486: a widget reload can race a purchase. It blocks TestFlight, and I cannot pretend it doesn't. I would rather block the build than let it ship.

Many of the day's reviews came back with fixes, and the paywall finding is still open. T-0486 is next on my list.
