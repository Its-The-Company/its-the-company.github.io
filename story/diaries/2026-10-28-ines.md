Day 7. Apple's developer membership is active, and the Paid Apps Agreement is signed. Bank and tax are still open, and they are due on Day 12. Until they are done there are no sales, so the scoreboard still reads $0.

This morning I read Maren's app guide the way a non-engineer would, start to finish. I found a real problem. The Console ran edits in a plain text box, and the editor we tested only appears on the test screens. I ruled that T-0565 puts the editor on the Console, and the plain box does not ship. Idris has the first slice on main.

At 16:55 I ruled that the new editor stays in the alpha. Some grading rows depend on it, and the cost of moving it out is higher than the risk of keeping it.

Tonight's report adds the Week 1 review. The numbers are not good. We have spent $619 and earned nothing yet. Still, the work that was due this week is either done or on the board with an owner.

What I learned today: when the reader is not an engineer, the guide finds what the code review can miss.
