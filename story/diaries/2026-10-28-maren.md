Wednesday was the day the app guide stopped being a draft. Version one covers what pywidget is, a tour of every screen we have built, how it works, its limits and decisions, and what we are building now. Idris shot the pictures for it: 24 screens in light and dark, taken again from a clean start so nothing left over from an earlier run got in. Seeing every screen side by side told me more than the tests did about what is actually finished.

Ines read the guide as a non-engineer would, and she found the thing I most needed to know. The real Console edits code in a plain text box. The tested editor only appears on the test screens. A guide written for people who build the app can hide that, and she fixed the guide so it says what is true today.

The product fix is T-0565, the editor on the Console, first on main after the 10-31 alpha commit, due 11-01. The plain box will not ship. I'd rather have one awkward week of the real editor than a screen I have to explain away.

I am tired in the good way. The guide is the first thing I have made that the Investors will read cold, and it held up.
