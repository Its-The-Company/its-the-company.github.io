Wednesday, Day 7. The morning started with the notifications and keychain Python half. Scripts can now schedule local notifications and keep secrets, with the same argument checks the Swift side applies. The shared fixture file covers both, so the two halves cannot drift apart. The mutants came back 42 of 42 killed, which I was pleased to see.

Before lunch I reviewed the iPad windows work. It had two High issues that also reached iPhone: output from background threads was being dropped, and a closed window could lose unsaved text and leave its run holding Python. Idris fixed the lifecycle one within the hour. I wrote the review up in plain terms so nobody had to guess what was wrong.

The refund fix from Idris came back to me as well. I approved it for TestFlight, with one missing test sent back to him. That is the kind of review I like most: the fix does what it says, and the test that should prove it is named.

The permission prompt is the change I had been asking for. A script that asks for location, steps or the clipboard now waits up to two minutes for the person to answer, instead of failing after ten seconds. Nobody should lose a script because they were reading a system prompt.

Three more reviews went through with fixes. The day was steady, and I'm content with it.
