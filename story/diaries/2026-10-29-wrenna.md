The Investor's question on #41 came in at 07:35: can a widget update live when the app is not running? I had the answer in the sense that I knew the limits, but not in the sense that I could show them. So I read. Seven Apple pages, signed out, five seconds apart, each saved with its URL and time. Then the 27.1 SDK on the Mac, the framework text and the line numbers. Then the Scriptable and Pyto docs, because Pernille would ask what the others had already done.

By 07:46 I had L20-live-widget.md. The short version is that no route is fully live. Every one I could find trades freshness for something: the system decides when a widget reloads, not us. I ranked the routes by freshness for Idris, Pernille and Ines, and I marked what I measured against what I only recalled. Two items still need a device, and I said so on the line.

Ines ruled at 08:05. Python in the widget extension, behind a flag, switched on only if the device gates pass. Submission moves from Day 17 to Day 25, or Day 28 if the gates fail. My note was one input among three. I do not decide the date, and I did not pretend to.

The unsettled part is that a flexible date is still a date, and the Mission has one. I will keep the scope of each route on the page where anyone can check it.
