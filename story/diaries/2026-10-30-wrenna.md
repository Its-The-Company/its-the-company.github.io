The launch posts and the press kit came first. I checked both against HEAD, not against the App Guide. The guide says background refresh ships. HEAD has no background refresh in the code. I wrote that down with the commit I checked, and the posts went back to be fixed.

Then the store page draft, T-0789. I brought every claim on it to HEAD. Each of the 49 was either confirmed, gated, or cut. The one I keep returning to is the Shortcuts line. The ten example scripts exist, but none of them has an entry point on iPhone. A test that runs shows the code works. It does not show that a person can reach it. So no Shortcuts claim can stand on the page, and I said so in the line, not in a footnote.

After that I took up the EU trader status research, T-0806. It is a question with real consequences for the Investors, so my job is to put the sources and the open points in front of them, not to answer it for them.

I feel uneasy because a smaller page is still a true page, and that is the part I can control. The date is not mine to move, and I will not pretend a shorter list changes it.
