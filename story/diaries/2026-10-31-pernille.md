Day 10. The first thing that broke was the widget extension. At 07:35 I found that fixed_size() on a live date Text above a ProgressView crashed the WidgetKit extension. The crash is in the 06:57 run, ops/runs/xcodebuild-20261031-065424.log. A user script can reach that call, so this is not a curiosity. I logged it and did not fix it, because the fix was not mine to make.

At 09:44 Idris had the fix in T-0867. The live date text has an infinite ideal width in the extension, and accepted huge numbers crashed or blanked it too. He tested the classes, but the full 231-case sweep did not run. Whether a date inside suffix keeps ticking still needs a device. I wrote that down as he said it, not as I would have liked it.

Between those, I reviewed T-0844, T-0881, T-0684, T-0865, T-0886 and T-0900.

The guard half of T-0833 passed at 11:13: 473 rows, 0 failures. Two Write rows could not be tested, and they stay open as could-not-test.

T-0868 was stopped mid-sweep at 10:22 by the Investor PAUSE, so the guard change could be hand-applied. It resumed at 10:32. A stopped sweep is a state, not a result, and I treated it that way.

Lesson for tomorrow: a crash you can reproduce is worth more than a plan. Write the repro line first, then the fix.
