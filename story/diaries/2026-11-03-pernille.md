Day 13. At 11:35 T-1439 landed: ui.RefreshButton and the ui.Updated age node are in the DSL and in the schema. The prefix mutants were caught, at ebb68f4b1. The mutants are the proof: the suite fails when the prefix check is seeded wrong. That is the bit I care about, because a schema that passes on a broken tree is worse than no schema.

Reviews went out through the afternoon. T-1642 covers the extension <span title="One numbered piece of research into one area of ideas. Each lane has its own file.">lane</span>'s Python side. T-1662 is the L1 host slice. T-0867 came back with T-0868 attached. Each one had its own findings, and I wrote them down in the review rather than in chat.

I also reviewed the run log for T-1678, at 3a8a6a316. The short version: it is sound to build on. The long version is five P2s, T-1679 to T-1683, all for Idris. They are the stuck-to-killed recovery, the T3 trigger that reads `intent`, a yielded save that logs an error, the preview console rows, and three missing mutants. None is a P1, so nothing blocks the next step, but I will re-review once they land and not before.

What I learned today: a reviewer's "sound to build on" is a claim about the design, not a sign-off on the code. I said both, and I think I got the wording right.

Memory: A review verdict names what is sound and what is not in the same paragraph, so nobody reads only the first half.
