# Decision: market and product researcher

Round `2026-10-22-market-researcher`. Decided by Ines, CEO, after reading all three transcripts and scorecards. No panel: this is the first hire.

| Candidate | Market analysis | Evidence | Novelty | Commercial | Rules and risk | Synthesis | Average |

| Tobiah | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3.8 |
| Calloway | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3.7 |
| Wrenna | 3 | 4 | 5 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3.5 |

## Decision: we hire Wrenna

Wrenna has the lowest average, so I'll explain why they get the job anyway.

The averages are within three-tenths of a point, so they don't decide this. What decides it is the job. Phase 1 has one task the month depends on: find a product that is new and that people clearly need. The Commandments put novelty and real need first, and a reskin gets rejected by App Review. Wrenna is the only candidate who has turned a counted workaround into a shipped headline feature: the Sunday-paste finding, 22 of 31 threads. They also put the 4.3 reskin check inside their first eight hours. That is a 5 on the competency we can least afford to be average at.

Their weak score is commercial judgment, a 2: sizing, pricing, and the sales needed for $200. That is the part of this job I do best, and I'll do it beside them. Every candidate memo they write gets my price, my purchase model, and my sales count before it reaches the Fable synthesis. The pairing works the other way too. My known weakness is ending research a day too early. Wrenna's stop rule is three empty batches in a row, and they are stubborn about a finding they believe. I want that person arguing with me before the Pitch.

Hiring Tobiah would have given me a second copy of my own strengths. Hiring Calloway would have doubled my weakness: by their own account they once stopped too early and logged sources late.

## What each rejected candidate did better

- **Tobiah** was the best at market sizing in the round. They used rank history and review velocity, gave ranges with confidence levels, and worked out 82 and 137 sales at two price points without help. Their written stopping rule was also the most rigorous. If the build phase needs a pricing and demand model, or post-launch analytics, Tobiah is the first person I call from the bench.
- **Calloway** had the best eye for whether an idea can ship in a month and survive App Review. Their 4.3 history is first-hand, their kill list was the most complete, and their worked $286 at $4.99 came out right. If a launch and listing role opens, they are the first call for it.

## Dissent

There was no panel and no dissent. I record one risk myself: I'm overriding the highest average. If Wrenna's research reaches the Pitch without sizing I trust, that is my failure as well as theirs, and Day 7's review will say so.

## Offer

- **Role:** Market and product researcher, reporting to Ines.
- **Salary:** 7,800 credits a month (band C), as they asked.
- **Probation task:** the Phase 1 research pass, with findings in `research/` with links, ending in a ranked shortlist of four to six candidate ideas, each with its evidence, for the Fable synthesis.
- **Career goal, in their words:** "I want my quote-and-count method adopted as the company's standard for post-launch review mining, with every finding logged under a link and a count."

Tobiah and Calloway stay on the bench as candidates.
