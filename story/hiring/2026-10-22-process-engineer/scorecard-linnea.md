# Scorecard: Linnea

Round `2026-10-22-process-engineer`. Interviewer: Ines. Written straight after the interview (interview-linnea.md), before reading any other candidate's interview or score. Scale from brief.md: 1 no evidence, 3 competent, 5 exceptional and shown in the answer.

## Scorecard

| Competency | Score | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measurement discipline | 4 | Q1: four timestamps on 31 review rounds, left out 4 with unclean records rather than guess, and named the luck (quieter client mix) in her own before/after. Q3: counted blocker age for a quarter, then stopped, and said that was wrong. Q2 failure was found from a missed deadline, not a number. |
| LLM agent operations | 2 | Knows context size, re-reads, and model choice drive cost; one year running agent workflows; has not tuned model routing or caching. Said so unprompted and asked for a reviewer on model changes. Below the bar for item 3 of the role. |

| Communication design | 5 | Three-column blocker list (what, who is waiting, who can unblock); action in the issue title; Investor answer routed onto the waiting worker's next packet; saw that too many issues train the reader to ignore them. Directly answers outcome 3. |
| Judgment and restraint | 4 | Chose the blocker path before model tuning, which is where the Investors have complained three times; declined to automate rare onboarding for a stated reason. Her habit of a day's talk before each change is a real cost in a 30-day window. |
| Team and narrative sense | 5 | Q2 is the exact harm this role can do (a time saving that cost candor), found and repaired with an apology and a private channel. Understands separation as how real colleagues hand work over. |

**Average: 3.83 / 5.**

**Strongest:** communication and team sense; she would fix the blocker path, the Investors' most repeated complaint, in the first run.

**Gap:** token economics and model choice, a third of the role's stated scope. She would need a measurement partner, and we have no seat assigned to that.

**What would make this hire fail:** the cost line stays unmeasured because the blocker work feels more urgent, and Day 8's measured savings arrive late.

**Recommendation:** hire if no candidate in the round scores higher across the board; if hired, the probation task stands as written, with the cost reconciliation (within 5%) checked by me on Day 4. Salary expectation 8,800, inside the 8,500 to 9,500 offer range.
