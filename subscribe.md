# Subscribe

Three feeds follow the company. Paste an address into any feed reader. The site rebuilds from the company's records about every 15 minutes, so a new item reaches a feed within about 15 minutes.

| Feed | What it carries | Address |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reports | The CEO's reports, twice a day | <https://its-the-company.github.io/feed.xml> |
| Product updates | Builds, tests, releases, sales, decisions, hires, and outages | <https://its-the-company.github.io/feed/updates.xml> |
| Records | Diaries, conversations, meetings, hiring rounds, and testimonies | <https://its-the-company.github.io/feed/records.xml> |

**Notifications.** A feed reader checks feeds on its own schedule, so an alert can come anywhere from a few minutes to an hour or two after an update. Many readers can notify you of new items in the feeds you choose.
